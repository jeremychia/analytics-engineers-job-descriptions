How related are analytics-engineering ads?

A continuum, not a set of job types

Each dot is one job description. Position says what the job does and what stack it uses; a line joins ads that are close on at least two of three independent measures. Colour is what the ad's prose emphasises. Click a dot to see its neighbours, or a legend entry to isolate a group.

Read it as soft regions. Each lens on its own gives a different partition (agreement between them: ARI 0.01–0.05), and the two axes carry only about 18% and 15% of the variance in their data, so no 2D map is faithful. Colour groups were only moderately stable (bootstrap ARI about 0.45). Seniority, role type and month do not line up with either axis. Grey = boilerplate-heavy, single-company batch (Amazon) or no English text. Drawn: 1,025 ads with at least 4 tagged responsibility bullets, and 1,121 edges supported by 2 or more of 3 measures.

No time trend is shown. Dates are scrape dates and the collection method changed in October, so growth of any cluster could not be separated from how ads were collected.
