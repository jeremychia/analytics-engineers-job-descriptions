What you will do

You will join the Data Team, reporting directly to our Lead Data. Your mission is to bridge the gap between raw data and business insights by building a robust, scalable, and high-performance data warehouse. You will empower over 50 employees across Product, Growth, and Finance teams by making data accessible and reliable. Supporting 19M users and 130k Daily Active Users (DAU).

In this role, you will:

Build & Model: Design and implement efficient, robust data models from a wide variety of sources to serve as our "Single Source of Truth".

Help define and manage the tracking plan to ensure complete coverage of all features.

End-to-End Ownership: Manage the entire data lifecycle, including ingestion, transformation, and usage through visualization and reverse ETL.

Technical Implementation: Participate in deploying new solutions for tracking, self-service analytics, and real-time data processing.

Data Excellence: Implement solutions to improve data quality, documentation, and versioning.

Key Data & Metrics you will handle

You will steer our strategic roadmap by overseeing a comprehensive range of metrics:

Business Macro KPIs: Monitor and forecast Revenue (CA), ARR, MRR, Margin, and LTV across our ecosystem of Users, Creators, Ambassadors.

Marketing Performance & Analysis: Evaluate the marketing mix by identifying the best acquisition and retentions methods for all kind of users.

Predictive Modeling: Implement sophisticated alerting systems and scoring models to identify user fragility and subscription potential.

Deep-dive & Cohort Analysis: Conduct granular analyses of User, Creator, and Ambassador cohorts based on acquisition channels, devices, geography, and spending habits.

User Lifecycle & Funnel: Analyze retention rates and monitor the full funnel from acquisition and engagement to long-term loyalty.

Global Usage Patterns: Synthesize global platform usage data to provide evidence-based arguments for product evolution.
