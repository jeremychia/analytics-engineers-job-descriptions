Forward Deployed Analytics Engineer — Healthcare AI
Location: Union Square, Manhattan, New York City
Work arrangement: 4 days on-site / 1 day remote

Openings: 7–8

About the Opportunity
We’re looking for senior Analytics/Data Engineers who understand healthcare finance data and can independently build production data pipelines from scratch.
This is a hands-on individual contributor role, with approximately 80% development and 20% client-facing work. You’ll take messy customer data, uncover the underlying business rules, and build the SQL/dbt transformation layer that becomes the source of truth for an agentic AI platform.
This is not a BI or reporting position. The focus is on building reliable production data models and transformations that power healthcare AI.
What Makes This Role Different
You’ll work at the intersection of customer financial and clinical data and AI systems, translating real-world healthcare business logic into reliable, production-ready data models.
Your work will involve:

- Healthcare financial and clinical data.

- Claims, reimbursement, revenue cycle, and health-system finance.

- Agentic AI applied to healthcare finance.

- SQL, Python, and dbt.

- Modern data pipelines and transformations.

- Customer-facing technical teams, finance teams, and clinical stakeholders.

Analytics engineering is the foundation of the platform. Accurate data supports reliable AI and earns customer trust.
What You’ll Do

- Review customer data models with finance and clinical teams.

- Reverse-engineer customer-specific business rules.

- Convert payer contract and chart-of-accounts logic into durable transformations.

- Build SQL/dbt models that map raw data into a unified ontology and semantic layer.

- Independently build and own production pipelines from the ground up.

- Own testing, validation, and data correctness.

- Expand the ontology as new customers and data sources are onboarded.

Required Qualifications

- 7+ years of experience in Data or Analytics Engineering.

- Strong hands-on skills in SQL, Python, and dbt.

- Direct experience working with healthcare data.

- Experience with claims, reimbursement, provider revenue cycle, or health-system finance data.

- Experience building and owning data pipelines end-to-end from scratch.

- Experience authoring SQL/dbt transformation layers that encode business logic.

- Customer- or stakeholder-facing technical experience, such as forward deployed engineering, integrations, or solutions engineering.

- Experience in a fast-paced health tech startup, health system, or healthcare consulting environment.

- A bachelor’s degree or equivalent hands-on healthcare data experience.

- Ability to work on-site in the NYC office four days per week and travel up to 10%.

- Must currently reside in the United States.

Backgrounds That Do Not Meet the Role’s Requirements
This role is not a fit for candidates whose experience is limited to:

- Primarily BI or reporting work.

- Mainly Tableau or Looker.

- Maintaining existing pipelines without building new pipelines from scratch.

- Clinical or EHR data without healthcare finance or claims experience.

- Adjacent healthcare areas, such as pharma rebates, without provider revenue-cycle experience.
