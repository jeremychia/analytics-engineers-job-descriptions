DBT Analytics Engineer
Location: Brussels, Belgium (Hybrid)

We are looking for a DBT Analytics Engineer to join a high-impact data transformation initiative within an international environment. The successful candidate will play a key role in designing and optimizing modern analytics transformation pipelines, ensuring data quality, scalability, and performance across enterprise-grade data platforms.

Key Responsibilities
• Design and develop transformation pipelines for scalable and reliable data workflows.
• Optimize the execution performance of dbt projects.
• Design, implement, and document data quality controls and validation tests within the dbt ecosystem.
• Set up automated regression testing and validation procedures for transformation outputs.
• Promote and share best practices for managing and evolving dbt projects.
• Translate business requirements into scalable and maintainable transformation pipelines.
• Produce technical documentation covering transformation logic, operational procedures, and migration decisions.
• Collaborate with data engineers, analysts, business stakeholders, and platform teams in an international environment.

Required Skills
• Proven hands-on experience with dbt (preferably dbt Core) in production environments.
• Strong experience in Analytics Engineering and modern data modelling methodologies.
• Expertise in SQL, including complex transformations, incremental processing, and performance optimization.
• Experience developing PySpark transformation scripts for large-scale data processing.
• Solid understanding of Apache Spark, partitioning strategies, file layout optimization, and performance tuning.
• Experience working with Apache Iceberg tables and related concepts such as schema evolution, partition evolution, snapshot management, and time travel.
• Strong knowledge of data validation, reconciliation, and testing approaches.
• Experience with Git-based workflows, code reviews, release management, and CI/CD practices.
• Experience with orchestration and scheduling tools, particularly Dagster and Spark-based workloads.
• Excellent communication and documentation skills.

Nice to Have
• Experience migrating legacy ETL processes from SAS to modern dbt and PySpark-based solutions.
• Experience working in multicultural and international environments.
