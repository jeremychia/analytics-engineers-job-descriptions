Business Intelligence Developer (M/F)
Banco Finantia
Lisbon, Portugal

Turn data into insights and build solutions that support better business decisions

Business Intelligence is not only about managing data. It is about transforming information into reliable, structured and meaningful solutions that help the business understand performance and make informed decisions.

We are looking for a BI Developer to join our team and contribute to the development, implementation and continuous improvement of Business Intelligence and data solutions.

You will bring strong technical and analytical skills, problem-solving capabilities and a collaborative mindset, working closely with the team to develop reliable BI solutions, ensure data quality and support the continuous evolution of our data and analytics environment.

Your impact

You will be involved in the development and maintenance of Business Intelligence solutions, contributing to:

- Performing technical analysis and providing development estimates;

- Developing and implementing Business Intelligence and data solutions;

- Designing and maintaining relational data models and database solutions;

- Developing and maintaining ETL processes;

- Testing developed solutions to ensure their quality, reliability and accuracy;

- Supporting development-related processes and resolving technical issues;

- Performing maintenance and support activities related to the team’s development processes;

- Contributing to the continuous improvement of BI solutions, data processes and development practices.

What you bring

- Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related field;

- Minimum of 3 years of professional experience in relational database modelling and data access;

- Solid knowledge of SQL Server, including Integration Services (SSIS) and Analysis Services (SSAS);

- Knowledge of ETL processes;

- Knowledge of Business Objects or other Analytics tools will be considered an advantage;

- Knowledge of .NET technologies will be considered an advantage;

- Strong planning and organisational skills, with a responsible and autonomous approach;

- Team-oriented and collaborative mindset;

- Strong interpersonal and communication skills;

- Good written and verbal communication skills;

- Critical thinking and problem-solving capabilities.

Why Join

If you are looking for an opportunity to develop your career in Business Intelligence, combining data, technology and analytical thinking while contributing to solutions that support business-critical information and decision-making, we would be pleased to hear from you.
