Description

Are you passionate about turning complex data into actionable insights that drive meaningful business decisions? As a Business Intelligence Engineer II at Amazon, you will design, build, and deliver analytical solutions that empower teams to make smarter, faster decisions at scale. You will own key components of our data infrastructure—building data models, crafting reports, and developing dashboards that directly influence strategy and operations. This role offers the opportunity to work across technical and business domains, partnering with stakeholders to define the right questions and deliver clear, impactful answers. If you are curious, resourceful, and energized by solving loosely defined problems with data, we want to hear from you.

Key job responsibilities

- Design and build mid-to-large-scale business intelligence solutions, including data models, dashboards, and automated reporting pipelines, ensuring they are testable, maintainable, and performant.
- Ability to recognize opportunities where generative AI could enhance products, workflows, or customer experiences
- Partner with stakeholders and cross-functional teams to translate ambiguous business questions into well-scoped analytical deliverables, recommending alternatives and anticipating future needs.
- Experience with AWS solutions such as lambda, S3, and Redshift, SNS
- Apply SQL, scripting languages (such as Python or R), and data visualization tools to develop metrics, uncover trends, and communicate findings that inform decision-making.
- Own the data lineage of your team's BI artifacts, understanding how data is generated, aggregated, and consumed—and proactively improve data quality, consistency, and operational reliability.

A day in the life

You might start your morning reviewing a dashboard you recently launched, checking data freshness and validating metrics against source systems. By mid-morning, you are deep in a design discussion with a partner team, aligning on requirements for a new analytical model. After lunch, you write and optimize SQL queries, test a data pipeline update, and draft documentation for a process you automated. You wrap up the day by sharing insights from a recent analysis with business stakeholders, fielding their follow-up questions and noting ideas for the next iteration.

About The Team

Our team is focused on building reliable, scalable intelligence that helps our organization make data-driven decisions with confidence. We value collaboration, technical rigor, and a genuine commitment to understanding the business problems behind every data request. We are investing in modern analytics capabilities and looking for team members who are excited to shape the future of how we generate and deliver insights. If you thrive in an environment where your work has visible impact and you enjoy partnering closely with both technical and non-technical colleagues, this is the team for you.
