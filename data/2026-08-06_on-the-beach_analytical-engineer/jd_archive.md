About the Role

We’re looking for an Analytical Engineer to help shape the trusted data models, metrics and semantic layers that power decision-making across On the Beach.

This role sits at the intersection of data engineering, business intelligence and emerging AI capabilities. You’ll help turn refined data into reliable, reusable and well-documented analytical products that can be trusted by teams across the business.

You’ll play a key role in making sure our data is not only accurate and well-modelled, but also easy to understand, easy to use and ready to support future AI-enabled analytics.

What you’ll be doing day to day

As an Analytical Engineer, you’ll work closely with Data Engineering, Analytics, Product, Commercial and Technology teams to create high-quality analytical data products.

You’ll help define and maintain the business logic, metrics and semantic structures that sit between our data platform and the teams using data every day.

You’ll be responsible for:

Designing, building and maintaining trusted analytical models, transformations and semantic definitions across key business domains

Creating clear, reusable metric definitions that help ensure teams are working from a consistent source of truth

Working closely with Data Engineers to define requirements between refined data layers and business-ready analytical products

Building and improving dbt models, tests and documentation to support reliable, well-governed analytics

Helping shape semantic layers that support BI tools, modern analytical workbenches and emerging AI use cases such as natural language analytics and text-to-SQL

Improving data quality through testing, monitoring, validation and clear ownership of analytical models

Partnering with stakeholders to understand reporting and insight needs, then translating them into scalable, maintainable data products

Supporting self-service analytics by making data easier to discover, understand and use across the business

Exploring how AI-assisted tools can improve analytical engineering workflows, including SQL review, test generation, documentation and quality checks

You’ll be a great addition to our team if you have the following skills, knowledge and experience

Strong SQL skills and experience building analytical data models in a production environment

Data modelling approaches such as dimensional modelling, star schemas, metric layers or similar

Working with dbt or similar transformation and orchestration tools

Building semantic models or reusable business logic for BI, analytics or self-service reporting

Working in cloud data environments such as Databricks, BigQuery, Snowflake or similar

Applying engineering practices to analytics, including Git, pull requests, testing, documentation and CI/CD

Improving data quality, lineage, governance and trust across analytical datasets

Working closely with analysts, engineers, product teams and business stakeholders to turn complex requirements into practical data solutions

Communicating clearly with both technical and non-technical audiences

Making pragmatic decisions in fast-moving environments, balancing quality, usability and delivery

Nice to have

Experience with enterprise semantic layers or tools such as Hex, Looker, Cube, dbt Semantic Layer or similar

Exposure to AI-enabled analytics, text-to-SQL, natural language interfaces or agentic data workflows

Experience using AI tools such as Claude, Gemini or similar to improve SQL development, testing or documentation

Understanding of how AI agents consume structured data, metric definitions and business context

Experience working with data contracts, medallion-style architecture or silver-to-gold data modelling patterns

What success looks like

Success in this role is about helping the business trust and use data with confidence.

You’ll create reliable analytical models, clear metric definitions and reusable semantic layers that support better decisions across the business. You’ll help reduce ambiguity in reporting, improve the quality of business-ready data, and make our analytical products easier to find, understand and reuse.

You’ll also help us prepare for the next generation of data consumption, where AI tools and natural language interfaces rely on well-structured, well-documented and trusted data foundations.
