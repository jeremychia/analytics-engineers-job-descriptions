BI/Visualization Specialist

Location: Stamford, CT
The candidate in this position must be comfortable working on-site at our Stamford, CT corporate office 5 days a week.

Who You Are

As a BI/Visualization Specialist at Curaleaf, you'll design and optimize dashboards and reporting tools that translate complex data into clear, actionable insights. Omni is Curaleaf's primary business intelligence and visualization platform, and this role will play a key part in developing, maintaining, and enhancing Omni-based reporting solutions across the organization. This role requires strong technical skills, attention to detail, and the ability to support data-driven decision-making in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

What You'll Do

- Design, develop, and maintain impactful, user-friendly dashboards and data visualizations, with a primary focus on Omni, to deliver clear, actionable insights that support teams across the organization

- Manage the full lifecycle of data products, including development, training, ongoing support, and enhancements to align with evolving business needs

- Collaborate closely with data and product owners to ensure the standardization, cleanliness, and accuracy of data

- Recommend the right analytics tools and visualization approaches for various use cases, with a focus on automation, scalability, and clarity

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business needs into optimized data solutions, while fostering a data-driven culture through trusted visualizations and metrics

- Serve as a subject matter expert in data visualization, championing best practices in reporting and staying current with emerging tools, trends, and techniques in modern analytics

What You'll Bring

- 2-4 years of experience in BI development and data product design, with a track record of creating impactful, user-focused solutions

- Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

- Hands-on experience with modern BI and visualization platforms. Experience with Omni is strongly preferred; candidates with experience in tools such as Looker, Sigma, Tableau, or Power BI who can quickly learn and adapt to Omni are also encouraged to apply

- Strong understanding of data modeling, analytics, and programming languages such as SQL, Python, and R

- Experience working with modern data warehouse and database technologies, including Snowflake and other relational or NoSQL platforms

Even Better If

- You understand core UI/UX principles and apply them to create intuitive, user-friendly data visualizations

- You have prior experience building dashboards, data models, or reporting solutions in Omni
