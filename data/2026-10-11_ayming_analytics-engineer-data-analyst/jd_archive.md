Job Description

What will your responsibilities be at Ayming?

- Partner with business stakeholders to understand processes, challenges, and opportunities for improvement.

- Translate business requirements into data models, dashboards, KPIs, and analytics solutions.

- Build and maintain analytical datasets using Databricks, SQL, and Python.

- Develop and enhance BI reports, dashboards, and self-service analytics capabilities.

- Analyze local data sources and integrate them into the broader data ecosystem.

- Support the identification, design, and deployment of Data & AI use cases.

- Ensure data quality, consistency, and documentation of KPIs.

- Act as a bridge between business users and the central Data & AI team.

- Promote data-driven decision making across the Spain organization.

- Collaborate closely with the Data Engineer to industrialize analytics products.

You are

- Business-oriented and interested in understanding how the company operates.

- Comfortable working directly with operational teams, managers, and leadership.

- Curious about how Data & AI can solve real business problems.

- Proactive, autonomous, and solution-oriented.

- A strong communicator able to bridge business and technical teams.

- A collaborative team player with an international mindset.

Qualifications

You have

- 3+ years of experience in analytics, BI, analytics engineering, or related fields.

- Strong SQL skills and experience working with large datasets.

- Good Python programming skills for data preparation, analysis, and automation.

- Experience with Power BI or similar BI platforms (Looker, Tableau).

- Experience building dashboards, KPI frameworks, and analytical models.

- Familiarity with analytics platforms like Databricks (Snowflake, Jupyther Notebooks, Google Colab).

- Understanding of data modeling and analytical data structures.

- Ability to communicate insights to technical and non-technical audiences.

- Experience gathering business requirements and translating them into data solutions
