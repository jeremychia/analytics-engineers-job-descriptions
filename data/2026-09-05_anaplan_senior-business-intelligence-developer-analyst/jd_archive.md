At Anaplan, we are looking for a Senior Business Intelligence Developer/Analyst to act as a data domain holder for our engineering reporting and insights within the GPTO (Product and Technology) department.

Sitting within our Engineering Enablement team, you won't just be fulfilling requests—you will own high-visibility data domains end-to-end. As our engineering and AI capabilities grow, the demand for clear, actionable intelligence has skyrocketed. We have around 190 KPIs across delivery, quality, investment, and AI adoption that need a true owner. This role is key in enabling and measuring the success of Anaplan's internal AI initiatives.

We need someone who can take a vague question from leadership, figure out the underlying business problem, model the data, build the semantic layer, and deliver self-serve insights that actually make sense.

Your Impact

Act as a "data domain holder" for 2-3 subject areas (e.g., executive scorecards, dependency health, investment mix, or DORA performance) from raw data to final stakeholder presentation.

Design, build, and document semantic layers that outlast you, abstracting complex data from tools like Jira and GitHub into simple, self-serve reporting.

Contribute to the Engineering team with development tasks and test data to ensure quality and completeness

Partner with senior engineering leaders to translate ambiguous business questions into specific, defensible data answers.

Consult and push back on requirements, identifying the most useful metrics and deciding what should and shouldn't be measured.

Combine traditional metrics and AI telemetry to create meaningful measures of Anaplan's internal AI initiatives

Build and own the dashboards tracking delivery, quality, dependency, investment, and AI adoption metrics.

Advise the team on the evolving AI/BI tooling landscape (Rovo, Atlassian Intelligence, embedded AI), helping decide when to build versus when to consume.

Coach and mentor junior analysts, championing data best practices so outputs remain clean and consistent as tool access broadens.

Your Skills

Strong experience building production BI in a fast-paced software or tech environment.

Python for the harder transforms and a strong working knowledge of development practices and tools (code repositories, build tools, scripting languages, agile development)

Deep SQL expertise and demonstrated comfort with dimensional modelling against warehouses with hundreds of tables.

Hands-on experience with enterprise BI tools such as Tableau, PowerBI, or eazyBI.

Strong working knowledge of engineering intelligence, including DORA metrics, value-stream measurement, and CI/CD pipeline data.

Excellent communication and consulting skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders, shape ambiguous requirements, and explain data limitations to executives.

High autonomy and the ability to own a domain end-to-end without needing a large support team.

Desirable

Familiarity with the Atlassian data model (Jira, Confluence, Jira Align, JSM Assets).

Background in a software development agency, SaaS environment, or a setting requiring you to manage multiple stakeholders and shifting priorities.
