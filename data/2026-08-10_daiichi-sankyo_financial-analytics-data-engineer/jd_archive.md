Job Summary

This role collaborates closely with Financial Analytics Product Managers, Data Scientists, and further functions to translate analytical and product requirements into scalable data and analytics products. The role contributes to the development, operation and continuous improvement of data products and analytical capabilities by supporting data integration, transformation, operationalization and delivery activities.
