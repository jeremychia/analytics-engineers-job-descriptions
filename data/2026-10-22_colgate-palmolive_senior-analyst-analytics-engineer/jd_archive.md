Brief introduction - Role Summary/Purpose:

- You will empower our multi-functional teams by Creating advanced analytical solutions that drive data-centric decision making across the entire organization.

- Your primary focus will involve Developing and transforming datasets using the medallion architecture framework to meet diverse business needs.

- By Owning a modern tech stack—including Snowflake, dbt, Python, and SQL—you will build sophisticated data structures while ensuring all deliverables are efficient, sustainable, and thoroughly documented.

- You will collaborate closely with various stakeholders to translate complex requirements into impactful, compliant analytics products.

Responsibilities:

- Analytics Development

- You will Collaborate with diverse partners to gather needs and produce compliant, sustainable analytics products. This includes Building insightful visualizations through tools like Sigma or Domo.

- By Developing consumption layers with Snowflake, dbt, Python, and SQL, you will provide the robust data structures necessary for sophisticated business reporting.

- You will ensure all data progression remains high-quality by Following the established medallion architecture framework.

- You will Leverage our core technology stack to deploy pipelines that align with enterprise-wide best practices and standards.

- Process Optimization and Innovation

- Focus on Optimizing internal workflows by automating repetitive tasks and streamlining how analytics are delivered to the organization.

- You will be responsible for Evaluating modern and emerging technologies to keep our analytics engineering capabilities at the cutting edge.

- Collaboration and Support

- You will Translate high-level business data strategies into actionable analytics execution through consistent stakeholder engagement.

- Support daily operations by Assisting with technical hurdles related to data access and reporting needs.

- By Cultivating strong relationships across technical and business units, you will work toward shared organizational objectives.

- Governance and Standards

- Maintain high technical bars by Reviewing peer code and enforcing engineering best practices across all projects.

- You will Uphold rigorous compliance with all enterprise policies and data governance standards.

- Ensure long-term sustainability by Documenting and maintaining critical components within the data ecosystem.

- Education: Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field.

- Experience: At least 2 years of professional experience as an Analytics Engineer, Data Engineer or related role.
