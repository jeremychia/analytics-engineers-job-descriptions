Vallourec is looking for an Analytics Engineer

As part of Vallourec's Group Data & Analytics organization, the Analytics Engineer will contribute to the development of enterprise analytics capabilities by designing and maintaining trusted, scalable and governed data models.

The role acts as a bridge between Data Engineering teams, Data Governance teams and business stakeholders to deliver reliable datasets, governed metrics and impactful reporting solutions.

Key Responsibilities

Develop and maintain analytical data models

- Design, develop and maintain data models using dbt.
- Build reusable dimensional models, fact tables and business metrics.
- Ensure data quality, consistency and reliability.
- Contribute to data modelling and governance best practices.

Example Projects

- Development of certified datasets for business domains.
- Design of dimensional analytical models and semantic layers.
- KPI governance and standardization initiatives.
- Automated data quality controls implementation.
- Data documentation and governance activities.
- Development and optimization of Power BI dashboards.

Contribute to data and metric governance

- Define and maintain business metrics, dimensions, hierarchies and business rules.
- Ensure documentation, lineage and governance of KPIs.
- Support data certification and metric governance processes.
- Contribute to the standardization of business definitions across the Group.

Develop Business Intelligence solutions

- Design, build and optimize Power BI dashboards.
- Translate business requirements into scalable analytical solutions.
- Support continuous improvement of analytics capabilities.
- Enable self-service analytics adoption across business teams.
