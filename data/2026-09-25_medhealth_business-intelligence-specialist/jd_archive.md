Job Description

Reporting to the Manager, BI and Finance, you'll play a key role in supporting the organisation's growing reporting and analytics needs. With several concurrent projects underway, you'll help increase team capacity while delivering scalable, high-quality reporting solutions.
This role will focus on:
- Supporting the migration of reporting and data assets from SQL Server to Databricks
- Designing and implementing robust data models that align with BI best practices
- Developing, maintaining and enhancing Power BI dashboards and reports
- Refactoring existing reports to improve usability, performance and governance
- Writing and optimising SQL queries to extract, transform and validate data
- Building DAX measures and semantic models that support business decision-making
- Working closely with stakeholders to understand reporting requirements and deliver actionable insights
- Supporting continuous improvement initiatives across reporting, analytics and data management functions
- Work to KPIs and performance targets in a fast-paced, purpose-driven environment

Qualifications

To be successful in this role, you'll bring:
- Demonstrated experience working with Databricks in a reporting or analytics environment
- Strong Power BI development experience, including dashboards, data models and DAX
- Solid SQL skills with experience working in SQL Server environments
- Experience designing and optimising data models for reporting and analytics
- Strong understanding of BI best practices, data governance and reporting standards
- Excellent stakeholder engagement and communication skills
- Ability to manage competing priorities across multiple projects and deadlines

Desirable:
- Experience working on data migration or transformation projects
- Exposure to Employment Services, Government contracting, or large multi-site organisations
- Tertiary qualifications in Information Technology, Business Intelligence or a related discipline
