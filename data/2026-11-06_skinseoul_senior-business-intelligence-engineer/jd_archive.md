Senior Business Intelligence Engineer
SkinSeoul · Seoul, South Korea

About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Business Intelligence Engineer to lead the development of scalable data infrastructure and analytics capabilities across our e-commerce ecosystem. In this role, you will be responsible for designing and managing data pipelines, developing business intelligence dashboards, and transforming complex data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making across marketing, product, and operations.

You will work closely with cross-functional teams to ensure data is accurate, accessible, and effectively utilized to drive business performance, improve customer understanding, and support the company’s growth through data-driven initiatives.

Responsibility

A. Data Integration & Pipeline Development

- Design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines integrating data from e-commerce platforms, marketing tools, and CRM systems into the BigQuery data warehouse.

- Manage ETL processes to ensure efficient data ingestion, transformation, and availability for analytics.

- Monitor pipeline performance and troubleshoot data issues when needed.

B. Business Intelligence & Analytics

- Develop and maintain business intelligence dashboards and reports (e.g., Looker Studio).

- Analyze key e-commerce metrics such as sales performance, customer behavior, conversion rates, and marketing ROI.

- Translate business requirements into meaningful data insights and visualizations.

C. Data Quality & Governance

- Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and reliability across datasets and reporting systems.

- Implement best practices for data validation, documentation, and governance.

- Maintain organized and scalable data models for analytics use.

D. Cross-Functional Collaboration

- Work closely with Marketing, Product, and Operations teams to define KPIs and data requirements.

- Support stakeholders in understanding data insights and making data-driven decisions.

- Provide data support for business initiatives and performance analysis.

E. Performance Optimization

- Optimize SQL queries, data models, and pipelines for efficiency and scalability.

- Improve data processing performance to support faster reporting and analysis.

- Continuously identify opportunities to streamline analytics workflows.

F. Tracking & Measurement

- Implement and manage Google Tag Manager (GTM) for accurate event tracking and user behavior monitoring.

- Support tracking implementation for e-commerce and marketing analytics.

- Ensure tracking data is reliable for reporting and decision-making.

Requirements

- Minimum 5 years of experience in Business Intelligence, Data Engineering, or Analytics roles within e-commerce companies.

- At least 2 years of experience in leadership roles and has led a team of at least 2–3 people.

- Strong proficiency in SQL and relational databases.

- Experience working with cloud data warehouses (e.g., BigQuery) and building data pipelines or ETL processes.

- Hands-on experience with Looker Studio, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), and Google Tag Manager (GTM).

- Prior experience leading or mentoring a data or analytics team.

- Fluent in English, both written and spoken.

- Experience in the beauty industry is a strong plus.

- Able to have Hybrid-working setup in MARU180, 180 Yeoksam-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul.

What You Will Gain by Working at SkinSeoul

- Be at the forefront of a company pioneering and disrupting the e-commerce and skincare industry with advanced AI technology.
