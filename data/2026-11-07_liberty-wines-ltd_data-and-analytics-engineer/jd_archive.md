Data and Analytics Engineer
Liberty Wines Ltd
Clapham, England, United Kingdom

Liberty Wines is transforming its data estate, migrating from on-premises platforms to a modern cloud-based analytics environment while expanding its AI and machine learning capabilities. Working alongside an experienced data consultancy, we are building a greenfield analytics platform using technologies such as BigQuery, Snowflake, dbt, Looker and Omni.

As our Data & Analytics Engineer, you will play a pivotal role in shaping the platform from the ground up, working across data engineering, modelling, reporting and stakeholder enablement. This is a rare opportunity to influence technology choices, establish best practices, and help embed a data-driven culture across the business.

What will you be doing

- Data Platform Build-out

- Design relational & lakehouse schemas in a modern cloud warehouse (e.g., BigQuery, Snowflake) using dbt.

- Work closely with our consultants on the re-architecture of SSAS cubes to modern Lakehouse models, a great hands-on learning opportunity.

- Set up layered, tested dbt models (staging through to governed marts) and ensure data pipelines are optimal and maintained.

- Contribute to embedding the new data platform and architecture in the business and working to ensure our investment is maximised.

- Data Engineering

- Enhance, develop, and maintain ETL / ELT, middleware, and integrations.

- Ingest data from WMS, ERP, ecommerce, IoT and ad-hoc sources (CSV, APIs, unstructured).

- Analytics & Reporting

- Model data (dbt and a governed semantic layer) and build stunning dashboards used by Sales, Buying, Ops & Finance

- Optimise datasets for performance, cost, and freshness (partitioning, clustering, and incremental models).

- AI / ML Enablement

- Work with Liberty’s AI & Automation Dev Team to prepare and expose curated data to our AI/ML toolsets and workflows.

- Stakeholder Enablement & Documentation

- Maintain clear documentation for BI projects, processes, and solutions.

- Enforce data governance practices to ensure compliance with organizational standards.

- Upskill analysts through show-and-tell sessions and guard-rail documentation.

- Governance & DevOps

- Monitor and improve CI/CD processes, source control (Git), and automate dbt and BI platform deployments.

- Adhere to and maintain data-quality rules, lineage, and catalogue standards.

About you

- Solid foundations in Data Engineering, BI Development or Analytics Engineering, with a strong desire to develop and grow alongside the platform

- Strong SQL skills and a solid understanding of data-modelling principles (e.g. dimensional modelling, 3NF)

- Working knowledge of SQL Server or a comparable relational engine beyond writing queries: execution plans, indexing and locking

- Experience in ELT/ELT and extracting from a live transactional system without disrupting it, and working to production change control

- Experience designing and building modern data pipelines and Lakehouse architectures

- Hands-on experience with at least one enterprise-grade data platform (e.g., Google BigQuery, Snowflake, Databricks, or equivalent)

- Familiarity with cloud services and deployment automation (e.g., GCP, AWS, Terraform, CI/CD, Git)

- Ability to deliver clear, insightful, and performant data visualisations and dashboards using tools such as Looker, Omni, Power BI, or other modern BI platforms

- Agile mindset: embraces iterative delivery, peer code reviews, and test-driven development

- Excellent stakeholder engagement and communication skills

Desirable:

- Experience with Lakehouse patterns; setting standards, quality checks etc.

- Parquet partitioning and clustering to optimise for query speed and cost

- GitHub Actions

- REST APIs

- ML Ops tooling
