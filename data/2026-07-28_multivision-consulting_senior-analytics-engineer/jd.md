Senior Analytics Engineer – 1x per Week in Lisbon

We are looking for a Senior Analytics Engineer to design, build, and optimize data solutions that help teams make better business decisions.
The role involves creating data models, pipelines, and analytics products, working with modern cloud technologies, and ensuring data is reliable, secure, and easy to use.

Main Responsibilities
- Build and maintain data pipelines and data models for analytics and reporting.
- Design solutions using modern cloud data platforms and Lakehouse architectures.
- Develop and optimize ETL/ELT processes.
- Ensure data quality, performance, security, and scalability.
- Use Git and CI/CD practices to manage data development workflows.
- Work closely with data scientists, analysts, and business teams to transform business needs into technical solutions.
- Follow data engineering best practices and company standards.

Must Have:
- Strong SQL skills.
- Experience with data modeling and ETL workflows.
- Experience with Snowflake.
- Knowledge of dbt for data transformation and modeling.
- Experience with cloud data platforms, preferably AWS.
- Experience building data architectures for analytics and BI.
- Experience with Git/GitHub/GitLab and CI/CD pipelines.
- Strong communication skills and fluent English.

Nice to Have:
- Python or R for scripting and data analysis.
- Power BI or Tableau experience.
- Apache Airflow or other orchestration tools.
- Agile/Scrum experience.
- Knowledge of AI and Machine Learning applied to data analytics.

What we offer:
- Permanent employment contract
- Health insurance
- Meal allowance of €10.46/day
- Training package with certifications of your choice
- Mental health and well-being program
