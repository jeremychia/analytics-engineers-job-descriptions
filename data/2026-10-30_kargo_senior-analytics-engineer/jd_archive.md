Kargo Global LLC seeks a Senior Analytics Engineer in New York, NY to:

- Analyze large datasets using SQL, Python, or similar tools to support business

decision-making.

- Develop and maintain data models to organize and interpret data.

- Perform data analysis to identify trends, patterns, and insights.

- Build and maintain data pipelines for processing structured and unstructured

data.

- Apply data modeling and analytical techniques to improve data accuracy and

usability.

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data needs and deliver

analytical solutions.

- Prepare reports, dashboards, and visualizations to communicate findings.

Requirements:

The employer will accept a Bachelor's degree (or foreign equivalent) in Computer

Science or related field and five (5) years of experience in data analysis, data modeling

or a related field.

Alternatively, the employer will accept a Master's degree (or foreign equivalent) in

Computer Science or related field and two (2) years of experience in data analysis, data

modeling or a related field.

Position requires experience in the following:

- Python and SQL.

- Statistical Analysis.

- Data Modeling.

- Analyzing Large Datasets.

- Data Visualization Tools.
