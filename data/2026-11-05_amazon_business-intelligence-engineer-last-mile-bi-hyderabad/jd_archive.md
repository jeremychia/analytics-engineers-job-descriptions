Amazon.com’s success is built on a foundation of customer obsession. Every day, Amazon delivers millions of packages to customers around the world with clock-like precision. Behind every successful delivery, billions of decisions are made by systems and by people in real time. Is the customer's address accurate? Can we pinpoint it on a map? Is an unattended delivery safe? Is there an access code for the building? What are the business hours if it's a commercial address? How do we optimize a delivery associate's route across dozens of stops? The list goes on.

At the core of all of it lies quality of underlying data that can help make those decisions in time.

This position requires a results-oriented, high-energy, dynamic individual with both stamina and mental quickness to be able to work and thrive in a fast-paced, high-growth global organization.
