Position Overview

The Data Analytics Engineer designs, builds, and supports RugsUSA's enterprise data warehouse and business intelligence platform. Working closely with business stakeholders and cross-functional teams, this role develops scalable data models, pipelines, and dashboards using the Fivetran, Snowflake, dbt, and Sigma stack to deliver reliable, high-quality data and actionable insights. The role also supports ongoing platform performance, data quality, and self-service analytics across the organization.

This is a hybrid role requiring on-site presence in our Manhattan office 3 to 4 days per week.

Essential Job Functions

Stand up data warehouse and business intelligence tools

- As part of a results-oriented team, participate in the design, implementation, maintenance and support of our Fivetran > Snowflake > dbt > Sigma data warehouse stack to enable the business to make informed decisions.
- Interact with internal stakeholders to gather/develop functional and technical requirements and translate them into reporting solutions.
- Provide quality and performant data modeling and visualizations solutions to RugsUSA cross functional business units
- Work collaboratively with Enterprise Data Analysts, Data Governance Team to help define, develop and implement solutions and provide services for the enterprise data warehouse.
