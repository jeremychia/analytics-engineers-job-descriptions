Data & Analytics Engineer
Bit Soft
Bucharest, Romania

About Us

Bitsoft is the market leader for software development of applications that help hotels and restaurants across Romania, Hungary, Bulgaria, and France run smooth and effective businesses. We are the strategic partner for multiple restaurant and hotel chains such as City Grill, KFC, Burger King, Starbucks, Radisson, JW Marriott, and Ramada. We are a growing business, aiming for expansion to other European markets.

Role Overview

We are looking for a Data and Analytics Engineer to join Bitsoft in Bucharest, reporting directly to the Software Development Manager - Digital. You will build reliable pipelines, define business metrics, and deliver hospitality analytics. You will also build agentic workflows that query governed data, coordinate tools, and automate analysis and business tasks.

Our current stack combines Apache Superset for business intelligence, AWS DMS for database replication, AWS Glue for data processing and cataloging, Apache Iceberg for lakehouse tables, and Amazon Athena for SQL analytics.

Working with product, engineering, finance, marketing, and customer-facing teams, you will take data solutions from requirements through deployment and monitoring. Priorities will be agreed with the Software Development Manager - Digital and product stakeholders, with an emphasis on dependable data, measurable business value, and maintainable solutions.

Responsibilities

Build and Operate AWS Data Pipelines

Use AWS DMS for database full loads and CDC, and AWS Glue to transform ingested data into curated Iceberg tables. Handle inserts, updates, deletes, duplicates, late events, and safe reprocessing.

Monitor DMS replication lag, Glue jobs, data freshness, Athena query performance, and AWS costs. Resolve failures, document recovery procedures, and improve recurring problems.

Maintain Iceberg partitions, compact small files, and manage snapshots and retention to balance query performance, storage cost, and recovery needs.

Create Trusted Models and Metrics

Build documented Iceberg data models and shared KPI definitions with business owners. Publish reusable Athena queries and Superset datasets and metrics, with clear grain, lineage, and ownership.

Implement automated quality checks and reconcile critical figures with source systems. Verify customer isolation across Superset dashboards, SQL access, AWS permissions, and AI interfaces.

Deliver Superset Dashboards and Business Insights

Develop Apache Superset dashboards and Athena analyses for hospitality operations, product usage, finance, and marketing. Configure filters and caching appropriately, and explain findings and limitations.

Enable self-service analytics through clear documentation, discoverable datasets, and support for business users.

Build AI Applications and Agentic Workflows

Design, build, and operate multi-step agentic workflows over governed data queried through Amazon Athena, coordinating LLM reasoning with SQL and approved tools or APIs.

Build workflows that detect anomalies, investigate KPI changes, or automate recurring reporting. Handle state, retries, and failures, and record execution traces for monitoring and debugging.

Evaluate task completion, answer accuracy, latency, and cost. Enforce customer isolation, read-only analytical access, and query limits; require human approval for actions that change business data.

Collaborate and Improve

Contribute to the data roadmap with the Software Development Manager - Digital and product stakeholders. Prioritize work by business value, data readiness, and effort.

Deliver reviewed, tested, and monitored releases. Share knowledge and adopt tools that improve quality, maintainability, or delivery speed.
