Why join us?

We're looking for a Senior Analytics Engineer to help shape how data powers our products.

This isn't a traditional data engineering or BI role. We're looking for someone who enjoys working at the intersection of data architecture, analytics, product development, and engineering.

You'll work closely with Product and R&D teams to design and evolve data models, build reliable pipelines, define meaningful metrics, and turn complex data into insights that improve our products and business decisions.

Depending on the product, your work could involve anything from developing data architecture and monitoring systems for our AI-powered platform to analyzing product performance, building customer-facing dashboards, and identifying opportunities to improve features and business outcomes.

You'll have the autonomy to make technical decisions, influence product development, and take ownership of projects from planning through deployment.

We're also embracing AI across our development workflows. We believe tools like Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, and other AI-powered solutions can fundamentally change how we work, and we're looking for someone who's excited to explore that potential.
