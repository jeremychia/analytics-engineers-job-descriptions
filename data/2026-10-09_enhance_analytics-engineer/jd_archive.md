About the role

Enhance is hiring a Junior Analytics Engineer in Dubai to help turn our data into insights that improve personal training (PT) across our partner gyms.

Enhance powers personal training for gym operators around the world. Our B2B SaaS platform helps gym chains sell, schedule and manage PT, and we also run PT services directly inside our partners' clubs. That gives us a rare view of PT across many brands, clubs and trainers, and this role helps turn that view into better decisions for every partner.

You'll join the Data team and learn from senior analytics engineers while building real models and dashboards from day one. You'll work with club managers, PT leads, Operations and Finance to answer questions about how clubs and trainers perform, how sessions are sold and used, and how pricing affects revenue and retention.

What you'll do

- Build and maintain data models. Write and update transformation models (dbt or similar) for PT packages, sessions, trainer schedules and revenue, with guidance from senior engineers.

- Help keep our metrics consistent. Apply our shared definitions for core numbers, such as how busy trainers are, how many clients stay and renew, and how much revenue each club and trainer brings in.

- Support pricing analysis. Prepare the data behind pricing reviews and help analyse how package price and discounts affect sales and margin.

- Support operations reporting. Build views of demand by club and time slot and of trainer availability, so teams can schedule and staff better.

- Build dashboards and reports. Create and maintain dashboards that club managers and PT leads use every week, and answer their ad hoc data questions.

- Keep data trustworthy. Add tests and documentation, check data quality, and flag issues before they reach a dashboard.

- Learn and grow. Take part in code reviews, pair with senior engineers, and take on more ownership as you grow.
