About The Role

We're scaling quickly, and data is a critical lever to unlock growth and improve patient outcomes. As our Analytics Engineering Lead, you'll drive the strategy for a specific business function. You'll collaborate with stakeholders to design and execute Nourish's growth roadmap, and identify how to scale more efficiently.

This role blends analytics engineering and strategic insights. You'll be responsible for building clean, scalable data models, and surfacing clear, actionable insights. As an early hire on the Data team at Nourish, you'll help define our long-term vision for data and collaborate closely with our Operations, Engineering, and Product teams.

Our Data team is based in NYC and goes into the office 2-3 days a week. While this isn't a strict requirement, we are looking for people who would be excited to be part of that culture.

Key Responsibilities

- Own the full analytics stack for a business-critical function: Take full ownership of the analytics workflow—from raw data to models, dashboards, and insights.

- Deliver high-impact insights: Partner with business stakeholders to proactively identify and lead analyses that uncover growth and efficiency levers, and shape team priorities.

- Build dashboards that drive action: Design intuitive dashboards for a broad range of stakeholders that deliver clarity and enable fast decision-making.

- Build and maintain a robust data foundation: Partner with Engineering to validate and optimize pipelines, ensuring our data infrastructure is accurate, scalable, and supports a trusted single source of truth.

- Champion a data-first culture: Guide teams to interpret and act on data, write clear documentation, and lead by example in driving evidence-based decisions.

We'd Love to Hear From You If You...

- Have 5+ years of relevant experience owning analytics end to end at a fast-paced, high-growth company.

- Have deep domain expertise in analytics engineering: You write clean, performant SQL, have hands-on experience with tools like dbt and Fivetran, and have a strong grasp of the fundamentals of data modeling, ETLs, and infrastructure.

- Are curious, strategic, and outcome-oriented: You proactively shape the right questions, translate complex data into clear, actionable insights, and balance speed with depth to drive clear, high-impact decisions across the business

- Are a builder who thrives on ownership and impact: You take pride in designing clean, scalable systems from scratch, and are excited to take end-to-end ownership - filling gaps, creating structure, and becoming the go-to person others rely on for clarity.

- Communicate complex ideas with clarity: You distill deep analysis into crisp, actionable takeaways, whether writing a Notion doc or presenting to leadership, and you earn trust across cross-functional teams by speaking the language of both operations and engineering

- Are motivated by impact and mission: You believe in the power of food as medicine to transform healthcare and are excited to improve lives at scale by contributing to Nourish’s mission.
