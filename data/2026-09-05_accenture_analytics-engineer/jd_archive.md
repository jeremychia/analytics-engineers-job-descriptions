Analytics Engineer

Data Eng, Mgmt & Governance Senior Analyst | Early Career | Full time

Job Summary

We are seeking highly qualified professionals to join our team as an Analytics Engineer Mid. As essential members of our data team, you will be responsible for transforming complex data into reliable analytical models and semantic layers that enable business intelligence, data science, and machine learning initiatives. We are looking for professionals with strong expertise in data modeling, SQL, ELT pipelines, data quality, and cloud analytics platforms.

Requisitos

Qualifications

Minimum of 3 years of professional experience in analytics, data engineering, or related data environments

Advanced proficiency in SQL, including query optimization, partitioning, clustering, and large-scale data processing

Strong experience with dimensional modeling, star schemas, medallion architecture, schema evolution, and semantic layer design

Experience building and optimizing ELT pipelines and analytical data models

Hands-on experience with orchestration tools such as Azure Data Factory, Apache Airflow, Microsoft Fabric, or Databricks Workflows

Proficiency in Python, PySpark, or Spark SQL for advanced data transformations

Knowledge of CI/CD, version control, and DevOps practices for data pipelines

Experience with cloud data platforms such as BigQuery, Snowflake, Azure Synapse, Delta Lake, ADLS, or Amazon Redshift

Knowledge of data governance, cataloging, and lineage concepts

Experience supporting BI, Data Science, Data Engineering, and Machine Learning teams

Degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Analytics, Machine Learning, Engineering, or a related field

English proficiency at B2 level or higher (mandatory; this will be assessed throughout the process).

Responsibilities

Model and transform data into consistent, scalable, and business-ready analytical structures

Design, build, and optimize ELT pipelines, transformation workflows, and automated quality tests

Develop dimensional models, semantic layers, and reusable datasets for reporting and advanced analytics

Optimize data models and SQL workloads for performance, scalability, and cost efficiency

Document business rules, data lineage, transformation logic, dependencies, and technical decisions

Monitor datasets, pipeline execution, data quality, service-level agreements, and platform performance

Implement data validation rules and observability controls to identify issues proactively

Translate business needs into clear, actionable, and scalable technical requirements

Apply governance, cataloging, security, and lineage standards across analytical assets

Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, version control processes, and automated deployment practices

Investigate complex data issues, identify root causes, and implement sustainable solutions

Maintain delivery quality while proactively communicating dependencies, risks, and technical constraints

Support junior team members through technical guidance, functional reviews, and knowledge sharing
