Analytics Engineer (BI Team)
OEC
Poland

Job Summary

Analytics Engineer in BI Team will work with OEC engineers to maintain all the database's safety and security to prevent unauthorized access and potential leaks of confidential data. You will join a team focused on creating tools and solutions that leverage the massive amount of data OEC processes each day. We build end-to-end analytics systems spanning data ingestion, transformation, warehousing, and reporting, and we are actively building AI-powered agents and assistants that help clients unlock insights from their data.

What You’ll Accomplish:

- Develop and maintain data infrastructure for analytics here at OEC.

- Build pipelines to extract data from primary and secondary sources.

- Build and support data visualization for Business and Product needs.

- Work with business and tech Product Managers to define reporting and prepare reports to assist with tracking and achieving their goals.

- Analyze parts and application data for trends, patterns, errors.

- Work with Engineers and Software Developers to identify potential data issues and help fix them.

- Contribute to planning and building analytics infrastructure.

- Build and maintain our modern data architecture by staging, transforming and finalizing datasets that meet our high standards of quality and are reused across the organization.

- Help develop and document this central source of truth for OEC and assist in our internal digital transformation.

Who You Are:

- You are well-versed in Mathematics and/or Statistics.

- You enjoy critical thinking, data-oriented, systematic approaches to solving problems.

- You have 3+ years of experience in data engineering, analytics engineering or business intelligence.

- You are proficient with the following: SQL, Python (incl. PySpark), Database Design, Dimensional Data Modeling, ETL/ELT Frameworks, Data Quality, Data Visualization: Power BI (DAX), Sigma or Tableau, Exploratory Data Analysis, Microsoft Fabric (Data Factory Pipelines, Dataflows Gen2, Notebooks), Cloud Data Warehouse: Microsoft Fabric (Lakehouse, Warehouse, OneLake / Delta Lake); familiarity with Snowflake and dbt is a plus.

- You are articulate. Your communication skills are top-notch, both in writing and verbal.

- You communicate fluently in English, both written and spoken.

- Able to keep track of multiple projects concurrently.

- Ability to work independently in an environment that expects great things every day.

- You pride yourself on being tech-savvy. You learn new processes and software tools quickly. If there is a problem with the data, you feel like you can’t rest until you figure it out.

- You’re very detail-oriented and organized. You believe in neatness, and you take pride in a job that is well done!
