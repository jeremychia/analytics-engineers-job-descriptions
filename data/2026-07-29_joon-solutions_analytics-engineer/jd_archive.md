Responsibilities

As an analytics engineer at Joon Solutions, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and implementing data solutions that meet our clients' needs. You will work with our clients to understand their business objectives, gather requirements, and develop solutions that enable them to make data-driven decisions. Your responsibilities will include:

Design and develop data pipelines that extract, transform, and load data from various sources into our clients' data warehouses using modern data stack technologies.

Design and maintain data models that provide a structured view of the data, allowing our clients to analyze and explore their data easily.

Perform descriptive analysis to provide valuable and actionable insights for clients' businesses. Prepare reports for the management stating trends, patterns, and predictions using relevant data.

Develop ETL processes to automate data ingestion and ensure data quality.

Work closely with our data scientists, data engineers, and project managers to deliver high-quality data solutions to our clients.

Ensure our clients' data is secured and compliant with relevant regulations and standards.

Provide technical support to our clients, troubleshoot issues, and ensure timely resolution of technical problems.

Participate in code reviews to ensure that our solutions are of high quality and follow best practices.

Contribute to the company's growth by identifying new opportunities for our services, building relationships with clients, and delivering high-quality solutions that exceed their expectations.

Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and technologies in data analytics and continuously improve your skills and knowledge.
