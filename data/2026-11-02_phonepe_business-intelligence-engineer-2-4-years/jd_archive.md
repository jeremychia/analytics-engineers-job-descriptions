Job Description

What kind of person are we looking for?

Strong data-first BI Engineers who are passionate about working with data and building systems that will provide data availability for an organization that’s growing at scale. If you’re a curious mind who constantly questions the status-quo, then you’d fit right in with us.

What would you get to do in this role?

- Be an integral part of defining the data consumption strategy across the org. You’ll also monitor performance trends and influence any interventions based on performance.

- Collaborate closely with the engineering and product functions to understand the data flow and architecture, finding the best tech solutions from an Analytics standpoint to build scalable data models.

- Drive integration of AI capabilities to build forward-looking, BI-focused applications and automated insights.

- Define processes and best practices when it comes to data modeling, consumption, querying, and reporting.

- Become an SME in the tech stack being used across the different analytics functionalities and become the go-to person for unblocking any tech boundaries.

Qualifications

What do you need to have to apply for this position?

- 2-4 years of data analytics or BI engineering experience in relevant roles.

- Advanced SQL & Big Data: Ability to write complex queries to manipulate and consolidate multiple data sources.

- Hive Expertise: Good knowledge of Hive internals, big data querying, and query optimization techniques.

- BI & Visualization Principles: A strong grasp of data visualization and BI design principles. (Expertise in specific tools like Qlik, PowerBI, or Tableau is not strictly required; we value your foundational understanding of how to build effective visual data stories).

- AI Integration: Knowledge of leveraging AI/LLMs to build smart, BI-focused applications.

- Data Architecture: Strong understanding of data handling, data modeling, and data flows.

- Communication: Ability to clearly explain thoughts and ideas either verbally or in written form.

- Knowledge of Python (especially for data processing, pipelines, or AI integrations).

- Good to have (Bonus Skills): Understanding of basic engineering concepts behind Airflow pipelines and data orchestration.
