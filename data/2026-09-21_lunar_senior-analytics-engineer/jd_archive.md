As our new Analytics Engineer you'll join Lunar's new Money Core – Regulatory squad, based in Copenhagen and reporting to Raul. We're re-establishing and unifying Lunar's regulatory data layer, one harmonized foundation that every compliance report can be built on.
What will you do?

- Build and own dbt models on Lunar's data warehouse that turn validated events into regulator-ready reporting outputs, forming a harmonized data layer that feeds regulatory reports.

- Work with a versioned storage layer for reportable snapshots, so we can reproduce or audit exactly what was reported for any given period.

- Define and maintain the data contract product squads must publish events against, and hold the line on it.

- Set up and run a lightweight change-management process for regulatory dbt models.

- Work with Airflow DAGs that orchestrate our dbt runs and scheduled regulatory exports.

- Stay close to new and upcoming regulatory scope as Lunar's product footprint grows.

What are we looking for in you?

- Strong SQL skills with experience building and maintaining dbt models (or similar transformation frameworks) on a modern data warehouse, including ongoing testing.

- Experience with data quality, lineage, or data contract practices across teams, ideally in a regulated or compliance-heavy environment.

- Familiarity with data classification or tagging approaches for sensitive data (PII, financial fields).

- Comfortable working with legal and compliance stakeholders, translating their requirements into a technical change-request process.

- Comfortable operating with ambiguity: this is a new team, so you'll help shape how we work.

- Nice to have: experience working with XML or other structured regulatory reporting formats.
