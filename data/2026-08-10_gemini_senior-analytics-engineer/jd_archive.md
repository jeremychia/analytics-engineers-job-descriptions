The Role:

Senior Analytics Engineer

As a member of our data team, you'll deliver high-quality work while designing scalable data models and solving challenges that impact the team's data architecture. You'll stay current with advances in the modern data ecosystem and apply them to build scalable solutions that align with the team's long-term goals. Your work will involve identifying root causes, documenting solutions, and implementing them with operational excellence in mind through data quality, validation, automation, and maintainability. Communicating technical concepts and analytical insights with leaders across the organization is paramount to success.

Responsibilities:

Design, architect and implement best-in-class data warehousing and reporting solutions

Design, build, and evolve scalable dimensional data models that serve as trusted foundations for Data Warehouse and BI solutions

Partner with product managers, data engineers, and business stakeholders to translate business requirements into scalable data models and actionable insights

Perform analysis to derive actionable insights for key stakeholders

Perform root cause analysis and resolve production and data issues

Develop and maintain automated data quality checks, validation frameworks, and testing to ensure the accuracy and reliability of data assets

Optimize SQL transformations and data models for performance, scalability, and maintainability

Build and maintain data dictionary and process documentation

Research new tools and technologies to improve existing processes

Minimum Qualifications:

5+ years of experience in analytics engineering, data engineering, or similar roles

Strong experience designing dimensional data models and schemas for enterprise data warehouses

Strong experience designing, building, and maintaining scalable data transformation pipelines

Strong experience developing analytics and BI solutions using platforms such as Looker, Tableau, or Power BI

Advanced SQL skills are required. Proficiency with Python for data transformation and automation is preferred

Experience translating business requirements into scalable dimensional data models and actionable insights

Experience with modern data transformation frameworks such as dbt

Experienced in working collaboratively across different teams and departments

Strong technical and business communication

Preferred Qualifications:

Experience designing enterprise-scale dimensional data models and semantic layers

Experience building trusted, reusable datasets for self-service analytics

Knowledge and experience of blockchain, financial markets, banking or exchanges
