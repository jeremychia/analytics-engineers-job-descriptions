Data Analytics Engineer (f/m/d)

A Snapshot of Your Day

Join the Data & Cloud Infrastructure team within Process Digitalization and help expand IoT Data Products for the Factory Data Cloud across global Transformer factories as a Data Analytics Engineer (f/m/d). You will design scalable data models, define standardized KPIs, and create reusable data products within an AI-enabled Data Mesh architecture. Working with production and IoT data, you will translate business requirements into trusted, production-ready solutions. You will collaborate with global factories and cross-functional teams to support consistent data use and industrial digitalization. Your work will help turn operational data into scalable and reliable business assets across more than 30 manufacturing sites.

How You’ll Make An Impact

- Own data products end-to-end, from understanding business requirements to delivering tested, documented, and production-ready transformation logic

- Design and apply layered data models using medallion architecture and dimensional modeling to create reusable and scalable data products

- Define and standardize KPIs by translating requirements from global factories and business functions into shared business metrics

- Build data quality checks, governance practices, and maintainability standards into data solutions from the start, while contributing to engineering standards through code reviews and shared development practices

- Collaborate with Data Engineers, Product Owners, and domain experts to support the Factory Data Cloud implementation and turn operational and IoT data into trusted assets

What You Bring

- University degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Data and Process Management, or a comparable discipline

- Many years of hands-on experience developing and operating analytics or data warehouse models in production environments, including dbt and Snowflake

- Strong expertise in dimensional modeling, medallion architecture, advanced SQL, dbt, and Snowflake Data Cloud

- Experience with Git, code reviews, testing, data quality, data governance, and CI/CD pipelines for analytics

- Knowledge of manufacturing and Industrial IoT data, KPI definition and standardization, strong stakeholder communication skills

- Fluency in English, and Good German

- dbt Certified Developer and Snowflake SnowPro Core Certification are a plus
