🎯 The Role

We're looking for a Senior Analytics / Data Engineer to join our Data team and take ownership of Filigran's Enterprise Data Platform (EDP) end-to-end — from ingestion to consumption. This is a senior, hands-on role: you turn raw data from across the company into reliable pipelines, trusted models, and governed, high-quality data that every team can rely on to make decisions.

The core of the role is Analytics Engineering — modeling, data quality, observability and governance. You'll also need enough Data Engineering skill to maintain and troubleshoot ingestion pipelines when they break, and a Data Analyst / BI mindset is a valuable bonus to help teams turn data into KPIs and dashboards.

You'll collaborate with virtually every team at Filigran (Sales, Finance, Product, Engineering, Support, People) in a fully remote, high-trust, async-first environment. This is a highly cross-functional role, and you'll help the whole company become more data-driven.

💼 Your Responsibilities

🧩 Analytics Engineering — the core of the role

Model and transform data with dbt, following a clean staging → intermediate → marts architecture.

Own data quality, reliability and observability: testing, monitoring, alerting on data issues, code reviews.

Maintain the data catalog and governance so data stays discoverable, understood, trusted and well-documented.

Build and maintain dashboards in Metabase that give teams clear, trustworthy visibility into their KPIs.

⚙️ Data Engineering — a necessary skill

Maintain and scale our ingestion pipelines (Airbyte / Airflow) that bring data from external APIs and sources into our warehouse.

Investigate and fix pipelines when they break, ensuring the platform keeps running reliably end-to-end.

📊 Data Analyst / BI — a valuable bonus

Partner with teams across the company (Sales, Finance, Product, Engineering, Support, People) to define metrics, model their KPIs, and support their decision-making.

Help teams build self-serve analytics and dashboards on top of the platform.

🔭 Across the board

Conduct technical watch and continuously improve the platform's architecture and tooling.

🤝 Who You'll Work With

Collaborate with stakeholders across every team (Sales, Finance, Product, Engineering, Support, People) to turn their needs into data products.

Work closely with the Engineering team on deployment, monitoring, and platform reliability.

🛡️ About the Data Platform

Filigran's data platform powers decision-making across the entire company. It ingests data from dozens of sources (Airbyte / Airflow), transforms it into trusted models with dbt, and serves it through Metabase dashboards and a maintained data catalog.

We have ambitious plans to take the platform to the next level, and you'll help shape them:

AI agents to make data more accessible — natural-language querying and agentic assistants so anyone at Filigran can get answers without writing SQL.

A self-reporting agent that automatically generates and distributes insights and narratives from the data.

A proactive alerting system that watches key metrics and notifies teams when values cross meaningful thresholds.

A semantic layer (e.g. Cube) to define metrics once and expose them consistently across every tool and consumer.
