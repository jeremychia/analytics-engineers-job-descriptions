Why this role exists:

The Analytics Engineer builds the trusted analytical foundations that enable high-quality reporting, analysis, data science, and decision support. Sitting between data engineering and analytics delivery, this role transforms, models, and structures raw or curated data into reliable, reusable assets ready for business use cases. This role focuses on creating well-governed, analytics-ready data layers that improve the speed, consistency, and trust in global insights.

Where you will make a difference:

In this role you will:

- Design and maintain analytics-ready datasets and reusable data models.

- Transform raw data into structured datasets to support complex reporting, analysis, and modelling.
