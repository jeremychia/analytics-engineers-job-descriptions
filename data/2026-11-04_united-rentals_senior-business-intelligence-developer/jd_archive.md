Senior Business Intelligence Developer
TEKsystems
Stamford, CT

Description

As an experienced Sr. Cloud BI Developer for development and creation of enterprise reporting. This role will be assisting business users and Enterprise Reporting & Analytics team in the successful creation and delivery of Sigma/Tableau dashboards and reports for internal and external clients.

What You'll Do

Collect and analyze information requirements from business users (or an IT proxy); translate those needs into use cases, requirements analysis and the general design of the Enterprise Reporting applications.

Develop Sigma/Tableau dashboards, visualizations and reports for complex problems. Use innovative insights and creativity in evaluating different approaches to meeting business requirements

Mentors the business and Analytics teams on Sigma/Tableau best practices, and act as a supporting resource for other developers.

Ensure standards and best practices are established and met for consistent and accurate use of Tableau/Sigma across the company. Ensure data security, integrity, and compliance with company standards and regulatory requirements.

Assist Enterprise Reporting team in the Tableau administration, including user provisioning, server management, license administration, and version upgrades.

Other duties assigned as needed

Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Certifications in Sigma/Tableau software

5 + years of Tableau development, web APIs and administration experience.

Minimum Tableau Certified Professional of good standing

1+ years of Sigma Computing development experience

5+ years of experience working with relational databases (Snowflake preferred)

Strong SQL coding of at least 5 years.

Strong data modeling skills of at least 2+ years.

Effective communicator both verbal and written.

Ability to diagnose and resolve problems independently with creative approaches.

Ability to communicate standards and best practices and train report developers on their use.

Ability to work with report developers, data modelers and business analysts to ensure the BI functional infrastructure meets the design and delivery needs of the business projects.

Ability to provide BI functional help desk support including 1st & 2nd level support, on-call troubleshooting & problem resolution.

Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States on a permanent basis. Sponsorship is not available for this position, now or in the future.

Onsite office attendance in either our Bridgeport, CT or Stamford, CT Office. 3 days in the office (Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday); Monday and Friday remote.
