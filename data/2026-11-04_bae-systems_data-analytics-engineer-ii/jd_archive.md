BAE Systems' Digital Architecture Team (DAT) is looking to hire a Data Analytics Engineer II to join our team. We are responsible for transitioning Engineering and Operations from legacy software and infrastructure to modern digital practices.

Our core product is data. Engineering and Operations produce a large volume and wide variety of data. In this role, you will help turn that data into trusted, analytics-ready information by developing data models and transformations, improving data quality, and partnering with customers to understand their needs and issues. Our system spans both modern cloud-based solutions and legacy Windows-based infrastructure.

Our team built a highly scalable containerized gRPC microservices platform and micro-frontends web applications. We need your help ensuring that data is collected, transformed, validated, documented, and made available for our end customers. You will collaborate with software engineers, analysts, and stakeholders to deliver reliable data products and continuously improve the way our customers use data.

Key Responsibilities

- Collaborate with engineers, analysts, and stakeholders to understand data needs and define clear analytics requirements.

- Build, maintain, and troubleshoot data pipelines and transformations that produce reliable, analytics-ready data.

- Develop and maintain data models, schemas, metric definitions, and documentation for reporting and analysis.

- Implement data validation and quality checks, investigate anomalies, and communicate issues and recommended actions.

- Integrate data from APIs, operational systems, data lakes, and data warehouses while maintaining appropriate data governance.

- Monitor pipeline performance and reliability, participate in code reviews, and contribute to continuous process improvement.
