Your role

As Analytics Engineering Lead, you will turn Clarios’ global data and analytics strategy into scalable, secure, and high-quality solutions.

You will lead the technical delivery of analytics products, metrics, integrations, and predictive capabilities. Working with business stakeholders, data scientists, architects, infrastructure teams, and global delivery partners, you will ensure solutions meet our architecture, governance, security, and quality standards.

Key responsibilities

- Lead the design and delivery of analytics solutions in line with Clarios standards.

- Own the technical roadmap, delivery backlog, resource planning, and effort estimates.

- Ensure consistent governance and deployment of global performance metrics.

- Oversee analytics integrations, proofs of concept, and technical delivery across platforms.

- Ensure solutions meet quality, security, performance, IT controls, and support requirements.

- Partner with data scientists to prepare data for predictive modelling.

- Advise business stakeholders on opportunities to use predictive analytics.

- Communicate progress, risks, issues, and dependencies to relevant stakeholders.

What we look for

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, Business, or a related field.

- At least five years of experience developing Business Intelligence solutions.

- Strong knowledge of data warehousing, data modelling, databases, and analytics architectures.

- Experience with Power BI, SQL, cloud data platforms such as Azure or Snowflake, and data integration technologies.

- Experience troubleshooting, optimizing performance, and resolving technical issues.

- At least three years of experience in Agile delivery with onshore and offshore teams.

- Strong problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder-management skills.

- Ability to translate business requirements into scalable and secure analytics solutions.

- Confidence working across global and cross-functional teams.

- Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

About Clarios

Clarios is the global leader in advanced, low-voltage battery technologies for mobility. Our batteries and smart solutions power nearly every type of vehicle and are found in 1 of 3 cars on the road today. With around 18,000 employees in over 100 countries, we bring deep expertise to our Aftermarket and OEM partners, and reliability, safety and comfort to everyday lives. We answer to the planet with a rigorous sustainability focus – advancing best-in-class sustainability practices and advocating for them across our industry. We work to ensure 100% of our products sold are recyclable, and we recycle 8,000 batteries an hour in our network. You can find more information here (PDF).
