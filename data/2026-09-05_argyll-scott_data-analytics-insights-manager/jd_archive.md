Data Analytics & Insights Manager (Modern BI & Data Engineering)

The Opportunity

Our Top Financial Services Client with an established regional footprint, is looking for a Data Analytics & Insights Manager to bring expertise in Business Intelligence (BI), performance reporting, and data engineering solutions. This position sits within a high-exposure analytics function, transforming complex, cross-channel data sets into executive-ready narratives that directly drive commercial strategy and operational efficiency.

Key Responsibilities

1. Management Information (MI), Reporting & Performance Insights

Deliver end-to-end MI and performance reporting across sales tracking, service productivity, funnel/conversion, customer activity, and channel performance.

Translate business questions into structured analytical approaches, uncovering performance drivers and offering actionable recommendations.

Construct executive-ready narratives and insight packs that clearly highlight trends, root causes, operational risks, and market opportunities.

2. Modern Dashboard Development & Visualization

Build and maintain interactive, high-UX dashboards tailored to diverse stakeholder groups, including senior leadership, product teams, and operations.

Apply modern visualization practices, ensuring a clear KPI hierarchy, intuitive drill-through design, and robust self-service filtering capabilities.

Optimize dashboard performance through advanced data model design, efficient calculation layers, and incremental refresh strategies.

3. Data Engineering & Database Programming

Design and maintain robust datasets and localized data marts, including reusable curated layers for scorecards and business dashboards.

Write efficient, production-grade SQL (utilizing CTEs, window functions, and query optimization techniques) while embedding automated data quality checks.

Implement modern data transformation approaches, utilizing modular ELT patterns, version-controlled transformations, and data validation routines.

Support the integration of disparate data sources (including CRM, product, and channel telemetry) while ensuring reliable refresh schedules.

4. Advanced Analytics & Automation

Deploy Python and SQL to automate manual reporting workflows, conduct statistical analysis, perform customer segmentation, and execute forecasting.

Develop repeatable analytical assets, such as parameterized reports, reusable code libraries, and standard enterprise KPI definitions.

Advocate for technical best practices across the team, including detailed documentation, structured peer reviews, and reproducible code.

5. Stakeholder Management & Delivery

Partner with cross-functional business, product, and technology groups to manage demand pipelines, gather requirements, and deliver against project timelines.

Communicate sophisticated technical findings in clear, non-technical business language to influence executive decision-making.

Ensure all data assets strictly comply with internal governance expectations, including data privacy laws, access controls, and auditability standards.

Requirements & Technical Skillset

Education: University degree in Quantitative Analysis, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related quantitative discipline.

Experience: Minimum of 5 years of experience across data analysis, database management, software/system engineering, and modern reporting development.

Domain Background: Prior experience within commercial, corporate, or digital banking is highly preferred, coupled with an understanding of sales/service performance frameworks.

Core Technical Stack: Strong hands-on capability in SQL and Python (Excel VBA capability is also recognized).

Data Architecture: Solid understanding of data modeling concepts (star schemas, facts/dimensions, KPI calculation logic, and data quality controls).

BI Tooling: Experience building production-grade enterprise reporting assets. Hands-on experience with Qlik Sense, QlikView, or Tableau is considered a distinct advantage.

Project Delivery: Strong project management skills, including structured planning, backlog prioritization, and risk/issue mitigation.

Soft Skills: Exceptional analytical thinking, structural communication capabilities, and a strong ownership mindset.

Language: Full professional proficiency in both English and Chinese.
