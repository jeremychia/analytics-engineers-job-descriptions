BI & Analytics Developer
Join a team with strong BI roots and big ambitions for Microsoft Fabric, modern analytics and trusted business insights.

At itm8, we are investing heavily in data, analytics and AI. We are looking for a BI & Analytics Developer who can turn our Microsoft Fabric foundation into trusted insights, modern analytics experiences and real business value.
Our common data platform is taking shape. Now we need someone who can help shape how data is consumed, explored and visualised across itm8 - from semantic models and governed self-service to innovative analytics and decision-support tools.

Your Role

As our BI & Analytics Developer, you will help itm8 move from traditional reporting towards a modern analytics landscape built on Microsoft Fabric, trusted data products and user-friendly insight experiences.

Working with business stakeholders, platform colleagues and fellow BI developers, you will translate business needs into reusable data products, intuitive visualisations, semantic models and analytics solutions.

In practice, you will:

- Design modern analytics, visualisation and decision-support solutions using Microsoft Fabric and relevant BI/analytics tools.

- Build semantic models and investigate data lineage and quality issues so insights can be trusted.

- Challenge traditional reporting approaches and find smarter ways to enable exploration, adoption and better decisions.

We are looking for a teamm8 who

- Does not believe another classic dashboard is always the answer.

- Enjoys investigating data, understanding where numbers come from and solving data quality issues.

- Is curious, pragmatic and motivated by emerging analytics and visualisation technologies that people actually use.

What you bring
Mindset matters most, but ideally you bring:

- Hands-on experience with Microsoft Fabric, for example Lakehouse, Warehouse, Data Factory, OneLake, semantic models or related capabilities.

- Experience building and maintaining data pipelines and reusable data products.

- Experience with Power BI or comparable BI/visualisation tools, including modelling, report design and interactive insight experiences.

- Comfortable with SQL and DAX; Python knowledge is a plus.

- Experience with Git integration, CI/CD, version control and deployment pipelines.

It would be an advantage if you also have experience with

- AI-assisted analytics, Copilot or similar tools that support insight generation.

- Data storytelling, information design and modern visualisation practices.

What you get
You will join a team evolving from traditional BI delivery towards Microsoft Fabric, modern analytics, data enablement, adoption and business impact.
You will influence how analytics, visualisation and decision support develop across itm8 and help create trusted data products for our continued AI journey.

About the team

You will join the Data & Insights Team, which is part of itm8’s Global Transformation Management Office (TMO).

We are an international team with big ambitions. Our goal is to build strong data products and a team where people grow, learn and enjoy coming to work. We invest in professional development, encourage curiosity and experimentation, and believe that trust, collaboration and a good sense of humour are just as important as technical excellence.
