Analytics Engineer
Capgemini
Vila Nova de Gaia, Porto, Portugal

YOUR ROLE

- Design, build and maintain curated data products using SQL and dbt.

- Develop scalable data models applying dimensional modelling best practices.

- Define and maintain certified business metrics and semantic-layer logic.

- Ensure data quality through automated testing, monitoring and data contracts.

- Support the migration of finance reporting from SAP BW to the lakehouse.

- Collaborate with business stakeholders and cross-functional data teams to deliver trusted analytics solutions.

YOUR PROFILE

- 2-3 years of experience in Analytics Engineering, Data Modelling or Data Products.

- Strong experience with SQL and dbt (or equivalent transformation frameworks).

- Knowledge of dimensional modelling, star schemas, slowly changing dimensions and semantic layers.

- Experience translating business processes and KPIs into reusable and governed data logic.

- Understanding of data quality, documentation, lineage and governance practices.

- Experience with Git, testing frameworks and CI/CD for analytics development.

- Experience with SAP BW structures and finance reporting logic (nice to have, not mandatory).

- Experience with finance or commercial data (nice to have, not mandatory).

- Knowledge of Databricks SQL and Unity Catalog, including metric views and governed data sharing (nice to have, not mandatory).

- Ability to work closely with business stakeholders and reconcile reporting requirements.
