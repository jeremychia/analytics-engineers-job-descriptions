Business Intelligence Engineer

Are you passionate about transforming data into clarity, building powerful BI solutions, and enabling teams to make smarter decisions?

Do dashboards, data models, and seamless pipelines spark your creativity?

If yes, then you might be the Business Intelligence Engineer we’re looking for.

At Boutiqaat, data is at the heart of every decision, and we need a builder, innovator, and problem-solver who can elevate how we collect, structure, and visualize information. You’ll develop and maintain BI interfaces, create intuitive dashboards, support key business strategies, and ensure our data flows are efficient, accurate, and actionable.

What You’ll Be Doing:

In this dynamic BI engineering role, you will:

- Develop, deploy, and maintain BI solutions including interactive dashboards, query tools, data visualizations, and data models.

- Use analytics from the Boutiqaat website and sales performance to build reports, comparisons, and visual insights.

- Analyze organizational data across finance, operations, HR, sales, and more to support strategic decision-making.

- Identify system inefficiencies and recommend data-driven solutions to improve performance.

- Work closely with teams to understand resource needs and solve operational challenges.

- Collaborate with stakeholders to translate requirements into BRDs, PRDs, data structures, and algorithmic workflows.

- Conduct interactive presentations and prepare analytical reports using Power BI or similar BI tools.

- Generate forecasts and provide actionable insights across business verticals.

- Set up, manage, and optimize data pipelines and data warehouse structures using SQL, Python, Airflow, and other ETL/ELT tools.

- Develop small applications for capturing manual data inputs and build APIs or reverse-ETL integrations to push insights into business tools (e.g., campaign planners, personalization engines).

- Support internal and external stakeholders with data extraction, modeling, and analysis.

- Present findings to senior management when required.

- Deliver data projects independently and collaboratively with cross-functional teams.

What You Bring to the Table:

To excel in this role, you should bring:

- Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or a related field.

- Proven professional experience as a BI Developer, BI Engineer, or Data Scientist.

- Experience designing, implementing, and maintaining data warehouses.

- Strong understanding of database management systems and architecture.

- Familiarity with BI technologies such as Power BI, Oracle BI, and hands-on experience with:

- SQL (advanced)

- SSRS

- SSIS

- Experience working with Airflow or other orchestration tools for managing data pipelines.
