About The Team

R2L is a consolidated organization within Amazon that was established in 2023 and is comprised of multiple high growth operational businesses, including Sub Same Day - Fulfillment Centers (SSD-FC), Sub Same Day - Distribution Centers (SSD-DC), and corporate and technical support branches. R2L owns the end-to-end customer fulfillment aspects of these businesses, including supply chain planning, distribution, central programs, delivery, technology, and central operations. The goal of R2L is to improve speed and customer experience, making it one of the most prominent growth engines for Amazon, with SSD-FC and RSR poised to grow by over 2X in the next 3 years. R2L currently operates in North America (US).

R2L Analytics team is responsible for providing Data Infrastructure and supporting Reporting & Analytics for R2L organization.
