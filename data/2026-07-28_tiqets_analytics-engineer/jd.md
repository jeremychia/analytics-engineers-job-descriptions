Working at Tiqets
The Analytics Engineering Team is the critical link between Data and Business teams. You'll work closely with our Data Engineers and Data Analysts, and help guide the company's approach to data governance as it matures.
As an Analytics Engineer, you will be the guardian of our data ecosystem, ensuring that our data is not only available but is high-quality, performant, and strictly governed.
What you will do
You'll be a strategic partner to Product Managers, Engineers, and the Business Design, build, and maintain dbt models powering our behavioral analytics and event-tracking pipeline, one of the largest and most actively used parts of our data platform, processing high-volume clickstream data daily

Data Curation & Modeling, own and improve analytical data modeling practices across projects

Help evolve our data governance framework by improving our Governance projects, ranging from data compliance to access control

Monitor and optimize the performance of our dbt pipelines and Looker infrastructure, proactively identifying automation and cost-efficiency opportunities

Collaborate with Data Engineers, Data Analysts, and stakeholders across teams to keep our modeling standards consistent and well understood

Review code within our analytics projects to help the team maintain best practices as we grow

Contribute to improving data infrastructure, from pipeline health to Looker usage

Get to the bottom of data issues, determine root causes and build solutions that resolve them permanently, not just patch symptoms

AI adoption, contribute to initiatives for unlocking AI in our analytical and reporting processes (DWH/BI Tooling)
If you are passionate about Data, enjoy working on challenging business problems in a fast-moving environment, and believe in the importance of teamwork, then this could be the right opening for you.
Responsibilities

Design and implement data models with SQL in DBT

Build explores and dashboards in Looker to allow others at the company to ask the right data questions

Deep dive into large business datasets to uncover valuable insights that can be turned into actionable hypotheses

Work with Product Managers, Developers, Marketeers, and Commercial Specialists to determine and measure the right metrics in their respective domains

Keep a close eye on the ever-changing data challenges at Tiqets, and collaborate closely with Strategic Analysts, Data Engineers, Data Scientists, and other Data Analysts on the team to solve them

Educate and empower others at the company to be more self-reliant for their data needs

Mentor others in the team with technical guidance

Collaborate on the architectural evolution of the Tiqets data model and reporting framework
What you will bring

3+ years of experience working as a Data or Analytics Engineer on data-intensive applications

Strong SQL skills and hands-on experience with dbt for data modeling and transformation

Proven track record of working with a modern cloud data warehouse like Databricks, Redshift, Snowflake, or BigQuery

Experience with Apache Airflow or a comparable orchestration tool

Python proficiency, with a track record of writing clean, maintainable code

Experience with Looker or a comparable BI/semantic-layer tool

Experience with cross-functional/cross-team collaboration, with excellent oral and written communication skills

Capacity to thrive in a scale-up environment, pragmatic attitude, ability to work independently, collaborate effectively with others, and take ownership of your work

Living in the Netherlands/EU
Nice to Have

Experience with data access control and masking patterns in dbt

Familiarity with data governance, security, and regulatory compliance considerations (e.g., GDPR, SOX, PCI DSS) as they apply to data platforms and data warehouses.

Curiosity about data observability and lineage tooling
