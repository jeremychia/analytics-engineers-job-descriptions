Senior Analytics Engineer (Data Platform)
Cake Digital Bank
Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

About The Role

As a Senior Analytics Engineer, you will bridge data engineering, analytics, and business, building scalable data models and pipelines to enable reliable, high-quality data for decision-making.

What You Will Do

Data Ownership & Collaboration

- Collect business requirements and translate complex needs into technical solutions

- Collaborate with Data Engineers and stakeholders to deliver end-to-end data solutions

- Consult with data consumers to define meaningful datasets for analytical use

Data Modeling & Pipelines

- Design, implement, and optimize data models (DWH/Data Mart, Star Schema, Dimensional)

- Build and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines with focus on scalability and performance

- Refactor data warehouse structures to improve consistency and efficiency

Data Quality & Standards

- Ensure data quality through testing, validation, and monitoring

- Create and maintain data documentation

- Improve coding standards, maintainability, and best practices across the data platform

What We’re Looking For

- Bachelor’s degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, Computer Science, Economics, or related fields.

- 3+ years of experience working as a analyst engineer, data engineer or similar role, ideally in a fast-moving digital or fintech environment

- Strong experience in data modeling and data architecture

- Deep understanding of SQL with ability to write and optimize complex queries

- Hands-on experience with data pipelines and data warehouse concepts

- Familiarity with BigQuery, dbt is a plus

- Strong analytical and critical thinking skills

- Ability to translate business requirements into scalable data solutions

Nice to Have

- Knowledge of Python or other programming languages

- Experience with BI tools (Power BI, Tableau, Looker)

- Familiarity with modern data stack and distributed systems
