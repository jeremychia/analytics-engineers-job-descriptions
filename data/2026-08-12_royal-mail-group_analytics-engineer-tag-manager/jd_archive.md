Analytics Engineer - Tag Manager

Royal Mail is continuing its transformation into a data-driven, product-led organisation and is looking for an experienced Analytics Engineer - Tag Manager to build and maintain the analytics data foundations that support digital products, customer journeys, experimentation and AI-enabled insights.

This is a highly technical, hands-on role working closely with Product, Analytics and Engineering teams to ensure high-quality, reliable data is available for analysis, optimisation and decision-making.

Key Responsibilities

- Own the implementation and optimisation of Tag Management solutions across web and app platforms.
- Develop and govern data layer specifications, SDRs and tracking documentation.
- Integrate and model data from multiple sources to create robust analytics datasets.
- Support customer journey analysis, experimentation and conversion optimisation initiatives.
- Ensure analytics data quality through testing, validation and monitoring.
- Leverage AI-enabled tools and techniques to improve analytics engineering processes.
- Partner with technical and business stakeholders, translating requirements into effective data solutions.

Key Skills & Experience

- Strong experience in Analytics Engineering, Data Engineering or Digital Analytics.
- Hands-on expertise with Tealium IQ, Google Tag Manager (GTM), Adobe Launch or similar tag management platforms.
- Strong understanding of digital analytics implementations, tagging and data layer design.
- Technical coding skills in: JavaScript, HTML, CSS
- Exposure to React and/or React Native environments.
- Experience integrating data from multiple sources and working within modern analytics ecosystems.
- Ability to translate business requirements into technical specifications and explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
- Strong attention to detail with a focus on building scalable, reliable solutions.

This is an excellent opportunity for a technically strong analytics professional who enjoys combining coding, data engineering and business understanding to drive better digital insights
