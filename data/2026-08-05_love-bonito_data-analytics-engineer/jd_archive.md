About The Role

Reporting to the Senior Data Manager, this role is based in ID and owns the analytical backbone of how Love, Bonito makes decisions - from self-serve reporting to forecasting models that tell us what to expect and where to focus next.

Main Responsibilities

 Dashboard &amp; Reporting

Design, optimize, and maintain performance dashboards that enable stakeholders to explore data independently

Define and own the metrics layer (clear, documented definitions) so numbers don't silently diverge across teams

Set and enforce dashboarding best practices (design standards, naming conventions, template library) across the analytics function 

Document SOPs for dashboard build, refresh, and handover so ownership can transfer without loss of context

 Forecasting &amp; Modeling

Build and ship production-grade forecasting models for run-rate and sales-event performance, tracked against actuals

Use forecasting output to inform campaign mechanics

 Customer Analytics

Build and maintain customer segmentation and market basket analyses to uncover cross-sell opportunities

Deliver reusable, scalable analytical frameworks, not one-off answers to one-off questions

 Governance

Own model documentation: assumptions, limitations and decision boundaries for any model that influences spend decisions

 Technical Optimization

Evaluate and optimise aggregation tables for performance and scalability

Automate recurring workflows to reduce manual effort and reduce time-to-insight

 Data Modeling &amp; Pipeline Architecture

Design table structures (fact/dimension modeling, partition strategy) for scalability and query performance - architecting new tables, not just tuning existing one

Own orchestration and scheduling of recurring pipelines (e.g. Airflow, dbt, Databricks Workflows) including failure monitoring and data-freshness SLAs

 Documentation &amp; Knowledge Management

Document data models and business logic so solutions are maintainable by other team members and usable as context by internal AI tooling

Who We Are Looking For:

We're hiring for someone who has already shipped production work

Statistical first-principles grounding: understands identification assumptions, confounding, and bias-variance tradeoffs well enough to choose a method because it fits the problem, not because it's the default reach

Has shipped at least one model or evaluation pipeline to production and can speak to what broke, how it was monitored, and how false positives were ruled out

Requirements

Qualifications 

Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field (Statistics, Data Science, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, Engineering) or related discipline; Master's a plus

1-3 years of relevant experience with demonstrated production ownership of at least one pipeline, model or dashboard suite

Strong proficiency in Python, SQL, statistics

Deep proficiency in Tableau (or equivalent BI tool) — able to set and enforce dashboarding best practices, not just build to spec

Pipeline orchestration/scheduling experience (Airflow, dbt or equivalent)

Data modeling experience (dimensional modeling/star schema or equivalent)

Self-starter who proactively finds and closes optimisation gaps

Comfortable using modern analytics/AI tooling to move faster

Collaborative team player who takes ownership and drives projects to completion

Nice to Have:
