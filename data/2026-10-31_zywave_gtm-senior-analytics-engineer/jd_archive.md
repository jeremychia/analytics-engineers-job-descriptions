Senior Analytics Engineer, GTM

Role Overview

Zywave is seeking a Senior Analytics Engineer, GTM to own and improve the revenue data foundation across Salesforce, Snowflake, and other GTM systems. This role focuses on data cleanliness, source-of-truth alignment, hierarchy integrity, metric logic, reconciliation, and reportable data models.

This senior individual contributor will work deeply in Snowflake and SQL, understand Salesforce data structures, consolidate data from multiple systems, and create trusted datasets for reporting and analytics. The role is distinct from a reporting analyst: it owns the underlying data layer that makes reporting accurate, scalable, and trusted.

Reporting Relationship

This role will report to the VP, Revenue Operations

•

Key partners include Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, Client Success Operations, Finance, Marketing Operations, Business Applications, Data / BI, Sales and CS leadership

Build and maintain trusted GTM data models in Snowflake that support reporting across Sales, CS, Finance, Marketing Operations, and Revenue Operations.

•
