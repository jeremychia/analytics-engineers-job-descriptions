- Implement data quality checks and monitoring processes to ensure data accuracy, consistency, and completeness.

- Promote and support the adoption of software engineering best practices in analytics, including version control, code reviews, and automated testing to ensure code quality and readability.

- Excellent written and spoken English communication skills;
