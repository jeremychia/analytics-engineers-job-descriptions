About The Role

As a Senior Data & Analytics Consultant/Engineer, you will lead the technical delivery of large-scale data platform and BI solutions. You will be asked to design, implement and own the delivery of robust Data Platform solutions and end-to-end components. The role will work closely with principal consultants, practice leads, project managers, and client stakeholders and teams. This is a client facing role, so you'll need to be an excellent communicator and good with people.

What You’ll Do

- Working with clients (businesses users) to gather, clarify requirements and business rules by running workshops with stakeholders.

- Provide overall thought leadership to our clients regarding data profiling and integration recommendations.

- Design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines and data platform solutions.

- Develop and optimize data models, transformations, and database objects within Snowflake.

- Troubleshoot data pipeline issues and ensure the timely delivery of critical business data.

- Build, support, and enhance workflow orchestration processes using Apache Airflow.

- Contribute to CI/CD processes and DevOps practices to improve deployment reliability and efficiency.

- Ability to perform performance tuning and optimisation on new and/or existing data warehouse solutions.

- Provide detailed documentation, end user training and knowledge-transfer services to customers, and internal teams.

- Managing development life cycle using various source code management tools.

Required Skills And Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related discipline

- Excellent verbal and written communication skills to work collaboratively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- Proven track-record of designing and building modern data platforms in a Data Engineering or Analytics Engineering capacity.

- Hands-on experience designing and maintaining Snowflake database objects, including tables, views, schemas, stages, and stored procedures.

- 5+ years of experience developing data engineering solutions using Python and SQL, including automation, data processing, and integration with platforms such as Snowflake and Apache Airflow.

- 5+ years of experience using Apache Airflow to build, schedule, monitor, and support data pipelines, including managing workflow dependencies and troubleshooting operational issues.

- 5+ years of experience designing and implementing enterprise data warehouse solutions, including dimensional modelling (star and snowflake schemas) and Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD).

- Experience working with CI/CD practices and deployment pipelines.

- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud Platform.

- Experience with dbt (Data Build Tool) is a plus.

Nice to have

- Advanced skills in Azure, Fabric, and Snowflake pipelines, with experience in tools such as Azure Data Factory, Coalesce, Matillion, dbt, Databricks, Fivetran, and Airflow (must have)

- SnowPro, Coalesce, Microsoft Azure, or Microsoft Fabric certifications are highly regarded.

- Strong skills in data platform implementation, configuration, performance and cost management tuning (i.e. Azure, Fabric, Snowflake)

- Hands-on experience with Power BI and/or other business intelligence and data visualization tools.
