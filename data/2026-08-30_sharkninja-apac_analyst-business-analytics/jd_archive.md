Job Responsibilities

1. Advanced Analytics & Dashboard Development

- Build and optimize complex DAX measures and SQL queries to provide deep-dive visibility into regional KPIs.

- Design and update interactive dashboards that track performance metrics such as sell-out trends, demand planning accuracy, and trade spend ROI.

- Translate raw datasets into visual stories that highlight risks and opportunities for the APAC leadership team.

2. Regional Performance Reporting

- Act as the point of analytical contact for regional leads across APAC, providing them with localized data insights.

- Conduct "Deep Dive Analysis" to identify why specific regions or product categories are over- or under-performing against prior year/targets.

- Support the monthly business review process with the senior leadership team by preparing data-driven performance decks.

3. Strategic Project Support

- Assist in the development of predictive forecasting models to help the APAC region move toward more proactive planning.

- Collaborate on cross-functional projects that bridge the gap between Sales, Supply Chain, and Finance.

- Execute high-priority special projects and ad-hoc analyses to address emerging business challenges, ensuring the SS&A team remains agile and responsive to shifting regional priorities and leadership requests.

4. Data Automation & Pipeline Management

- Own regular ingestion, cleaning, and transformation of fragmented sales data from various APAC regions.

- Standardize diverse reporting formats into a unified data structure to eliminate manual data entry.

- Monitor and troubleshoot existing data pipelines to ensure weekly/monthly reporting remains uninterrupted.

5. Data Integrity & Checks

- Support the standardization in reporting across regional and market teams.

- Perform rigorous Data Quality Assurance checks to ensure that the sales and analytics data align with what local market counterparts present with no data discrepancies.

6. Support Reporting, Analysis, Ad-Hoc Projects and Requests

- BAU Reporting to leadership and supporting decks for board meetings

- Supporting Ad-Hoc projects to drive business goals

- Providing support and collaborating with functions like marketing, demand & supply etc.

Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in business Analytics, Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Finance, Economics, or a related discipline.

- 4–8 years of experience in business intelligence, commercial and sales analytics preferably in a regional APAC, multinational or sales & finance environment.

- Advanced proficiency in Power BI, including development of complex dashboards and data models.

- Demonstrated experience in dashboard development, KPI reporting, variance analysis, and executive-level business performance reporting.

- Understanding data modeling, fact and dimension tables and business concepts such as gross margins, COGS, gross to net sales expenses preferred.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and performance drivers across markets and product categories.

- Familiarity with sales forecasting/modelling, predictive analytics, demand planning, trade spend analysis, or commercial performance analytics is highly preferred.

- Proven ability to work cross-functionally and with regional business teams in a fast-paced environment.

- Strong communication and data storytelling skills, with the ability to translate complex analytical findings into clear actionable business recommendations for leadership audiences.

- Highly detail-oriented, proactive, and capable of managing multiple priorities and ad-hoc analytical requests under tight timelines.

- Experience within consumer goods, retail or electronics.
