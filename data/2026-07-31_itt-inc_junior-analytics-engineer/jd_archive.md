About ITT:

ITT Inc. (NYSE: ITT) is a diversified global leading manufacturer of highly engineered critical components and customized technology solutions serving the flow, aerospace and defense, transportation, industrial, and energy markets. Through operational execution, innovation, and disciplined capital deployment, including strategic acquisitions such as SPX FLOW, ITT partners with its customers to deliver enduring solutions to the key industries that underpin our modern way of life.

Headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, ITT has a market cap of roughly $20 billion and employs more than 15,000 people across 40 countries and serves customers in approximately 125 countries. Following the acquisition of SPX FLOW, the company is expected to generate $5.4 billion in annual revenue.

Our businesses are organized in three distinct segments, each based around our core engineering DNA:

Flow Technologies: A global leader in, providing a broad portfolio of pumps, valves, mixing and agitation equipment, heat transfer, homogenizers, separation, water and wastewater treatment, and engineered process solutions. Position Summary

Are you an Analytics Engineer with a passion for data?

Do you enjoy working across the entire data stack to design end-to-end solutions that turn data into actionable insights?

Do you want to be part of a global industry leader?

At ITT, we are looking for an Analytics Engineer to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing analytics dashboards, developing and maintaining data pipelines and building data models that help the business make data-driven decisions. This position offers the unique opportunity to work with cloud data technologies, enabling you to have a comprehensive impact on the organization's data strategy.

Essential Responsibilities

To succeed in this role, you will need to:

Be curious and eager to work across the entire data stack, from data ingestion to data visualization

Be open to learn and explore data analytics techniques and tools

Have a strong problem-solving mindset and a passion for data-driven insights

Thrive in a fast-paced environment with evolving priorities

Possess excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate across teams

More specifically you will:

Design and implement data models to support business analytics and reporting

Work closely with data engineers and business stakeholders to understand data requirements and deliver scalable data solutions

Build interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI to provide actionable insights

Be part of the development and maintenance of cloud data pipelines that ingest, clean, transform, and store data from various internal and external sources

Ensure data quality and governance practices are followed throughout the data lifecycle

Work with SQL, Python, and Azure Data technologies

Collaborate with business units to design self-service analytics solutions, enabling users to create their own reports and analyses

Stay up to date with the latest trends in data analytics, continuously improving tools, processes, and best practices on Azure

Position Requirements

2+ years of experience in data analytics, or a similar role

Experience in data modeling and database design for reporting and analytics use cases

Experience in data visualization tools, more specifically Power BI with a focus on designing user-friendly dashboards

Solid knowledge of SQL and Python for data processing, data analysis and transformation tasks

Hands-on experience with cloud data platforms, preferably Azure

Experience working in agile development environments and be a collaborative team player

A team-oriented mindset and a passion for data-driven innovation

Preferred Qualifications:

Experience with version control tools (git), working in dev and production environments

Familiarity with data governance practices to ensure data accuracy, consistency, and security

Exposure to data science concepts for analytics will be considered a plus

Education:
