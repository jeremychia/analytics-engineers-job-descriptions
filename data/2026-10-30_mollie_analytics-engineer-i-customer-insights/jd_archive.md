Your impact

Mollie's Revenue Operations domain gives our commercial organisation the tooling, data and automation to do more with less. As Analytics Engineer I in the Customer Insights team, you'll focus on the analytics and BI side of the products we ship.

You'll build the dbt models behind your own dashboards and turn that data into a clear, ongoing read on adoption, usage and impact. You'll be the person who can tell the team and its stakeholders, with data, whether what we shipped is actually working.

Your numbers will directly shape which BETs get iterated on, doubled down on or cut. You'll work side by side with Application Engineers on tools that Sales and SDRs use every day, at a fast moving moment with the GoCardless integration about to start.

What You'll Ship

- Small, regular improvements to existing Customer Insights products, like dbt model fixes, new tests and dashboard fixes, shipped independently.

- Adoption, usage and impact tracking for Customer Insights products, such as Account Book adoption, BigMind usage or HubSpot UI card engagement.

- That tracking built end to end, from the underlying dbt model through to a dashboard that stakeholders actually check.

- Build a standing set of adoption and impact dashboards that the team and its stakeholders use to prioritise what we shipp, and it's analytics and BI deliverables.

What You'll Bring

- 1 to 3 years of experience in SQL and working dbt experience. You're comfortable building and maintaining models and tests, not just querying, because you'll own the models feeding your own dashboards.

- Experience with a BI or dashboard tool such as Omni, Looker, Tableau or similar. You know how to display data so it delivers a clear message.

- Data visualisation and data storytelling skills.

- Experience with GTM, Reveneu Operations or Marketing is a big plus.

- Comfort working directly with business stakeholders.
