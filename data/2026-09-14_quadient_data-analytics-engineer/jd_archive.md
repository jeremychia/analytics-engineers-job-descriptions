Job Description

We’re looking for a Marketing Analytics Engineer who enjoys turning complex data into something people can actually trust and use.
Sitting at the intersection of data engineering, analytics and marketing, you’ll build the models and pipelines that help our Marketing teams understand performance, customer journeys and the real impact of their investments. From multi-touch attribution and funnel analytics to scalable DBT models, you’ll help create a reliable data foundation for smarter marketing decisions.
- Design and build scalable marketing data models, covering multi-touch attribution, digital and offline touchpoints, account-based marketing, funnel analytics and marketing impact.
- Develop and manage DBT projects, from staging and intermediate models through to trusted, business-ready data marts.
- Build and optimize data pipelines and models with a focus on performance, scalability and cost efficiency.
- Partner with Marketing and Operations teams to translate business questions into meaningful metrics, KPIs and reporting solutions.
- Help create a consistent and reliable view of marketing performance across complex B2B customer journeys.
- Maintain strong standards around data quality, testing, documentation, lineage and governance.
- Collaborate with IT and Martech teams to ensure our analytics ecosystem follows security, compliance and engineering best practices.

Qualifications

- Around 3–5 years of experience in Analytics Engineering, Data Analytics Engineering or a similar role.
- Strong hands-on experience with DBT, including data modelling, testing, documentation, lineage and Git-based development workflows.
- Experience with modern cloud data warehouses, ideally Google BigQuery, and strong SQL skills.
- Good understanding of analytical data modelling concepts such as facts, dimensions, star/snowflake schemas and metrics layers.
- Experience working with marketing data, including tracking, advertising platforms, attribution and campaign performance.
- Experience designing and optimizing Multi-Touch Attribution (MTA) models and connecting marketing investment with revenue, ROI and ROAS.
- Understanding of complex B2B marketing journeys, including different sales motions and sales-cycle lengths.
- Comfortable partnering with both technical and non-technical stakeholders and translating business needs into robust data solutions.
Knowledge gaps can be filled. Even if you don’t satisfy every single requirement or meet every qualification listed, we still want to hear from you.
