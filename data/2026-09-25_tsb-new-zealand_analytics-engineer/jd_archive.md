About the role
You’ll be joining an inclusive Data Analytics team, which is part of the wider Operational Excellence team, to deliver business insights to help drive the business forward.

The Analytics Engineer will be the bridge between business data consumers and technical systems, working closely across the business and technology teams. You will be responsible for the development and implementation of data models.

Who are you?
We’re looking for a talented individual with relevant software development experiences, great attention to detail and who will contribute to the design and implementation, of complex data solutions that provide additional data for analytical / reporting purposes.

You will be technically proficient with advanced SQL skills and experience in database technologies. Experience in DevOps will be beneficial.

In this role, you can expect to:
- Contribute to the design and implementation, of complex data solutions.
- Support data modelling activities (conceptual, logical and physical).
- Provide expert advice and education in the usage and interpretation of data.
- You will work with other engineering teams to continually acquire new data.

We are looking for candidates who have:
- Demonstrable experience in data and analytics engineering in complex organisations
- Experience in cloud platforms, specifically Databricks.
- Experience with Medallion Architecture and building gold and silver layers.
