Are you our new Senior Analytics Engineer?

At Tracksuit, data isn't a back-office function — it's the backbone of every decision we make. In this role, you'll build and evolve the internal analytics platform that powers our Product, Finance, and GTM teams (Customer Success, Sales, and Marketing) across our growing global organisation.

You'll work closely with Data Engineers, Data Analysts, Software Engineers, Product Managers, and Operations colleagues across our international offices, shaping the quality and strategic direction of our internal data capabilities at scale.

Please note: This role can be based in either our Auckland or Sydney office.

As our Senior Analytics Engineer, you'll specifically be responsible for:

- Partnering with senior stakeholders across Finance, GTM and Product: understanding the decision they're trying to make, then building the thing that moves it
- Making the value obvious: explaining in plain language what a model changed for the business, and shipping one number people trust over five nobody opens
- Owning data quality, reliability and accuracy across our analytics platform, and setting the standard for how Tracksuit works with data
- Running and scaling our core platform (Snowflake + dbt) and the integrations feeding it: HubSpot, Subskribe and our product database
- Building self-service data products so the business can answer its own questions instead of queuing up ad hoc requests
- Doing whatever the problem needs: we're a scale up, so some weeks are deep modelling and others are unblocking someone in CS, fixing a pipeline, or mentoring someone junior

That's the role - who are you?

- You make senior people smarter, quickly: comfortable in front of execs and non-technical stakeholders, you'll push back kindly when the ask isn't the real need, and you land the "so what" without the jargon
- You measure yourself on impact, not output: you can name the decisions your work changed
- Low ego: you share the messy draft early, give credit generously, and care more about the right answer than being the one who had it
- You love simplicity: your instinct is to delete rather than add, and you'll take the boring, well-tested model over the clever one
- You thrive in the mess of a scale up: ambiguity and shifting priorities don't rattle you, and you pick up the unglamorous work without being asked
- 7+ years in data, with advanced SQL and dbt on Snowflake, strong modelling judgement, and the ability to own ELT pipelines and infrastructure end to end
- AI tools in your daily workflow (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot), with the judgement to verify what they produce

Nice-to-have

- Experience modelling Finance or GTM data (revenue, retention, pipeline)
- Prior experience in a B2B SaaS start up or scale up
- Experience building or contributing to a semantic layer or metrics layer

Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree (or higher) in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Technology, or a closely related analytical field — or equivalent practical experience
