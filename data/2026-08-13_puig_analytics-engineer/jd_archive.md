The Opportunity:

Puig USA is seeking a technically strong Analytics Engineer to support the next phase of its data and analytics transformation. This role will build and maintain analytical datasets, semantic models, Power BI solutions, automated workflows, and business-facing data products that enable better decision-making across the organization.

This position is ideal for someone who combines strong technical expertise with a practical, solutions-oriented mindset and is comfortable delivering value in a fast-paced, evolving environment. The Analytics Engineer will partner closely with business stakeholders and collaborate across regional and global teams to support Puig USA's long-term data and analytics strategy.

Approximately 80% of the role will focus on Analytics Engineering, including semantic modeling, Power BI development, SQL-based data transformation, Python automation, reusable datasets, and business partnership. The remaining 20% will support Data Platform Readiness, including lightweight data ingestion, API integrations, cloud platform exposure, data quality initiatives, and foundational work that helps prepare the organization for more scalable, cloud-enabled data and analytics capabilities.

This role is being established during a period of meaningful organizational and technology transformation. The U.S. Business & Data Analytics team currently operates an analytics ecosystem across Power BI, Python, Power Automate, SharePoint, SAP, retailer data feeds, and business-managed processes, while the enterprise data platform continues to evolve. The successful candidate will help deliver immediate business value while contributing to the development of more scalable, modern analytics capabilities for the future.

What You'll Get to Do:

Analytics Engineering

- Design, build, and maintain analytical datasets and semantic models that support reporting, self-service analytics, and business decision-making
- Develop scalable Power BI datasets, data models, reports, and dashboards with a strong focus on usability, performance, and consistency
- Use SQL and Python to transform, automate, and prepare data for analytical use cases.
- Partner with business stakeholders to translate requirements into practical, sustainable analytics solutions
- Improve data accessibility, documentation, and reuse across the reporting environment

Data Integration & Platform Readiness

- Build and maintain lightweight data ingestion and automation processes using SQL, Python, APIs, and other available tools
- Help strengthen data quality, validation, reliability, and reusability across reporting and analytical datasets
- Partner with regional and global teams on emerging cloud data initiatives and evolving data architecture practices
- Contribute to foundational data engineering activities and scalable data products as the organization's capabilities and platform landscape mature

Automation & Operational Support

- Build and maintain Python-based automation solutions that streamline recurring data and reporting processes
- Support Power Automate, SharePoint, and related low-code or no-code workflows used by the U.S. analytics team
- Troubleshoot production issues, data anomalies, and process failures across analytics and automation solutions
- Identify opportunities to reduce manual effort, improve reliability, and simplify ongoing support

Data & Analytics Transformation

- Partner with the Director of Business & Data Analytics to help shape the future analytics and data ecosystem for the U.S.
- Work collaboratively across the Business & Data Analytics team and with cross-functional stakeholders so solutions are connected, shared, and aligned to broader priorities
- Help identify opportunities to modernize reporting, automation, data management, and analytics workflows
- Contribute to the evolution of scalable data products, cloud-based data platforms, and modern analytics engineering practices
- Balance near-term business needs with longer-term technology and data transformation objectives
