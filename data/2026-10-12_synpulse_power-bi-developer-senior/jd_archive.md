We are looking for a Senior BI Developer to lead the technical evolution of our Management Information function. In this role, you won't just build reports; you will own the end-to-end delivery of automated reporting solutions, drive Power BI adoption across the organization, and act as a bridge between complex data structures and strategic business insights. Working closely with our internal data team, you will shape our data models and establish best practices for a high-performing analytics culture.

Your Key Responsibilities

- End-to-End Delivery: Lead the design, development, and deployment of complex BI solutions and interactive dashboards using the Power BI service.

- Technical Leadership: Translate complex business requirements into robust technical specifications and scalable data architectures.

- Data Engineering & Modeling: Design and optimize advanced data models (Star/Snowflake schemas) and manage complex data transformations using Power Query (M).

- System Optimization: Evaluate and improve existing BI systems, ensuring high performance, data accuracy, and security across all reporting layers.

- Stakeholder Management: Partner with function leads to identify Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and provide actionable insights that drive decision-making.

- QA & Governance: Establish and maintain rigorous unit testing, troubleshooting, and documentation standards for the analytics platform.

- Experience: 6+ years of proven experience in Business Intelligence or Data Analytics.
