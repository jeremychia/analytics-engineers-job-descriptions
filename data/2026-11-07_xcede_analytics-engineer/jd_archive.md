Analytics Engineer

Location: London | 1-2 days in the office per week

Salary: Up to £55,000 plus bonus

An established technology business is looking for an Analytics Engineer to join its team, helping turn complex data into reliable models and insights that support decisions across the business.

You’ll work closely with Product, Marketing, Engineering, BI and Data Science, building data products that support reporting, experimentation and customer insights. This is an opportunity to take ownership of transformation pipelines, improve data quality and contribute to the development of a modern data platform.

The role

As an Analytics Engineer, you will:

- Build, optimise and maintain scalable data transformation pipelines using dbt and Snowflake.

- Design reusable, well documented data models that make data accessible and trusted across the business.

- Improve the performance, security and cost efficiency of the Snowflake data warehouse.

- Develop automated data quality tests, monitoring and alerting to improve reliability.

- Work with technical and business stakeholders to translate requirements into effective data solutions.

- Promote good practices across data modelling, testing and documentation.

- Contribute to the evolution of the platform, including orchestration tooling such as Dagster.

What we’re looking for

- Strong SQL skills, including experience writing and optimising complex queries.

- Commercial experience using dbt to build and maintain data transformation pipelines.

- Experience with Snowflake or another modern cloud data warehouse, such as BigQuery.

- A good understanding of data modelling and how to structure data for analytics and reporting.

- Familiarity with Python for automation, data manipulation or integration tasks.

- An interest in improving data quality, reliability and usability.

- Strong communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively with technical and non-technical colleagues.

Technology environment

The wider stack includes Snowflake, dbt, SQL, Python, Fivetran, Dagster, Metaplane and Tableau, alongside AWS, Terraform and GitHub.

Experience with every technology listed is not required. Strong SQL and dbt skills, alongside relevant cloud data warehouse experience, are the key priorities.

What’s on offer

- Base salary of up to £55,000 plus bonus.

- Hybrid working with 1-2 days per week in the London office.

- The opportunity to influence data models, platform improvements and engineering practices.

- A collaborative environment with exposure to teams across the business.
