Analytics Engineer - Voyado

Join Voyado as an Analytics Engineer and help build the trusted data foundation behind the analytics and AI capabilities used by thousands of retailers.

We are super excited to announce that Robin Bodin is now looking for an Analytics Engineer to join our Insights & Activation product area, focused on building the trusted data foundation that powers analytics, predictive models, and future AI capabilities across Voyado Engage. Our mission is to make analytics data reliable, governed, and actionable end to end — from canonical data products and semantic standards to the metrics and insights surfaced to customers. As part of this journey, we're evolving both our data foundations and analytics products to create a more scalable, transparent, and trustworthy platform. We work in short feedback loops, close collaboration with stakeholders, and a strong belief that progress comes from learning and iteration — not just shipping.

About the role
This is not a traditional BI or dashboarding role. Team Intelligence is responsible for the analytics and reporting capabilities inside Voyado Engage. We're evolving towards a modern analytics foundation where governed datasets, certified metrics and semantic definitions are treated as shared product assets that power both embedded analytics and future AI-driven experiences.

As an Analytics Engineer, you'll build and own the data products behind our customer-facing analytics. Working hands-on with SQL, Databricks and modern data tooling, you'll model, validate and improve datasets, metric definitions and data quality processes. A key part of the role is identifying inconsistencies, duplicated logic and unclear ownership, and turning that into scalable and trusted solutions.

The role sits at the intersection of analytics, product and engineering. You'll collaborate closely with Product Managers, UX, analytics engineers and domain teams to ensure our analytics are understandable, consistent and reliable for customers

Some of the things you'll be up to:
- Designing and building governed data products with clear schemas, quality tests, lineage and ownership
- Writing SQL to model, transform and validate structured data
- Building and maintaining semantic definitions and certified metrics across analytics surfaces
- Collaborating with domain teams to improve data quality and upstream ownership
- Contributing to documentation, contracts and standards that make analytics reliable and understandable
- Supporting the foundation behind embedded BI and future AI-powered analytics capabilities

So, who are you?
We're looking for someone who combines strong technical ownership with a thoughtful and collaborative mindset. You care deeply about data quality, consistency and building systems that others can trust and scale on top of. You probably enjoy turning ambiguous business questions into clear and reliable metric definitions, and you prefer solving problems at the source rather than patching them downstream. You care about details like semantic clarity, documentation and naming conventions because you understand their long-term impact on both people and systems. At the same time, you enjoy collaborating across engineering, product, UX and customer-facing teams to build analytics experiences that are both technically robust and genuinely useful.

Besides the above, we believe you've got:
- Strong SQL skills for data exploration, transformation and validation
- Experience with dbt or similar tooling for governed data transformations
- Experience building or maintaining data products, semantic layers or certified metrics
- Understanding of concepts such as lineage, data contracts, canonical entities and quality testing
- Familiarity with modern data platforms such as Databricks, Snowflake or BigQuery

Experience within retail tech, e-commerce, Databricks or lakehouse-style data platforms is considered a strong plus.
