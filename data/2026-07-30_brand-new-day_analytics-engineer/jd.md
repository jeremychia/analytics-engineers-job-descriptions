# Analytics Engineer — Brand New Day

**URL:** https://werkenbijbrandnewday.recruitee.com/o/data-engineer-analytics-engineer-bangkok-1
**Location:** Bangkok
**Date Posted:** 2026-07-30

---

Brand New Day is strengthening its data foundation to better support Finance, Risk, and Regulatory Reporting. With a robust cloud data platform already in place, we're looking for an experienced Analytics Engineer to accelerate the delivery of reliable, high-quality data products.

We are building a BCBS 239-compliant data warehouse that will serve as the single source of truth for regulatory reporting and risk management. If you enjoy translating complex regulatory definitions into clean, dependable data models and want to work on datasets that directly impact financial governance, this is your opportunity to make a measurable impact within our growing Data & AI team.

What will you do as an Analytics Engineer?

Do you get energy from turning complex regulatory definitions into clean, trusted datasets that stand up to audits and pressure from regulatory deadlines? Are you the kind of person who appreciates a well-structured data model, clear lineage, and tests that catch issues before Finance or Risk Management does?

Great! Come strengthen our regulatory and risk management data products as a mid-level Analytics Engineer. In this role, you'll help build and maintain the data model for our regulatory reporting and risk management datasets. You'll translate regulatory and risk definitions into robust DBT models on Azure Databricks, and make sure our gold datasets are consistent, well-documented, tested and audit-ready.

Your key activities and responsibilities:

Design, develop and maintain data models on Azure Databricks using dbt and SQL

Build high-quality regulatory and risk datasets in the gold layer, ensuring consistency, traceability and semantic clarity across all layers

Translate regulatory and risk definitions into clear, consistent data logic in close collaboration with engineers, analysts and product owners

Implement robust testing, documentation and validation practices to ensure data sets are reliable and audit-ready

Contribute to data modelling best practices, including improving reusable logic, preventing duplication and documentation

Be proactive in issue analysis and issue resolution in collaboration with stakeholders

Make data analyses, derive insights, and recommend actions to take

You'll join our Data & AI team in Bangkok. We are a growing group of data engineers, analytics engineers and information analysts located in both Amsterdam and Bangkok who like to build valuable things, keep things practical, and help each other out.

What you'll receive from us

A pleasant salary

A provident fund

Medical and life insurance

Fourteen days of paid leave, excluding Thai public holidays

Weekly lunch and sports

Room to develop yourself, including training budget and autonomy to improve how we work

A high-ownership role in a fintech, where banking and payments are mission-critical

Close collaboration with data, engineering and business teams across Bangkok and Amsterdam

A Bangkok-based role with structured travel to Amsterdam for alignment and team connection

Compensation and local benefits to be discussed
