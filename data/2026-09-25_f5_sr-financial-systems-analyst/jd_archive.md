Position Summary:

We are seeking an experienced Senior Financial Systems Analyst to help us scale our Accounting team for the future. You’ll leverage your accounting, artificial intelligence (AI), and technical expertise to build solutions that enhance our accounting processes. You’ll develop tools, models and data products that automate complex workflows and provide actionable insights within our internal control framework. This role will serve as a key contributor in modernizing the accounting function through automation, data-driven reporting, and scalable process design.

This is an opportunity for a seasoned analyst who enjoys designing, building, and enabling automation—while collaborating directly with accounting teams to standardize processes, reduce manual effort, and increase efficiency.

Primary Responsibilities:

Lead transformation initiatives to enhance accounting scalability, efficiency, and data integrity.

Work across Accounting and cross-functional teams to uncover efficiency opportunities, including AI use cases, document requirements, and translate complex financial workflows into well-controlled technical solutions.

Identify, design, and implement AI and automation solutions (e.g., system workflows, data integrations, reporting tools) to streamline accounting processes.

Use tools such as BlackLine and Alteryx to automate journal entries, reconciliations, controls, reporting, and other accounting activities.

Improve data architecture and reporting consistency across ERP systems, subledgers, and reporting tools, while ensuring completeness and accuracy of data, and alignment between source systems.

Support end to end delivery—from analysis and solution design through configuration, testing, deployment, and stabilization.

Partner with Accounting and SOX compliance teams to assess and develop an effective internal controls approach to automated accounting solutions.

Perform other related duties as assigned.

Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities:

Functional experience in Accounting

Hands on experience with BlackLine and building workflows in Alteryx (required)

Demonstrated experience with AI/LLM tools, automation platforms and RPA (strongly preferred)

Proficiency in SQL and experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI (preferred)

Background in Oracle eBS ERP implementation and/or upgrades (preferred)

Demonstrated initiative in process improvement

Ability to work both independently and as part of a team with the ability to drive tasks to completion

Ability to effectively engage and communicate cross functionally at various levels of the organization both domestically and across the globe

Understanding of US GAAP and SOX frameworks

Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen eye for detail

Strong project management skills and the ability to handle multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Analytics, or related field.

Minimum of 8 years of experience in an accounting or finance systems reporting or data analysis role, preferably within a SaaS or technology environment.

Experience working in a large public accounting or consulting firm or multi-national corporate enterprise is preferable.
