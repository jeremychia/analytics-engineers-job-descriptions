Business Intelligence Engineer III, Amazon Now
Amazon
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

Description

We are looking for a Business Intelligence Engineer to join the Customer Experience (CX) team at Amazon Now. The BIE will own the end-to-end data ecosystem for CX — building scalable ETL pipelines, automated dashboards, and self-service reporting frameworks that track key metrics across the customer journey including conversion funnels, delivery experience, retention, and NPS. The role involves partnering cross-functionally with Product, UX, Engineering, and Operations teams to define success metrics for feature launches, conduct deep-dive analyses on customer behavior and journey pain points, support A/B experimentation, and build anomaly detection to proactively flag CX degradation.

About The Team

Our Quick Commerce team is passionate about solving complex location-based challenges that directly impact millions of customers. We work at the intersection of technology and operations, using advanced geo-spatial solutions to transform the way customers receive their deliveries. Our team combines expertise in product management, engineering, and operations to build innovative solutions that scale globally.

Basic Qualifications

- 10+ years of professional or military experience

- 5+ years of SQL experience

- Experience programming to extract, transform and clean large (multi-TB) data sets

- Experience with theory and practice of design of experiments and statistical analysis of results

- Experience with AWS technologies

- Experience in scripting for automation (e.g. Python) and advanced SQL skills.

- Experience with theory and practice of information retrieval, data science, machine learning and data mining

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience working directly with business stakeholders to translate between data and business needs

- Experience managing, analyzing and communicating results to senior leadership
