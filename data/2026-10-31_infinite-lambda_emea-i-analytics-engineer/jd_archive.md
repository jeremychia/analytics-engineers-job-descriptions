About Us

About The Role

At Infinite Lambda, Analytics Engineers turn vast amounts of data into insightful, easy-to-use, automated BI solutions. This is a highly client-facing role where you will work directly with our partners’ project teams to deliver high-impact analytics and reporting that drive better business decisions. You will leverage modern data tools with a strong focus on dbt for reliable data modeling/transformation and BI tools for analytics to build scalable, self-service solutions.

Anticipated start date: January 2027

Requirements

This client-facing role carries significant responsibility toward both our clients and delivery teams. We are looking for an experienced analytics professional who combines strong technical skills with excellent consultancy abilities.

Must-have Technical Skills

You need 5+ years of relevant experience and strong proficiency in:
- SQL (advanced fluency is non-negotiable)
- dbt (solid, hands-on experience required, including data modeling, transformation, testing, documentation, and deployment)
- ELT processes and modern data pipeline best practices
- Version control with Git
- BI tools: Strong experience with Tableau/ Power BI / Qlik / Looker
- Experience with Snowflake, Databricks, or similar cloud data platforms is highly desirable.

Must-have Client-facing & Consultancy Skills

Because most of the upcoming work is client-facing, the following are essential:
- Proven client-facing or consultancy experience — you enjoy working directly with stakeholders, gathering requirements, providing recommendations, and delivering impactful solutions
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Comfortable working in a distributed, agile team environment
- Self-starter with strong ownership and initiative
- High attention to detail and pride in delivering high-quality work

This role is ideal for someone who is technically strong in dbt and modern BI platforms and thrives in a client-centric, consultancy-style environment.
