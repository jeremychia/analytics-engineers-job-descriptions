About the role

Cameron Kennedy is partnering with a leading international bank in the City of London to recruit a Business Intelligence Developer for its IT team.

You’ll own the bank’s regulatory and management reporting systems, making sure that reports to the PRA, FCA and Bank of England are accurate, on time and reliable. You’ll also be the key link between the London business and the Head Office development centre, so fluent Mandarin and English are essential.

What you’ll be doing

- Developing, maintaining and supporting the Axiom regulatory reporting platform, covering COREP, FINREP, Liquidity, Bank of England returns and EMIR

- Running daily reporting operations, including ETL jobs, Axiom workflows and RPA processes, and resolving issues quickly

- Supporting the bank’s AML, tax and audit systems (GoldTier, GVRT, Pentana and Accuity), including resilience and security improvements

- Turning business and regulatory requirements into technical specifications, then managing testing, UAT and go-live

- Working with vendors and Head Office on regulatory upgrades and new reporting requirements

- Keeping systems secure, up to date and well documented in line with IT policies

What we’re looking for

- 5+ years’ software development or technical support experience in a database or financial reporting role

- Strong Oracle and/or MS SQL Server skills: complex queries, data transformation and report logic

- Banking or financial services background, ideally on regulatory reporting projects

- Understanding of data warehouse and data lake concepts

- Experience with Agile delivery

- Fluent Mandarin and English, spoken and written

Nice to have

- Database performance tuning, indexing and triggers
