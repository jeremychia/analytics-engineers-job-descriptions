About the Role

As a mid-level Analytics Engineer in our Data Analytics team, you will bridge the gap between underlying data pipelines and key business insights. Sitting at the center of a collaborative team that works in two-week agile sprints alongside product managers and data engineers, your core focus will span the end-to-end analytics experience—starting from data modeling all the way to actionable insights.

You will spend roughly 60% of your time on data modeling and 40% on BI, dashboards, and analytics, turning complex data into actionable insights for media owners, DSPs, and internal stakeholders. You will write high-performing SQL queries, optimize dbt models, and translate technical metrics into clear business narratives that empower colleagues to self-serve their data needs. Your immediate focus will be supporting our exciting migration from SnowFlake to ClickHouse, followed by opportunities in 2027 to help build semantic layers and introduce further AI and advanced analytics.
