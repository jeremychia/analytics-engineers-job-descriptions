Responsibilities:

Deliver business metrics and insights through reports, and interactive dashboards to develop and execute on client coverage strategic priorities.

Effectively interact with Client Coverage, Client Data Strategy, Sales Enablement, and other teams around the Firm to understand the business needs.

Work with large structured and unstructured data from multiple internal and external data sources to incorporate into analytical tools and dashboards.

Define and enforce visualization and reporting standards across the team.

Manage the Business Intelligence (BI)/analytical tools lifecycle, including delivery, training, user adoption and retiring old artifacts.

Partner with the Client Data & AI Enablement Lead and colleagues across the firm to ensure dashboards are built on clean, governed data by converting business requirements into SQL datasets.

Apply advanced analytical techniques to uncover patterns and insights into complex data sets.

Requirements:

Bachelor's Degree in IT, Business Analytics or related field of study.

6-8+ years' hands-on experience in building BI and analytics tools within Financial Services / Asset Management.

Strong analytical and technical skills; must be proficient in SQL, Python and Tableau or comparable tools; experience with Alteryx, R and utilizing statistical modules and machine learning algorithms would be helpful.

Ability to deliver meaningful visualizations from complex data sets that are understandable by various levels within the firm.

Strong oral and written communication skills with ability to explain technical ideas to business users.

Attention to detail and business acumen essential.

Team player with strong work ethic and focus on meeting deadlines & objectives.

Ability to work independently and demonstrate critical thinking.

Excellent time management, organizational skills, and attention to detail.

Location: New York City; hybrid with a minimum of three days per week in office.
