Position Overview
What the heck is a Data & Analytics Engineer, right?!
Think of it as the architect, builder, and strategist of our sales data universe, all rolled into one.
This role is the nerve center of Curvion’s reporting ecosystem, making sure every number, metric, and trend we rely on is accurate, trustworthy, and actionable.
You’ll build and optimize data infrastructure in BigQuery, fine-tune ETL/ELT processes, design dashboards in Looker Studio, and ensure our sales leaders have clear, reliable data to guide decisions.
In short, you’re the person who makes the data magic happen so our teams can operate smarter, close more deals, and scale faster.
If you love clean data, efficient workflows, solving complex data problems, and turning raw numbers into actionable insights, you’re going to thrive here.

You SHOULD Apply to This Role If:
- You’re fluent in SQL and genuinely enjoy query optimization

- You have strong Python skills and understand how to use it to solve real data problems

- You love taking messy, scattered data and making it reliable and usable

- You can build dashboards that make complex information easy for sales leaders to understand

- You enjoy building pipelines and workflows that work reliably and efficiently

- You can spot a reporting anomaly from a mile away

- You’re obsessed with accuracy, data integrity, and efficiency

- You naturally investigate the root cause when something doesn’t look right

- You get fired up about eliminating manual reporting processes

- You want to directly impact revenue without having to jump on sales calls

- You’re comfortable working independently while still collaborating closely with a high-energy team
