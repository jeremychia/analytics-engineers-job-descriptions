About The Role & Team

IDC seeks a skilled, motivated, and dedicated Data Analytics Engineer to help build some of the most advanced data products to join a team that is building a leading intelligence platform for IT vendors. You will design and launch innovative analytical solutions by handling large datasets and developing tools for complex data operations. You’ll be working on Market & Vendor Data Products . If you are passionate about working with data and solving complex problems, this role is for you!

What You’ll Do

- Develop tools and applications in Python to perform data processing, data analysis, modelling, forecasting. Utilize statistical, mathematical, and algorithmic principles.

- Prototype, test, and deploy end-to-end data operation pipelines.

- Develop web-based applications using Streamlit for data visualization and reporting.

- Design, build, and optimize Python/Snowflake-based data operation pipelines for ingesting, cleaning, transforming, and loading data.

- Collaborate with global technical and analyst teams to gather requirements.

Required

What You Bring:

- BA/BS in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Data Engineering or equivalent experience (3-5 years) in related fields.

- 1-2 years of relevant work experience in a technical role, preferably with experience managing individuals for team outcomes.

- Proficiency with Python and SQL-based environments , preferably Snowflake , or similar databases (e.g., MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle, or Microsoft SQL).

Nice To Have

- Experience with cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.

- Experience developing algorithms for data quality checks and outlier detection.

- Familiarity with machine learning and Gen AI.
