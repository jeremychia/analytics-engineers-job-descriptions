About The Role

We are looking for an experienced BI Engineer to play a critical role covering product innovation and data integrity. You will split your expertise between enhancing the JobAdder Analytics Product (Looker/Python/SQL) and supporting our Internal Business Intelligence ecosystem (Tableau/DWH Strategy). Most importantly, you will own the "Single Source of Truth," ensuring every KPI across the company is documented, governed, and technically sound.

- Rigorous peer code reviews in GitHub to maintain high-quality standards.

- Balance "Business as Usual" (BAU) support and troubleshooting with the development of long-term product features and releases.

2. BI Delivery & DWH Strategy
