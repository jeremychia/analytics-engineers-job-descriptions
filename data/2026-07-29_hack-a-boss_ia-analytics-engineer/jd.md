# IA Analytics Engineer — Hack A Boss

**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4443251664/
**Location:** Madrid, Spain
**Date Posted:** 2026-07-29

---

We're looking for an engineer who wants to shape the future of AI-powered analytics.

If you're excited about building semantic models, enabling natural language analytics, and making data accessible through LLMs, this role is for you.

What you'll be working on

Designing high-performance analytical models on modern OLAP databases (ClickHouse or similar)

Building AI-powered analytics experiences, including:

Text-to-SQL

Conversational BI

RAG over structured data

Semantic layers for governed analytics

Creating semantic models from the ground up, defining measures, dimensions, joins, and pre-aggregations that power reliable business insights.

Developing APIs and backend tooling using TypeScript (and collaborating with Ruby on Rails services).

Designing real-time analytical pipelines with streaming technologies such as Kafka and ClickHouse ingestion.

We're looking for someone with

Strong SQL and experience with ClickHouse or another columnar OLAP database

Deep understanding of dimensional modeling, star/snowflake schemas, and query optimization

Experience integrating LLMs into analytics products

Knowledge of prompt engineering, function/tool calling, semantic modeling, and reducing hallucinations

TypeScript experience and familiarity with modern backend development

Ability to translate business questions into scalable data models

Nice to have

AWS or GCP

Docker & Kubernetes

Cube.js, dbt, LookML, Metabase, Superset, or Tableau

Vector databases and embedding-based retrieval

If you enjoy solving complex data problems and building the next generation of AI-native analytics platforms, we'd love to hear from you.
