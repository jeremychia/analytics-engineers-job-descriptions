Analytics Engineer

BI Engineering Senior Analyst | Early Career | Full time

Job Summary

We are seeking a highly qualified professional to join our team as an Analytics Engineer. This role sits at the intersection of Business Intelligence and Data Engineering, focusing on transforming, modeling, and delivering high-quality business data for analytics and reporting. We are looking for professionals with a strong business mindset combined with solid data and engineering knowledge.

Requisitos

Qualifications

Minimum of 3+ years of experience in Business Data, BI Engineering, or Analytics Engineering roles

Strong proficiency in SQL and Python

Solid experience with data modeling, data transformations, and analytical datasets

Exposure to cloud data platforms and data warehouses

Hands-on experience with data cleaning, validation, and testing

Experience working with data transformation frameworks

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

Excellent communication and collaboration (soft skills)

Good level of English (written and spoken)

Experience with BI and data visualization tools

Understanding of APIs and data integration concepts

Knowledge of ELT processes and data workflows

Responsibilities

Design, develop, and maintain KPI dashboards and executive reporting solutions, including metric calculation frameworks and data visualization

Build and manage data transformation pipelines following ELT best practices

Perform data cleaning, validation, and testing to ensure data quality and reliability

Deploy and test analytical datasets and transformations

Collaborate closely with business stakeholders to understand reporting and analytics needs

Integrate data from multiple sources using APIs and structured workflows

Support and optimize BI tools and semantic layers

Document data models, transformations, and analytical logic

Contribute to continuous improvement of analytics engineering processes and tooling

Additional Information

Location: Lisbon

Working Model: Hybrid
