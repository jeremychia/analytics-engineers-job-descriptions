Join Our Business Intelligence Team

We're looking for a talented Business Intelligence (BI) Analyst to join our growing BI team. In this role, you'll work closely with Product, Operations, Payment, Finance, Risk, and Data Engineering to transform data into actionable insights and drive data-informed decision-making across the organization.

The ideal candidate is analytical, business-oriented, and thrives in a fast-paced environment. You should be able to quickly understand complex business domains, collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams, and independently deliver high-quality analytical solutions with minimal onboarding. Prior experience in cryptocurrency, digital assets, fintech, or other data-intensive industries is highly preferred.

Responsibilities

Partner with business stakeholders to understand analytical requirements and translate them into scalable reporting and dashboard solutions

Design, develop, and maintain dashboards and self-service reporting using Tableau or other BI tools

Write efficient and optimized SQL to extract, transform, and analyze large-scale datasets

Work closely with Data Engineering teams on data modeling, ETL pipelines, metric implementation, and data quality improvements

Define, maintain, and govern business KPIs and metric definitions to ensure consistency across teams

Perform ad hoc analyses to identify business opportunities, operational issues, and performance drivers

Monitor key business metrics, investigate anomalies, and provide timely insights and recommendations

Support business initiatives through performance analysis, experimentation, and strategic reporting

Present analytical findings clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

Continuously improve reporting automation, analytical processes, and data governance

Requirements

Qualifications - Required

Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field

3+ years of experience in BI, Data Analytics, or similar roles

Advanced SQL skills (joins, window functions, CTEs, optimization)

Hands-on experience with Tableau, Power BI, or equivalent

Experience working closely with Data Engineering teams

Strong understanding of data warehouse concepts and metric design

Excellent analytical, communication, and stakeholder management skills

Ability to quickly learn new business domains with minimal onboarding

Self-motivated and comfortable in a fast-paced environment

Qualifications - Preferred

Experience in cryptocurrency, digital assets, fintech, payments, or exchanges

Experience with Databricks, Spark SQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift

Python (Pandas) for analysis and automation

Experience designing KPI frameworks and executive dashboards

Knowledge of experimentation, data governance, and metadata management

Technical Skills

Advanced SQL

Tableau or equivalent BI platform

Data Visualization

Business Analysis

KPI & Metric Design

Databricks / Spark SQL (Preferred)

What Success Looks Like

Deliver trusted, scalable, and actionable business reporting

Build intuitive dashboards for business stakeholders

Partner effectively with Data Engineering

Generate insights that drive measurable business impact

Become a trusted analytics partner across the organization
