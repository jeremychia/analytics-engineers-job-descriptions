Data Analytics Engineer
Booking Holdings (NASDAQ: BKNG)
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

At Booking.com, our mission is to make it easier for everyone to experience the world. With strategic long-term investments into what we believe the future of travel can be, we are opening career opportunities that will have a strong impact on our mission.

The Financial Systems team provides technology expertise to the finance department, and is responsible for SAP HANA and connected 3rd party systems at Booking.com. We want to change the way people work with SAP, by building a finance application platform that supports simplification of business processes, and empowers the finance community with better financial insights.

As a BI Engineer, you will be responsible for building and maintaining reporting solutions. You will have a thorough understanding of SAP Hana and data integration and be responsible for designing and implementing business requirements as well as data accuracy in reports. You will support the team in day-to-day SAP Hana development and support, monitoring the production environment, identifying and fixing issues, and improving system performance. As this role involves daily interaction with the finance department, it is essential that you have a good background in finance, and strong verbal and written communication skills in English.

Due to the rapid and continuous growth of the organization, the tasks and duties of this role may evolve over time. You will be keen to develop and grow within the role and to take on additional challenges and responsibilities as they occur.

B.Responsible:

- Development and support of BI environment in collaboration with finance stakeholders. Ongoing development and maintenance of SAP Hana reporting platform.

- Responsible for data loading, monitoring and system performance.

- Engage with users to understand their needs and develop/maintain application, logical and technical data architectures.

- Integration and implementation of changes.

- Training of key users.

B.Skilled:

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field.

- 7+ years of expertise in the IT industry

- Experience with SAP data products: SAP S/4(data structures, CDS Embedded), SAP HANA XSA development and XSA & DB Security concepts (Roles, Privileges etc), SAP BDC/Datasphere, SAP Hana cloud, BW/4 on HANA

- Snowflake Data modelling , Dbt. Good to have experience

- Experience on Dimensional Modelling or Data Vault.

- Strong knowledge of SQL, Stored Procedures and Functions

- Understanding of different descriptor file formats like YAML.

- Exposure to Apache Airflow or any similar orchestration tool is a plus

- Exposure to security concepts in Snowflake is a plus

- Experience with Git concepts (GitHub / Gitlab) is a must.

- Experience with CI/CD, pipelines is a plus

- Knowledge of agile development processes (SCRUM or Kanban) and experience with Jira a plus

- Knowledge and experience in SAP based Financial Reporting is a plus

- Superior attention to detail and quality required

- Proven ability to adopt new technologies

- Demonstrated interest for researching new technologies and developing business cases to support stakeholders across the organization

- Ability to have a good grasp of the details and manage multiple priorities. Able to work in a quick turnaround environment.

- Strong analytical and problem solving skills.

- Strong communication skills in English, both verbal and written.

- Independent, organized, flexible, proactive and result-oriented.

- Ability to own process end to end
