Your Role

As an Analytics Engineer, you will:

- Build and maintain Snowflake data models using medallion architecture, transforming raw data into cleansed, conformed, and business-ready datasets.

- Develop and govern a centralized semantic layer to ensure consistent definitions of business metrics, KPIs, and rules, including ARR, MRR, revenue, and churn.

- Create and maintain Power BI datasets, automated board reports, management reporting, and dashboards for Customer Experience and Product teams.

- Implement automated testing, data quality checks, and CI/CD processes to ensure the accuracy, reliability, and performance of data models and reporting assets.

- Optimize Snowflake queries and compute efficiency to improve platform performance and resource utilization.

- Build and support data ingestion pipelines from core business systems, including customer databases, HubSpot, MYOB, and Zendesk.

- Monitor pipeline health, manage schema changes, troubleshoot failures, and maintain reliable customer ID matching across data sources.

- Apply data governance standards, metadata management, documentation, role-based access controls, and data security policies across Snowflake and Power BI.

- Collaborate with stakeholders and cross-functional teams to gather requirements, deliver data solutions, and support data-driven decision-making.

- Prepare curated and well-documented datasets for Snowflake Cortex, Snowflake Intelligence, and AI-powered analytics tools.

- Leverage approved AI tools to accelerate SQL development, data modelling, testing, and documentation while validating all AI-generated outputs before implementation.

- Support data privacy, security, and compliance requirements, including SOC 2 and PCI DSS controls.

What You Need

Non-negotiables

- At least 4 years of experience in analytics engineering, BI development, or data engineering.

- Advanced SQL skills and hands-on experience with Snowflake, including warehouses, roles, and performance tuning.

- Strong knowledge of dimensional modelling, data warehousing, star schemas, fact and dimension tables, and slowly changing dimensions.

- Experience with Power BI data modelling, including Power Query and DAX.

- Experience building and maintaining governed semantic or metrics layers.

- Hands-on experience with data transformation tools such as dbt, Git-based version control, automated testing, code reviews, and CI/CD.

- Practical experience with ELT pipelines, data ingestion, and orchestration.

- Working knowledge of AI tools for SQL development, modelling, and analysis, with the ability to validate and verify generated outputs.

- Strong analytical and communication skills, with the ability to translate business requirements into data models and explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

- Ability to collaborate with global teams across different time zones.

Preferred Skills/expertise

- Python experience for data pipelines, automation, or lightweight data applications.

- Experience with Fivetran, Airbyte, or AWS-native ingestion services such as DMS, Glue, and Lambda, including CDC patterns.

- Experience working with multi-tenant SaaS data environments.

- Familiarity with Snowflake Cortex, Snowflake Intelligence, or other AI-powered and natural language analytics tools.

- Knowledge of data governance, metadata management, and row-level and column-level security.

- Working knowledge of GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act, SOC 2, or PCI DSS compliance requirements.

- Experience working with SaaS business metrics, including ARR, MRR, NRR, and churn.

- Exposure to the hospitality or travel industry.

- Snowflake SnowPro certification.
