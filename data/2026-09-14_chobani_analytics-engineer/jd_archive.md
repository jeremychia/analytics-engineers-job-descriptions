Summary

We're looking for an analytical and collaborative data professional to help us make better use of our data. In this role you'll work closely with business teams to understand their needs and build the reports, dashboards, and data models that support day-to-day decision making. You'll be comfortable working across multiple data sets, identifying gaps, and either building solutions yourself or partnering with data engineers to get there.

Responsibilities

- Build and maintain data models that support business reporting and analytics

- Partner with Supply Chain, Retail Execution, Commercial Finance and other stakeholders to understand workflows and improve them with data tools

- Help automate manual processes and improve how data is delivered across the organization

- Create custom pipelines to ingest data into Snowflake data warehouse from external sources as needed

- Assist with addressing any gaps in reporting as result of system upgrades and system implementations

- Assist with cleanup and standardization efforts of DBT instance

Requirements

- 5+ years in a data analyst, data engineer, or BI developer role

- 5+ years writing SQL

- Experience with Python is a plus

- 2+ years working with cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift)

- 2+ years working in DBT

- SAP experience is a plus

- Experience building reports and dashboards for business stakeholders

- Comfortable translating business questions into data solutions

- Experience in CPG or Food & Beverage a plus

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or related field preferred

- Ability to travel up to 10%

About Us

Chobani is a food maker with a mission of making high-quality and nutritious food accessible to more people, while elevating our communities and making the world a healthier place. In short: making good food for all. In support of this mission, Chobani is a purpose-driven, people-first, food-and-wellness-focused company, and has been since its founding in 2005 by Hamdi Ulukaya, an immigrant to the U.S. The Company manufactures yogurt, oat milk, and creamers – Chobani yogurt is America's No.1 yogurt brand, made with natural ingredients without artificial preservatives. Following the 2023 acquisition of La Colombe, a leading coffee roaster with a shared commitment to quality, craftmanship and impact, the Company began selling cold-pressed espresso and lattes on tap at cafés nationwide, as well as Ready to Drink (RTD) coffee beverages at retail. In 2025, Chobani acquired Daily Harvest, a modern brand offering consumers nutritious, delicious and convenient ready-to-make meals.

Chobani uses food as a force for good in the world – putting humanity first in everything it does. The company's philanthropic efforts prioritize giving back to its communities and beyond. Chobani manufactures its products in New York, Idaho, Michigan and Australia, and its products are available throughout North America and distributed in Australia and other select markets.
