Senior BI Engineer
XTB online investing
Warsaw, Mazowieckie, Poland

XTB is a global company from the financial industry, focusing on online trading of financial instruments. We are the largest FinTech in Poland and a leader in Central and Eastern Europe, and the range of our operations covers several countries, including Asia and South America. At XTB, we focus on the development of our employees, giving them opportunities to gain knowledge and skills in various fields, as well as offering a number of training and development programs. If you are looking for challenges and want to gain valuable experience in an international business environment, XTB is the right place for you.

We are a certified Great Place to Work company.

Responsibilities

- Designing, modifying, and maintaining business reports and applications using Power BI and Databricks.
- Working in a Databricks/dbt environment, including developing and maintaining data processing pipelines.
- Building and collaborating on the development of AI Agents within the Databricks environment.
- Preparing and modifying SQL scripts for data modeling.
- Supporting the team in requirements analysis, identifying data sources, and building ETL processes.
- Collaborating with cross-functional teams and programs to create datasets that meet reporting needs and address systemic issues.
- Developing and maintaining project documentation.
- Implementing best practices, identifying and resolving bottlenecks, and optimizing processes.
- Mentoring team members and supporting the development of their technical skills.
- Collaborating within the project team using Jira for planning, tracking, and task management to ensure efficient project delivery.

Requirements

- Minimum 5 years of experience in Business Intelligence, data visualization, and analytics.
- Proficiency in data modeling, process optimization, and building data marts.
- Advanced knowledge of Power BI (DAX, DAX Studio, Tabular Editor, T-SQL).
- Advanced knowledge of Databricks (DABs, Lakeflow Pipelines, Metric Views, and optimization techniques).
- Experience in Power BI environment management, working with .pbip files alongside version control systems (e.g., Git).
- Understanding of DevOps practices and hands-on collaboration in process automation (e.g., CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code).
- Practical experience working in cloud environments (Databricks, Azure).
- Implementing data quality controls and validation processes to ensure accuracy, consistency, and completeness.
- Openness to learning and adapting to new technologies and concepts.
- Familiarity with Data Governance concepts and Data Mesh architecture.

Nice to have

- Familiarity with AI Coding Agents (Claude Code, etc.).
- Certifications in Power BI, Databricks, dbt, Azure, or GCP.
- Knowledge of financial instruments and markets.
