Do you want to contribute to the energy transition and a sustainable world? And does developing data-driven business solutions give you energy? Then join our Data & Analytics team as an Analytics Engineer!
You will be a part of the Vattenfall BtC (Business to Consumer) Data & Analytics department, which develops data driven solutions for our customers and customer processes. The department is spread out in three business domain teams; where data experts and business collaborate closely to realize valuable data science solutions. Each Data & Analytics domain team consists of an Analytics Translator, Analytics Engineers and Data Scientists, and is supported by Data- & Machine Learning Engineers from IT.
We value joint development in ways of working and sharing best practices. This is facilitated in our data science authority and data foundation authority. As an Analytics Engineer, you will participate in the Data Foundation authority for knowledge sharing and creating standards for our work.
Your role

As an Analytics Engineer, you know how to support our business by creating the right insights from data. You can find the relevant data for every insight on our platform. You are an expert in adding new logic to it and in modelling this data in a way that enables users to answer their questions. You create relevant data sets and dashboards. In addition, you collaborate with the team how to keep improving our data products and convey your enthusiasm about data to the organization. In this position, you will have the chance to work with innovative technologies and make an impact!

Your tasks and responsibilities:
- Translate stakeholder requirements into actionable insights
- Develop data sets and dashboards that empower users to answer their questions and to support business decision making
- Explain how to interpret and use data sets and dashboards in a straightforward way to various stakeholders
- Convey enthusiasm about data to inspire and engage the organization
- Leverage data to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for business growth
- Stay up to date with innovative technologies in the field of data analytics and leverage them to drive impact
Location: Amsterdam

Qualifications

Who are you?
As an Analytics Engineer you have a passion for data and are a good communicator. You are analytical, results-orientated, and like to work within an agile team.
Next to this you bring:
- A completed HBO/WO education with a strong data or technical component
- 3+ years of experience in (Analytics) Engineering;
- Strong SQL and data modelling skills; experience with Snowflake and cloud platforms (Azure);
- Experience with CI/CD, DevOps, git and code reviews;
- Experience with data visualization tools;
- Enjoy delivering data sets or dashboards with business insights.
Your qualifications will be tested with a business case.
