Senior Analytics Engineer

    Do you enjoy transforming raw data into trusted, reusable, and business-ready insights? Are you passionate about building the analytics layer that connects modern data platforms with business intelligence? Do you thrive  working with modern cloud platforms, semantic models, and visualization tools to enable data-driven decision-making? Then don't hesitate to apply for a challenging future in analytics.

what you’ll do.

 Collaborate with clients to design and deliver modern analytics solutions, from business requirements to interactive dashboards and reports

 Design and develop data models, curated datasets, and semantic models that provide a trusted foundation for reporting, self-service analytics, and AI

 Facilitate design and requirements sessions with business and technical stakeholders

 Build and maintain data transformations using SQL, dbt, Python, or other modern analytics engineering tools

 Develop interactive dashboards, reports, and visualizations using tools such as Power BI, Tableau, Qlik, or Looker, applying data visualization and storytelling best practices

 Perform data profiling, validation, and quality checks to ensure reliable and consistent analytics

 Support the design of modern data warehouses, lakehouses, and analytics platforms in collaboration with data engineering teams

 Work closely with data architects, data engineers, data analysts, and business stakeholders to ensure analytics solutions are aligned with business needs

 Produce functional and technical documentation

 Stay up to date with developments in analytics engineering, semantic modeling, modern BI platforms, AI-assisted analytics, and cloud data technologies

what you’ll bring.

 Bachelor’s degree in IT, applied mathematics, statistics, business analytics, or other relevant field, or equivalent practical experience

 5+ years experience in analytics engineering, business intelligence, or data analytics, preferably within a consulting environment

 Strong experience with requirements gathering, data modeling, semantic modeling, and dimensional modeling for analytics and reporting

 Hands-on experience with SQL and modern data transformation frameworks such as dbt; experience with Python is a plus

 Experience building reports and dashboards using Power BI, Tableau, Qlik, Looker, or similar BI platforms

 Good understanding of modern data platforms, including data warehouses, lakehouses, cloud platforms, and analytics workflows

 Exposure to or interest in AI/ML integration and modern business intelligence concepts

 Experience working in Agile environments

 Strong analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to bridge business and technology

Non-negotiable: strong communication skills in Dutch and English
