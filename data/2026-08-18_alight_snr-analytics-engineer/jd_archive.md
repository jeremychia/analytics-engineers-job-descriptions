JD – Analytics Senior Software Engineer

Experience & Expectations :

Leverage extensive experience (5 to 8 years overall Analytics experience).

We are seeking a hands-on 
Analytics Senior Engineer
 to bridge the gap between Data Engineering and Business Analytics within the Alight ecosystem. The role focuses on designing robust analytics datasets, mentoring junior engineers, and driving BI solutions with a strong focus on performance, scalability, and governance

The ideal candidate will work closely with Data Engineers, BI teams, and Product stakeholders to build robust semantic models, and support enterprise analytics platforms.

1. Data Modelling

Design enterprise-grade data models

Own semantic layer architecture

Standardize KPIs and business metrics

Ensure consistency, reusability, and scalability of data models

2. Analytics Engineering 

Work alongside Data Engineers to understand the 
ETL/ELT pipelines

3. BI & Reporting Enablement

Build and optimize complex reports & dashboards

Define data model strategies

Handle large-scale analytical workloads

Design and optimize datasets for BI tools (AWS QuickSight, Tableau, Power BI)

Partner with BI developers to build dashboards, KPIs, and analytical reports

Support ad-hoc analytics and business use cases

4. Data Quality, Governance & Security

Implement advanced 
RLS/CLS models

Define best practices for BI and data usage

5. Performance Optimization

Tune complex SQL transformations

Minimize dashboard latency and cost

Ensure efficient handling of large datasets across distributed systems

6. Leadership & Mentorship

Mentor junior engineers

Review code, models, and dashboards

Technical Skills (Alight Ecosystem Focus)

Expert SQL (Redshift or similar MPP systems)

Visualization Tools – QuickSight, Tableau, Power BI

Database – Redshift, DynamoDB

Strong AWS data ecosystem knowledge

Data modelling expertise

Preferred / Good-to-Have Skills

AWS Certification (Cloud Practitioner / Associate)

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field

Soft Skills

Ability to translate business requirements into data solutions

Strong collaboration across distributed teams (onshore/offshore)

Ownership mindset with focus on delivery quality\
