About the role

The Junior Business Intelligence (BI) Developer will support with the development and optimisation of the company’s BI platforms, supporting with the creation of high-quality, actionable insights to both internal stakeholders and external clients. This role requires the individual to support with data-driven decision-making across the business and enhance the company’s data infrastructure and reporting capabilities. This role is suited to someone early in their BI career. You will work under the guidance of the BI Manager, with structured support to build your Sisense skills.

Duties and Responsibilities

• Respond to routine report and data requests from across the business.

• Make changes to existing data models within BI platforms in line with development updates and sprints.

• Build dashboards and widgets to an agreed design, including formula development and filter management.

• Carry out data quality checks, reconciling reports against source data and ensuring compliance with GDPR and other data protection regulations.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams and third-party consultants to gather requirements and deliver BI solutions.

• Work closely within the product team to align with sprint delivery.

• Investigate and report dashboard and build issues.

• Support strategic decision-making through trend analysis and business performance insights.

• Operate within an Agile delivery framework and support with cleansing data and implementing efficient management of BI delivery.

• Build knowledge of Sisense and SQL through structured learning and training

Essential Skills

· Some exposure to a BI or dashboarding tool (Sisense, Power BI or similar), with experience delivering at least one dashboard based report end to end to a real stakeholder.

· A creative approach to data presentation decisions and problems, aligned with stakeholder requirements.

· Experience with SQL, SQL Server, and other database management systems.

· Good understanding of General Data Protection Regulations

· An understanding of working in an Agile methodology

· Willingness to learn and an ability to work independently, perform their own research and work under pressure.

· Excellent professionalism and personal presentation

· Excellent written and spoken communication skills

Desirable Skills

• Qualification in computer science, data science, or other STEM type field

• Previous experience working in either recruitment and / or pre-employment screening in a previous role.

• Experience of diagnosing and resolving performance issues across BI data models.
