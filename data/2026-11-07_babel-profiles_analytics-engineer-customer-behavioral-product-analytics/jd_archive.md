Analytics Engineer (Customer Behavioral / Product Analytics)
Babel Profiles
Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain

Ready to shape the digital experience for millions of shoppers across Europe? On behalf of Europe’s leading consumer electronics and omnichannel retail giant, we are seeking an Experienced Data Analyst – Digital Sales to join their international tech hub in Barcelona. Embedded within their Digital Sales Data Foundation team, you will drive strategic, data-backed decisions across their high-traffic e-commerce platforms, turning complex user behavior data into measurable growth opportunities and actionable KPIs.

Collaborating closely with Product Owners and Data Engineers, you will champion end-to-end A/B testing and experimentation, design scalable tracking frameworks, and work hands-on with SQL, GCP (BigQuery), and modern BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker).

If you bring a solid track record in digital analytics, strong statistical rigor, and the ambition to influence large-scale product roadmaps, this is an outstanding opportunity to make a tangible impact—apply today and take your career to the next level!

Main Responsibilities

- Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and business teams to shape, deploy, and continuously elevate tracking, digital analytics, and experimentation roadmaps across the digital journey.

- Architect scalable tracking frameworks from design to documentation, guaranteeing high data integrity, reliability, and seamless usability across BI reporting, A/B testing, and third-party tools.

- Turn data into product strategy by defining key performance metrics, building intuitive dashboards, and translating complex user behavior into concrete, revenue-driving recommendations.

- Own the experimentation lifecycle end-to-end, overseeing hypothesis formulation, sample size calculations, statistical analysis, and clear go/no-go rollout guidance.

- Scale the testing ecosystem across multiple platforms and European markets by optimizing workflows, building automated tooling, and establishing shared testing frameworks.

- Get hands-on with enterprise cloud data, querying complex datasets, navigating pipelines, and partnering side-by-side with data engineers and software developers to ensure seamless data flows.

- Serve as a trusted analytical advisor to cross-functional stakeholders, driving data literacy, mentoring teammates, and fostering a culture of continuous learning.

Core Skills/Experience

- Solid track record in digital or product analytics (CRO, experimentation, or digital sales), ideally gained within high-traffic e-commerce or fast-paced digital product environments.

- Deep command of tracking concepts and modern BI stacks, including hands-on experience designing KPI frameworks, ensuring data governance, and building reporting in tools like Tableau, Power BI, Looker, Adobe Analytics, or Firebase.

- Proven expertise in statistical experimentation, comfortable steering the entire A/B testing lifecycle—from hypothesis setup and power calculations/sample sizing to significance testing and communicating business impact.

- Advanced technical querying capabilities, with strong hands-on proficiency in SQL and direct experience navigating complex data pipelines and cloud data warehouses (especially GCP / BigQuery).

- Technical fluency with developers, bringing demonstrated experience in drafting clear measurement requirements, tracking specs, data flows, and implementation logic for engineering teams.

- Forward-thinking approach to modern workflows, comfortable utilizing version control (GitHub), AI-assisted productivity tools (Copilot, AI agents), and modern automation tools.

- Exceptional stakeholder diplomacy, with the ability to bridge commercial needs and technical solutions, articulating complex analytical insights with clarity and confidence.

- High ownership and intellectual curiosity, combining the autonomy to self-organize and solve ambiguous problems with enthusiasm for collaborating in cross-functional agile teams.
