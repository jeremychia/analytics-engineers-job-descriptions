About Empowered Buildings

Empowered Buildings is a technology-driven building automation and energy management company focused on improving the efficiency, reliability, and operational performance of commercial and residential buildings.

Through our proprietary BLOOM platform, we combine real-time monitoring, building automation, advanced analytics, and AI-driven insights to identify inefficiencies, optimize equipment performance, reduce energy consumption, and deliver measurable cost savings.

About the Role

We are seeking a technically curious, analytical, and solutions-oriented Building Optimization & Data Analytics Engineer to help customers improve building performance through data, engineering, software, and artificial intelligence.

This role combines technical analysis, operational problem-solving, software development collaboration, and direct customer engagement. The successful candidate will investigate equipment performance, identify inefficiencies, develop optimization strategies, and translate complex building data into actionable recommendations.

The individual will also help advance BLOOM's analytical capabilities by identifying opportunities to leverage AI, automate workflows, and develop scalable approaches to building optimization.

We value individuals who demonstrate independent judgment, technical curiosity, resourcefulness, and ownership of problems from investigation through resolution.

Key Responsibilities

Building Performance & Optimization

- Analyze real-time and historical data related to energy consumption, HVAC equipment, sensors, and building operations.

- Identify inefficiencies, equipment faults, abnormal consumption patterns, and energy savings opportunities.

- Develop and implement data-driven strategies to improve equipment performance, energy efficiency, and operational reliability.

- Investigate technical issues, identify root causes, and coordinate corrective actions with engineering and operations teams.

- Establish performance benchmarks and KPIs to monitor operational improvements.

- Quantify and verify energy savings and financial outcomes resulting from optimization initiatives.

Software, Analytics & Artificial Intelligence

- Utilize BLOOM, databases, APIs, and analytical tools to extract and interpret building performance data.

- Apply AI tools to accelerate technical analysis, troubleshoot problems, automate workflows, and improve productivity.

- Develop scripts, queries, analytical models, and reporting tools to support investigations and optimization.

- Collaborate with software developers to enhance fault detection, predictive analytics, automated reporting, and optimization capabilities.

- Identify opportunities to automate repetitive processes and standardize analytical methodologies across customer portfolios.

- Translate engineering challenges and operational requirements into actionable software improvements.

Customer Engagement & Technical Problem-Solving

- Work directly with customers to understand operational challenges, energy costs, and performance objectives.

- Investigate customer-reported issues and develop practical, data-supported solutions.

- Present technical findings, optimization recommendations, and financial benefits to technical and nontechnical stakeholders.

- Collaborate with customers to implement and evaluate energy conservation strategies.

- Take ownership of assigned initiatives, including investigation, implementation, follow-up, and performance verification.

Operational Excellence & Continuous Improvement

- Develop repeatable analytical processes, reporting standards, and troubleshooting methodologies.

- Identify recurring equipment issues and develop proactive strategies to improve reliability and reduce service requirements.

- Maintain clear documentation of technical findings, recommendations, corrective actions, and outcomes.

- Collaborate across engineering, software, and operations to improve workflows, knowledge sharing, and service delivery.

- Support the development of automated alerts, performance dashboards, and portfolio-level reporting.
