About Your Role

We're hiring a Senior Business Analytics Engineer to produce the revenue numbers Juniper Square runs on. This role produces one computed definition of metrics for different ARR metrics for Sales, Finance, Fund Admin and leadership teams at different levels.

You'll own the dbt transformation logic and the semantic-layer definitions on top of our internal data warehouse, as part of a company-wide rebuild of how revenue moves from quote to recognized revenue. You'll also be the link between business stakeholders and our Data Engineering team, turning business questions into precise technical requirements. And you'll build the AI-assisted reporting layer that lets people and AI agents get the same trustworthy answer from the same source.

What You'll Do

Transformation & data modeling
- Own dbt models for revenue and customer data sourced from CRM, CPQ, billing, and ERP systems
- Model the chain from quote to order to provisioning to invoice to recognized revenue at the entity and service grain
- Move reporting-level data generation out of Salesforce and into the warehouse, where other systems can consume it

Semantic layer & metric definitions
- Define and govern certified metrics like ARR and CARR and the dimensions behind them
- Write definitions precise enough for both people and AI agents to reason over consistently, backed by documentation, tests, and lineage
- Reconcile metrics with NetSuite and the Finance close so the numbers tie out

Requirements Translation
- Translate business questions from Sales, Finance, Fund Administration, and leadership into technical requirements for Data Engineering, covering source data, grain, freshness, and quality
- Work with our Lead Business Systems Analyst and Integrations Engineers so the commercial model and source data are reportable from the start

AI-assisted reporting & measurement
- Build an AI-assisted reporting layer on top of governed semantic definitions, so questions asked through tools like Claude return certified answers
- Build self-serve dashboards for revenue, operations, and leadership stakeholders
- Stand up measurement for the Lead-to-Cash program so monthly progress is shown in numbers rather than status updates

Governance & controls
- Apply access controls, data classification, and change management suited to financial and customer data in SOX, SOC 1, and SOC 2 scope
