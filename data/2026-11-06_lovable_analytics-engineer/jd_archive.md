Analytics Engineer

TL;DR - We’re looking for analytics engineers to own our warehouse’s semantic and modeling layer, turning raw data into well-defined, trustworthy datasets and metrics. Our internal users are primarily agents: every human queries data through their agent. You care deeply about analytical consistency, meaningful metrics, and building foundations that agents can reliably interpret and use to answer business questions.

What we are looking for:

- Expertise with SQL, dbt, SQLMesh or similar tools (data modeling, testing, macros, docs)

- Experience with data warehousing concepts, cloud warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks) and BI tools (Looker, Tableau, Power BI, Hex, Metabase, etc)

- Understanding of dimensional modeling, data contracts, and metrics/semantical layers

- Familiarity with modern ELT and orchestration workflows (Airflow, Dagster, Prefect, etc)

- Strong business acumen and ability to translate domain logic into scalable data structures

What you will do:

- Build and maintain modular, tested, and version-controlled data models that agents can reliably discover, interpret, and query

- Partner with domain teams to translate business logic into reusable models and metrics with explicit definitions and clear context

- Own the semantic layer, defining consistent metrics and data contracts across domains so agents answer business questions accurately

- Collaborate with Data Platform Engineers to optimize query performance and warehouse cost for agent-driven analytics

- Automate documentation, lineage, and governance so agents can understand data meaning, provenance, and usage constraints

- Establish standards for analytics development, data modeling, and naming conventions that make agent-mediated self-service analytics reliable for everyone
