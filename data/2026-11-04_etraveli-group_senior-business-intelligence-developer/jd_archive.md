Senior Business Intelligence Developer

Etraveli Group is a leading global flight technology provider, specializing in flight sales and offering flight content delivery and fintech products. We are here to solve complexity, by connecting millions of flights and travelers across the globe, from search and selection to trip and beyond.

We partner with major global platforms such as Booking.com, Google Flights, Skyscanner, and Kayak, providing seamless flight booking and related services. Our B2B portfolio includes Precision, our specialist in travel fintech; Tripstack, our B2B Flights as a Service Provider and world leader in virtual interlining, serving as the gateway to Etraveli Group’s flight content and fulfillment services; Wenrix, the AI Infrastructure for profitable growth in air; and Flygresor.se, Sweden’s leading flight comparison site. We also operate our own online travel agency brands including Gotogate, Mytrip, and Flightnetwork.

Every day we strive to make the world smaller for our customers and bigger for our people. Our diverse team of more than 3200 passionate professionals is what makes us the industry’s tech wonder and the best in the world at what we do.

Major offices in Sweden (HQ), Greece, India, Canada, Israel, Poland, UK, and Uruguay.

Job Responsibilities

- To strengthen our capabilities in analysis and data visualization, Etraveli Group is now recruiting two experienced Business Intelligence Developers to join the BI team

- Your main task will be to create and maintain ETL flows and BI applications in QlikSense and Looker.

- Your role will require both a deep understanding of the business needs and the skill set to acquire, handle and analyze the relevant data. You will have a close direct connection and collaboration with other groups and departments acting as stakeholders for the applications you develop.

- Our BI team is split across the offices in Athens, Uppsala, Stockholm & India and cater to the whole organization.

- The present role will be specialized towards the Corporate Development department. Our processes and technical setup are mature, but constantly changing and adapting with the business.

- The data you will be using is mostly structured in databases maintained by the IT-department and we use several different database technologies. Less structured data will also occur frequently.

- Working at Etraveli means working at a company with grand plans and great expectations. You will have awesome colleagues together with whom you will solve complex problems in a fast moving business.

Job Requirements

- Development and maintenance of BI applications in Qlik and Looker (e.g. dashboards, dynamic reports, automatic send outs).

- Development of ETL processes handling both structured and unstructured data

- Information engineering and data modeling

- Working closely and directly with business stakeholders Specification:

- 6+ years of hands on BI application development experience in Qlik or Looker.

- 3+ years of hands on experience in ETL development and SQL

- A university degree in a quantitative field (physics, economics, statistics, engineering or similar).

- A passion for both business and numbers

- Excellent communication skills

- Experience of version control using git

- Personality-wise, we believe that you are curious, methodical, ambit
