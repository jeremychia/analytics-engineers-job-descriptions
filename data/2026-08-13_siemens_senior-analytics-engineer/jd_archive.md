We are looking for: Senior Analytics Engineer

You'll break new ground by:

- Build outstanding, scalable data models by extracting and transforming data from various sources using our modern ingestion tools like SnapLogic and Confluent Cloud.
- Shape and implement the data architecture, including standards for data models, metadata, and security, ensuring adherence to the overall architectural vision.
- Drive protocols in a DataOps environment, participating in code reviews, retrospectives, and encouraging a culture of knowledge sharing and continuous improvement.
- Implement effective and sophisticated database solutions in Snowflake to store, retrieve, and transform company data for analytical applications.
- Produce meaningful metrics and logs to debug, trace, and supervise data pipelines and models, ensuring high data quality and reliability.
- Be a valued technical leader, mentor, collaborator, and champion of protocols within an autonomous, cross-functional agile team.
- Act as a key consultant and enabler for data teams across Siemens, helping them tackle complex data modeling and data warehousing challenges.

You're excited to build on your existing expertise, including:

- A university degree in IT, Engineering, Economics, or a related quantitative field.
- Extensive experience in data engineering and data warehousing, with a consistent record of building and maintaining complex data models.
- Expert-level programming skills in SQL and deep proficiency with dbt.
- Strong experience working with cloud data warehouses, particularly Snowflake.
- Familiarity with modern data ingestion frameworks and tools such as SnapLogic or Confluent Cloud.
- Proficiency in at least one general-purpose programming language, preferably Python.
- Proven experience with major Cloud technologies (AWS, GCP, Azure) and working in agile software development environments.
- A problem-solving and data-first approach, with the ability to create real business value.
- Excellent command of English is required and German is an added advantage.
