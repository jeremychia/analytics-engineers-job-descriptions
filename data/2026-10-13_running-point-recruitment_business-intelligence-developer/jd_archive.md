Business Intelligence Developer

Location: Devon

Working pattern: Three days in the office and two days from home

An established business is seeking a Business Intelligence Developer to help develop its data capabilities and make reliable, meaningful information central to business decisions.

This is a varied role covering the full data process, from integrating information across different systems and developing data pipelines to building models, analysing performance and delivering Power BI reporting.

The existing environment uses Python, Microsoft SQL Server and Power BI. As the business’s requirements grow, the successful candidate will help assess this setup, recommend improvements and contribute to decisions about future platforms and technologies.

Working within a team of two and reporting to the Business Information Manager, you will combine practical development with the opportunity to influence how the business collects, manages and uses data. This work will also support the foundations for its wider AI ambitions.

The role

- Develop, maintain and improve data pipelines using Python and SQL Server, bringing together information from databases, files and APIs.
- Build reliable data transformations and models that support reporting, analysis and wider business requirements.
- Create and maintain Power BI dashboards, semantic models and DAX calculations that provide clear, actionable information.
- Work directly with stakeholders to understand their questions and translate business needs into practical data solutions.
- Investigate discrepancies, improve data quality and introduce appropriate validation, logging and monitoring.
- Improve the performance, reliability and maintainability of existing pipelines, models and reports.
- Contribute to consistent definitions of business measures and reporting standards.
- Help strengthen development practices, including source control, testing, documentation, peer review and structured deployment.
- Evaluate potential platforms and approaches, explaining their strengths and limitations in relation to cost, scalability, maintenance and business value.

The experience required

- Commercial experience in business intelligence, data engineering, analytics engineering or a closely related development role.
- Strong SQL skills, including complex queries, data transformations and performance optimisation.
- Practical Python experience for data processing, integration, automation or pipeline development.
- Experience developing and troubleshooting ETL or ELT processes.
- Strong Power BI experience across data modelling, DAX, dashboard development and performance optimisation.
- An understanding of data warehousing and dimensional modelling.
- Experience integrating data from multiple sources and checking its accuracy and consistency.
- The ability to gather requirements, investigate business questions and communicate findings to colleagues with different levels of technical understanding.

Experience within an established data environment is particularly valuable. This could include exposure to Microsoft Fabric, Snowflake, Databricks, Azure, AWS or similar technologies. No single platform background is preferred; the ability to explain different approaches and apply that knowledge practically is what matters.

Who this would suit

This role would suit a developer with solid commercial experience who enjoys working across different parts of the data process and wants greater involvement in technical decisions.

You should be comfortable sharing recommendations, discussing alternatives and working collaboratively to find solutions that meet the business’s needs. Previous team management experience is not required.

There is scope to shape the balance of responsibilities around your strengths while contributing across the wider data environment.

The opportunity

You will join at an early stage of the business’s data development, with the opportunity to influence platform choices, introduce better ways of working and see the impact of your contribution across the organisation.
