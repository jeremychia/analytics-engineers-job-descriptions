The roleWe are looking for a Lead Analytics Engineer to help build and scale a modern data platform. This role sits at the intersection of data engineering, analytics modeling, and data governance. You will design and orchestrate data workflows, develop curated and serving-layer models, define KPI logic, and establish quality and governance standards that enable trusted analytics and downstream AI/ML use cases.
What you will do- Design, build, and maintain orchestration workflows using Airflow.
- Develop and optimize curated and serving-layer models using dbt.
- Implement data transformations such as cleaning, deduplication, patterns, and schema harmonization.
- Build dimensional models, marts, and KPI logic for reporting and advanced analytics.
- Define and maintain KPI definitions, business glossary standards, and semantic consistency across data products.
- Configure and optimize datasets in BigQuery for analytical workloads.
- Implement data quality and governance controls, including automated tests, lineage validation, and metadata standards.
- Establish and improve end-to-end testing frameworks for pipelines and transformations.
- Partner with upstream and downstream teams to ensure reliable, well-documented, high-quality data delivery.
- Support BI, analytics, and data science teams with trusted datasets and reusable data assets.

What you bring- Strong experience with SQL and modern analytical data platforms, especially BigQuery.
- Hands-on experience with dbt, including models, tests, macros, documentation, and layered transformations.
- Experience building and operating workflows in Airflow or similar orchestration tools.
- Strong Python skills for data processing, pipeline logic, testing, and automation.
- Solid understanding of dimensional modeling, KPI design and data transformation patterns.
- Experience implementing data quality, governance, and metadata practices in production environments.
- Familiarity with Git-based development workflows and CI/CD practices.
- Ability to work across engineering, analytics, and business teams to translate requirements into scalable data products.

Nice to have- Experience with real-time or near-real-time analytics environments.
- Exposure to semantic modeling and business glossary design.
- Familiarity with cloud-based analytics tooling in GCP.
- Experience supporting BI tools such as Power BI, Tableau, or similar platforms.
- Exposure to advanced analytics or ML-enablement use cases.
