At Catalyst, People are the heartbeat of our company. We believe that good quality people will have a positive impact on our business. We are looking for a Data Analytics Engineer to join our growing team. If you are passionate about being part of the team, building some of the most critical products, and Working alongside teams in the industry across product, engineering, and design, Let’s join us!

Job Description

- Build and maintain reliable data pipelines, data models, and analytical datasets to support business and product needs.

- Transform and integrate data from multiple sources using SQL, dbt, Snowflake, and ETL/ELT tools.

- Develop and maintain dashboards, reports, and data solutions using Metabase.

- Work closely with Product, Business, Finance, and Operations teams to translate business problems into data-driven solutions.

- Ensure data accuracy, quality, consistency, and availability across critical business metrics.

- Continuously improve our data platform, automation, and analytics processes to make data more scalable and accessible.

Requirements

- Minimum 2 years of experience in Data Analytics Engineering, Data Engineering, BI Engineering, or a related role.

- Strong SQL and data modeling skills.

- Experience using dbt and cloud data warehouses such as Snowflake or BigQuery.

- Experience building ETL/ELT pipelines and working with orchestration tools such as Prefect or Airbyte.

- Experience with BI tools such as Metabase, Looker, Tableau, or Power BI.

- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with a business-oriented mindset.
