Data & BI Engineer

Position Summary

Looking for a highly technical and business-focused Data & BI Engineer to manage, develop, and enhance the organization's enterprise data, reporting, analytics, and automation capabilities.

This role is responsible for owning the end-to-end data lifecycle, from extracting information from source systems and APIs to processing and storing data in MySQL, developing Power BI semantic models and dashboards, and delivering actionable insights to business stakeholders.

The Data & BI Engineer will support multiple business functions, including Finance, Human Resources, Talent Acquisition, Marketing, Operations, Learning & Development, and Shared Services. The position combines data engineering, database development, business intelligence, Python automation, and analytics consulting within client's Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365 environment.

The successful candidate will work directly with business stakeholders to understand operational needs, define meaningful KPIs, automate manual processes, improve data quality, and build scalable solutions that support informed decision-making.
This is an end-to-end engineering role, not simply a report development position. The ideal candidate will own the journey from source-system integration through data processing, database management, analytics development, application support, production operations, and business insight. The role also includes supporting Linux-based application environments, automated data processing workloads, and business-critical analytics platforms.

Key Responsibilities

Data Engineering & Integration

• Design, develop, and maintain automated ETL/ELT processes to extract, transform, validate, and deliver data from multiple source systems.
• Build and maintain integrations between business applications, APIs, databases, and enterprise reporting platforms.
• Develop scalable and reusable data pipelines using Python, SQL, and appropriate integration technologies.

• Monitor data processing workflows and troubleshoot pipeline failures, integration issues, and data discrepancies.
