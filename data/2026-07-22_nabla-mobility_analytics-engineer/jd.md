NABLA Mobility Inc. is a startup driving operational optimization in the aviation company through the development and provision of strategic network solutions powered by AI and machine learning (ML).

Despite advances in technology, decision-making in the aviation sector still heavily relies on manual processes and expert judgment. As a result, issues such as turbulence, traffic congestion, and delays continue to impact flight safety, operational efficiency, and environmental sustainability.
We address these uncertainties by conducting advanced analyses of real-time and historical flight operation data.

Our flight optimization software, "Weave", functions as a decision-support tool that quantitatively evaluates trade-offs and proposes optimal solutions for strategic flight changes. It enables airlines to reduce fuel consumption while enhancing safety.

In addition, our software "OBST" semi-automates obstacle data processing. Based on NOTAMs and AIP information around airports, OBST quickly and easily evaluates the impact of obstacles and automates the takeoff performance calculation process. It is designed to accommodate the specific operational conditions of each airline.

We also offer a flight disruption forecasting solution, which detects potential operational irregularities in advance and supports timely, effective responses.
As a pioneer in the industry, NABLA Mobility integrates cutting-edge academic theories, advanced technologies, and deep operational know-how to lead the future of global aviation.
