About Strathclyde Partnership for Transport
Strathclyde Partnership for Transport (SPT) is the largest of Scotland’s seven Regional Transport Partnerships, serving a population of more than 2.2 million people across 12 local authority areas in the West of Scotland.
SPT’s role is to bring together local authorities and key regional and national partners to plan and deliver an integrated, sustainable transport system that supports inclusive economic growth, tackles inequality, and improves quality of life. At the heart of this is a clear ambition: to deliver a world-class, low-carbon transport system that meets the needs of people and communities now and in the future.
SPT sits at the centre of regional transport planning, combining strategic insight, robust analysis, and practical delivery. The organisation plans and supports transport across all modes, working closely with councils, Transport Scotland, the Scottish Government, operators, and communities to shape how transport services evolve over time.
Alongside its strategic planning role, SPT delivers and supports a wide range of transport activity across the region. This includes investment in bus services, infrastructure and subway, management of major transport programmes, and the delivery of passenger-focused improvements that help keep communities connected.
SPT is now entering a period of significant transformation. Completion of the Subway Modernisation project, major programmes such as Bus Reform and Clyde Metro represent some of the most ambitious transport initiatives ever undertaken in the West of Scotland.
Together, they will reshape how public transport is planned, governed, and delivered, placing passengers, communities and long-term sustainability at the heart of decision-making.
This is an organisation with a strong public service ethos, a collaborative culture and a growing national profile. For professionals who want to work at scale, influence long-term outcomes and help deliver meaningful change, SPT offers a uniquely compelling environment.
You can read more at our SPT website.
About this role:
We are seeking an Analytics Engineer to join our Business Intelligence team and play a key role in transforming how data is managed, modelled and used across SPT. As we deliver our Data Strategy and establish a modern cloud-based data platform, you will help build trusted, scalable data products that support decision-making across the organisation.
Working closely with business stakeholders, analysts and technical teams, you will develop and maintain data pipelines, data models and semantic layers that turn complex data into reliable, accessible information. You will help establish modern engineering practices, improve data quality and governance, and support the delivery of strategic initiatives that will shape the future of transport across the west of Scotland.
About You
You will have experience working with modern data platforms and a strong understanding of data modelling, transformation and integration principles. You’ll be comfortable developing data pipelines and analytical solutions using tools such as SQL and cloud-based technologies, with an appreciation of data governance, quality and security requirements.
We are looking for someone who combines strong analytical thinking with a collaborative approach. You will be able to work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders, translating business requirements into robust data solutions.
Most importantly, you’ll be motivated by the opportunity to help establish a data-driven culture and contribute to some of Scotland’s most significant and transformative public transport programmes.
Fulltime hours will be 35 hours per week Monday to Friday. However, you may be required to undertake work outwith this norm to meet the responsibilities of the post.

Job Purpose:
Support the Head of Business Intelligence to deliver SPT’s strategic aims with regard to
information services by building and running the data platform that SPT’s reporting and
analysis depends on: automated ingestion from source systems, modelled and tested
data, and the semantic layer that business intelligence is built on.
Work closely with senior managers across SPT to develop and maintain effective data
analytics which support decision making and effective business operations across SPT.
Ensure data is managed effectively to enhance organisational intelligence and
understanding with reliable, timely and meaningful reporting across all business areas.
Main Responsibilities and Duties:
1. Design, build and maintain automated data pipelines from SPT’s operational and
corporate systems into the data platform, replacing manual extract processes.
2. Model data into documented, tested, reusable structures that support consistent
reporting across the organisation.
3. Build and maintain the semantic models that Power BI reporting consumes, ensuring
definitions are consistent and performance is fit for purpose.
4. Implement automated data quality testing, monitoring and alerting, so that failures
are detected before users encounter them.
5. Establish and maintain version control, deployment processes and environments
appropriate to a small team.
6. Document data lineage, definitions and known limitations to support the
organisation’s data governance framework.
7. Work with source system owners and suppliers to specify and agree the data SPT
receives, including from third parties.
8. Support the Head of Business Intelligence in developing analytical capability across
services, including advising colleagues building their own reporting.
9. Contribute to the technical elements of platform selection, sizing and cost
management.
