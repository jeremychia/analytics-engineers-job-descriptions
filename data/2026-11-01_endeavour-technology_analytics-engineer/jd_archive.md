Company Overview:

Endeavour Technology Ltd. has its headquarters in Dublin, Ireland. Endeavour is a company that captures the IoT cellular connectivity experience. Our ground-breaking product nSpire is a SaaS platform that has collectors on the edge (on devices) and provides real value-based connectivity insights to our customers.

Our vision is to improve the quality of connectivity with a specific focus on Business Critical, Mission Critical and Life Critical connectivity. With deep IoT, telecoms, analytics, and information technology experience, we are expanding our global presence to support customers around the world as the IoT and 5G markets continue to grow.

Endeavour Technology turns data from connected devices into actionable intelligence. Our nSpire platform measures real-world connectivity quality from the device’s perspective, helping leading companies in automotive, in-flight connectivity, and telecoms understand and improve the experience their customers get.

Software agents running on devices collect large, rich data sets that power guided analytics workflows used by hundreds of business users. These workflows are built on SQL, cloud databases, and Tableau – and our team uses AI tools such as Claude every day to work faster, explore problems more thoroughly, and deliver stronger results.

Some of our successful solutions include Connected Cars (Auto OEMS), and Industrial Internet of things (IoT) including 5G Enterprise/MEC, NPN Networks.

nSpire ensures that customers have the confidence and assurance that the connectivity delivered is always fit for purpose and enables IoT customers to utilise the QoS data in their IoT products and services.

Role Description:

We’re looking for an experienced Analytics Engineer who can take a product brief or use case and independently turn it into a robust, scalable analytics solution, with Tableau at the core of what you deliver.

AI is part of how we work. You’ll use tools like Claude as a thinking partner – to shape your approach to a problem, speed up SQL and dashboard development, check your logic, and sharpen how insights are communicated. Your analytical judgement stays firmly in charge; AI helps you get to the best answer faster.

You’ll sit in our value delivery group and work closely with the CTO, Analytics and Product teams to bring real-time, data-driven solutions to life for our clients.
