Data Analytics Engineer | Power BI, SQL & Python
TBS - Tech & Beyond Solutions Lda · Matosinhos, Porto, Portugal

Location: Matosinhos, Porto, Portugal

Work Model: Hybrid — at least 1 day per week in our Matosinhos office

Job Type: Full-time
About the Role
Tech and Beyond Solutions is looking for a technically skilled Data Analytics Engineer to join our Data Team, with a strong focus on Business Intelligence, data visualisation and reporting.

In this role, you will develop and maintain Power BI dashboards, write and optimise SQL queries, and transform business data into actionable insights. You will also use Python to automate tasks and support data processing, helping improve the efficiency, accuracy and reliability of our data solutions.

Working closely with business stakeholders, Data Analysts and development teams, you will contribute to the continuous improvement of our reporting tools and data ecosystem.

We are looking for someone with strong analytical skills, a hands-on mindset and the ability to take ownership of technical challenges.

This is a hybrid position requiring at least one day per week in our office in Matosinhos, Portugal.
Key Responsibilities
- Power BI Development: Build, maintain and enhance interactive dashboards and reports to monitor business performance and support data-driven decision-making.
- SQL & Query Optimisation: Develop, maintain and optimise SQL/T-SQL queries, ensuring data accuracy, reliability and performance.
- Data Analysis & Visualisation: Translate business requirements into meaningful metrics, reports and visualisations that provide actionable insights.
- Python & Automation: Independently develop and maintain Python scripts to automate repetitive tasks, process data and improve existing workflows.
- Data Management: Support data preparation, transformation, validation and quality checks to ensure consistent and reliable reporting.
- BI Solutions & Pipelines: Maintain and improve existing BI solutions and contribute to ETL/ELT workflows where required.
- Collaboration: Work with business users, Data Analysts and product development teams to understand requirements and deliver effective data solutions.
- Code Review & Continuous Improvement: Review SQL scripts, identify potential issues and contribute to better coding practices and knowledge sharing.

- At least 2 years of relevant professional experience in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, BI Development or a related field.
- Strong hands-on experience with Microsoft Power BI, including dashboard and report development.
- Strong proficiency in SQL Server and SQL/T-SQL, including complex queries, data manipulation and query optimisation.
- Practical experience with Python, including the ability to develop and maintain scripts independently for data processing or automation.
- Good understanding of data modelling, data transformation and data quality principles.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail.
- Good command of English (B2 level or above).
- Ability to work independently, take ownership of tasks and collaborate effectively with a multidisciplinary team.
- Availability to work in a hybrid model, with at least one day per week at our Matosinhos office.
