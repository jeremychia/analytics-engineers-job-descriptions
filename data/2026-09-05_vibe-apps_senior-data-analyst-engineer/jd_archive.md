Role overview

Senior Data Analyst/Engineer
Full-time
Ho Chi Minh, Viet Nam
Job description
- Own analytics and reporting for VAICentral (Vibe Apps AI Central): Unified AI & Data platform that connects structured ERP/DWH data with unstructured enterprise knowledge (documents, SOPs, procedures).
- Design, build, and maintain DataMarts and trusted metrics on ClickHouse (and related analytics stores) so business users and leadership get reliable, real-time insights.
- Partner with Engineering on ELT/CDC pipelines (e.g. Airbyte, Kafka, Airflow) and dbt models—from source systems (SAP, CRM, core DB) to curated marts with clear data lineage.
- Define KPIs and dashboards for enterprise use cases (Finance, Revenue, Operations, Compliance) and embed them via Apache Superset with appropriate row-level security (RLS).
- Support Natural Language Query (NLQ) and GraphRAG analytics quality: validate that Vietnamese business questions map correctly to SQL/Cypher results, measure answer accuracy, and reduce hallucination risk for reporting use cases.
- Analyze AI platform signals (query volume, latency, retrieval quality, token/cost via tracing tools such as Langfuse) and turn them into product and operations recommendations.
- Collaborate with Product, AI Engineering, and customer delivery teams on industry scenarios (e.g. pharmaceuticals, banking & finance, manufacturing & supply chain) to convert domain questions into measurable metrics and reusable reporting patterns.
- Establish data quality checks, metric definitions, documentation, and self-serve analytics practices across Pool 2 (Analytics Storage) and related VAICentral modules.
Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Data Science, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
- Minimum 2 years of experience as a Data Analyst / Analytics Engineer (or similar), with senior-level ownership of DWH or product analytics for enterprise platforms.
- Strong SQL skills and hands-on experience with analytical databases/warehouses—especially columnar OLAP such as ClickHouse (or equivalent: BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift).
- Practical experience with dimensional modeling / DataMart design and transformation tools (dbt strongly preferred).
- Experience building and governing BI dashboards (Apache Superset preferred; Power BI / Tableau / Looker also acceptable), including access control or RLS concepts.
- Familiarity with ELT/CDC and orchestration concepts (Airbyte, Kafka, Airflow, or similar) and ability to partner with engineers on pipeline correctness and lineage.
- Comfort working at the intersection of structured analytics and AI-assisted retrieval (RAG / knowledge bases); ability to evaluate NLQ or AI-generated analytical answers against ground-truth data is a plus.
- Strong stakeholder communication: translate business questions into metrics, and present clear insights to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- English reading/writing for technical documentation; Vietnamese communication for business stakeholder work is an advantage.
Benefits
- Competitive salary commensurate with skills and experience.
- Recognition and bonuses based on performance and work efficiency on a quarterly/annual basis and according to company regulations. Or can share by ratio profit on the Project (if needed).
- Full benefits according to Vietnamese Labor Law (Social Insurance, Health Insurance, Unemployment Insurance).
- Quarterly team building activities and annual company trips.
