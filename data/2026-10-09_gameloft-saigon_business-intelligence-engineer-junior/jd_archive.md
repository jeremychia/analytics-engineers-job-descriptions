Business Intelligence Engineer (Junior)
Gameloft Saigon
Ho Chi Minh City, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON​

What you will focus on

As a Business Intelligence Engineer, you’ll own the telemetry and reporting foundation that the studio relies on to make data-driven decisions. You’ll work at the intersection of raw event data, analytics, and business reporting - making sure our data is clean, consistent, and trustworthy.

The key challenge is to build a scalable tracking architecture while maintaining high data quality and reliable dashboards across the studio.

What it means on a daily basis​

- Own and improve the telemetry structure by defining events, IDs, parameters, and tracking standards across features.

- Validate and monitor telemetry data in BigQuery, identifying and resolving data quality issues before they impact analysis.

- Write, review, and optimize SQL queries to ensure KPIs and business metrics are calculated accurately.

- Maintain and improve dashboards across Metabase and Looker, ensuring consistent reporting and resolving issues caused by data or schema changes.

- Automate data processing and integrate data from multiple sources using Python and/or Node.js.

Who you would be working with​

- Data & BI teams – working together to maintain data quality, reporting standards, and a unified analytics workflow.

- Game/Product teams – collaborating on telemetry requirements and tracking implementation across features.

- Technical teams – coordinating on data architecture, schemas, integrations, and technical changes that may impact reporting.

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED​

- SQL skills with hands-on experience in Google BigQuery.

- High proficiency in Metabase and Looker, or comparable BI and visualization tools.

- Knowledge of database architecture and data modeling, including schema design, data warehousing, and normalization/denormalization.

- Proficiency in Python and/or Node.js for scripting, automation, and data processing.

- Experience collecting data from multiple sources and cleaning, transforming, and integrating it into a unified database.

- Strong understanding of data quality, telemetry/tracking, and dashboard governance.

WHO YOU ARE

- Highly detail-oriented and genuinely care about data accuracy and consistency.

- Structured and systematic, with strong documentation habits.

- Proactive in identifying data issues and solving them before they impact stakeholders.

- Strong ownership mindset - you take responsibility for the quality and reliability of the systems you manage.

- A clear communicator who can collaborate effectively with technical and non-technical teams.
