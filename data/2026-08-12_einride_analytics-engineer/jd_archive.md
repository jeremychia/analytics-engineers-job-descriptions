We are looking for an enthusiastic, creative Analytics Engineer to join our growing Analytics Platform team. You won't just be writing code; you will be a key player in shaping our data architecture, championing data literacy, and empowering the transport industry to go electric through simple, beautiful, and highly effective data solutions.

You Will

- Anticipate business needs: Work proactively with stakeholders to not just understand, but predict their future needs in terms of datasets and dashboards.
- Scale our data foundations: Design robust data models and build reliable pipelines alongside our engineering teams.
- Drive business insights: Define and implement key metrics to assess business performance and enhance our analytics-driven products.

We Expect You To Have

- Passion for high data quality and experience scaling analytics workflows.

- A proven ability to thrive in both collaborative team settings and independent work.

Ideally, You Also Bring

- Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (we use Terraform).
- Experience with a programming language for data analysis (e.g., Python, R).
- A strong eye for UI/UX design in dashboards and data visualization.
- Solid business acumen, financial literacy, and an understanding of predictive modeling.
