Job Description

- Design, develop, and maintain scalable data models that support business intelligence, analytics, and reporting needs.
- Bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams by translating business requirements into reliable and actionable data solutions.
- Build, optimize, and maintain ETL/ELT pipelines to transform raw data into trusted analytical datasets.
- Develop and maintain data warehouse and data lake solutions across cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform).
- Implement dimensional and semantic data models to enable self-service analytics and improve data accessibility.
- Ensure data quality, consistency, and governance through testing, validation, monitoring, and documentation practices.
- Collaborate with Data Engineers, Data Scientists, Business Analysts, and Product teams to deliver high-quality data products.
- Optimize SQL queries, data transformations, and reporting layers to improve performance and scalability.
- Support the development of analytical solutions, dashboards, KPIs, and business metrics.
- Contribute to the adoption of data best practices, standards, and governance frameworks.
- Create and maintain technical documentation, data lineage, and knowledge-sharing materials.
- Participate in code reviews, version control processes, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience working in Analytics Engineering, Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, or related data-focused roles.
- Strong proficiency in SQL and data modeling concepts.
- Experience designing and implementing ETL/ELT pipelines and data transformation workflows.
- Solid understanding of dimensional modeling techniques (Star Schema, Snowflake Schema, Data Vault, or similar).
- Experience working with cloud-based data platforms (AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform).
- Familiarity with modern data warehousing architectures and analytical ecosystems.
- Experience using version control systems such as Git.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to understand complex business requirements.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
