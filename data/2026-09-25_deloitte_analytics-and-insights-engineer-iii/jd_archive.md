Work you'll do

As an Analytics and Insights Engineer III on the Advertising, Marketing & Commerce team, you will be responsible for…

Designing and executing analyses that address client business questions across paid, owned, and earned channels

Developing measurement frameworks, learning agendas, data strategies, and performance reporting approaches for marketing, advertising, and commerce programs

Analyzing large data sets to uncover insights and deliver recommendations using tools such as Google Analytics, DV360, Ads Data Hub, Adobe Analytics, Salesforce, or Salesforce Marketing Cloud

Creating dashboards and visualizations using Tableau, Datorama, Data Studio, Looker, or similar tools for executive and client audiences

Supporting measurement plans, testing plans, tagging strategies, modeling strategies, and experimentation deployment, including A/B testing

Ability to work independently and collaborate as part of a team

Qualifications

Required:

4+ years of experience managing digital marketing or advertising analytics, including marketing mix modeling or attribution solutions for large-scale clients

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent professional experience

Experience designing analyses to answer business questions across paid, owned, and earned channels

Experience creating dashboards or visualizations using Tableau, Datorama, Data Studio, Looker, or similar tools

Ability to travel 30%, on average, based on the work you do and the clients and industries/sectors you serve.

Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship, now or at any time in the future.

Preferred:

Experience defining analytics strategies, modeling strategies, tagging strategies, and measurement or testing plans

Experience applying analytical methods to customer data sets and digital campaign performance

Experience using Excel, Structured Query Language, Tableau, or other data analysis tools

Experience with digital analytics platforms and tag management solutions

Experience managing project timelines and deliverables across multiple workstreams

Experience presenting insights to executive-level or client-facing audiences
