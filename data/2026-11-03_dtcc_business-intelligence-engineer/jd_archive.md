The Impact You Will Have In This Role

The Business Intelligence Associate, Snowflake Developer will be responsible for building data pipelines using their deep knowledge of Talend, SQL and Data Analysis on the bespoke Snowflake data warehouse for Enterprise Intelligence.

Your Primary Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain data models to support analytics and reporting requirements.
- Write and optimize SQL queries, stored procedures, and data transformations within Snowflake.
- Support the building and maintenance of data pipelines and ELT/ETL workflows on the Snowflake platform.
- Assist in implementing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins for automated testing and deployment.
- Manage code changes and collaborate with the team through Bitbucket, following established branching strategies and code review practices.
- Work with analysts and product teams to understand business requirements and translate them into data solutions.
- Follow data quality standards and contribute to documentation and governance practices.
- Participate in code reviews, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement initiatives.
- Familiarity with dbt, Airflow, or similar data transformation/orchestration tools. (Preferable)
- Experience with Power BI — building dashboards, datasets, DAX measures, and row-level security. (Preferable)
- Background in reporting and analytics — creating executive-level reports and self-service reporting solutions. (Preferable)
