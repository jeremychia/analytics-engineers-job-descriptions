Role Overview:

We are looking for a Microsoft Fabric Analytics Engineer to integrate our teams and contribute to a strategic data and analytics transformation project in the financial services sector.

You will support the modernisation of a large-scale corporate information platform, redesigning its reporting capabilities and helping migrate its data foundations to Microsoft Fabric. The role combines advanced Power BI reporting, semantic modelling and data engineering, working with high-volume financial, risk and profitability data.

What you will do:

- Redesign and enhance Power BI dashboards as part of a broader migration to Microsoft Fabric;

- Build reporting-oriented data models and semantic models for complex financial and corporate information;

- Work with curated data from the Gold layer of a Medallion Architecture, enabling more complete and detailed reporting;

- Support the implementation of foundational data structures in Microsoft Fabric, including Lakehouse, Warehouse, pipelines and Direct Lake;

- Collaborate with business and functional teams to interpret and present financial information, including balance sheets, consolidated and adjusted accounts, profitability indicators and risk data;

- Develop and maintain data integration processes, ensuring data quality, consistency and traceability;

- Define and implement mechanisms for data versioning, historical retention and reprocessing;

- Design scalable dimensional models using Star Schema and Snowflake Schema, applying the most appropriate approach to each reporting scenario;

- Optimise the performance of reports, semantic models, queries and DAX calculations;

- Perform structured troubleshooting to distinguish and resolve issues affecting data sources, queries, models, calculations or report visuals;

- Contribute to technical and functional documentation and promote reporting and data modelling best practices.

What you are like:

- Experienced in delivering Microsoft Fabric solutions in real project environments;

- Proficient in Fabric Lakehouse, Warehouse, data pipelines and Direct Lake;

- Strong background in Data Engineering, including data integration, Medallion Architecture, versioning and reprocessing strategies;

- Advanced knowledge of Power BI, including semantic models, DAX and Bookmarks;

- Able to evaluate and apply Import, DirectQuery and Direct Lake according to performance, governance and reporting requirements;

- Proficient in dimensional data modelling, including Star Schema and Snowflake Schema, and knowledgeable about their respective trade-offs in Power BI;

- Experienced in designing reporting solutions for large datasets containing millions of records;

- Skilled in diagnosing and optimising dashboard performance across the data source, query, semantic model, DAX and visual layers;

- Experienced in building data models, defining relationships and structuring facts and dimensions for analytical consumption;

- Comfortable working with technical and business stakeholders to translate complex information into clear and effective dashboards;

- Previous experience in the banking or financial services sector is valued;

- Knowledge of accounting and financial statements, including balance sheets, consolidation, profitability, rating and credit risk, is valued;

- Microsoft certifications such as DP-600: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate or DP-700: Fabric Data Engineer Associate are valued;

- Autonomous, rigorous and committed to data quality;

- Interested in understanding the functional and business context behind the information presented;

- Strong analytical, communication and structured problem-solving skills.
