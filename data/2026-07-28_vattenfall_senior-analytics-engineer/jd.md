Senior Analytics Engineer

Are you driven by turning complex data into clear insights that empower real business impact? As a Senior Analytics Engineer, you play a key role in strengthening our data foundation and enabling teams across BtC NL to make better, faster decisions. Working at the heart of our Data & Analytics organisation, you collaborate with Analytics Translators, Data Scientists and IT engineers to build high-quality datasets, dashboards and analytics products. If you enjoy combining strong technical skills with stakeholder collaboration — and you're ready to help elevate our data capabilities — then this role is an exciting next step.

In this role, you translate stakeholder needs into high-quality datasets and analytics products. You take a central role in improving data architecture and ensuring high standards of data quality through your involvement in the Data Foundation Authority. You are part of a domain team consisting of Analytics Translators, Analytics Engineers and Data Scientists, supported by Data & ML Engineers from IT. You guide junior colleagues and work closely with business stakeholders to ensure our data solutions are adopted and used in daily decision-making.

Your responsibilities
- Model data in Snowflake and develop scalable data pipelines to improve time-to-market.
- Build curated datasets, dashboards and analytical assets — including clear documentation and guidance for end-users.
- Contribute to continuous improvement of data architecture, platform patterns and quality standards (Snowflake/Azure).
- Support and mentor junior team members in best practices and development.
- Work with stakeholders across marketing, service and propositions to ensure data products drive value.

Location: Amsterdam

Qualifications

Who are you?
You are a solution-oriented team player who communicates clearly with both technical and non-technical colleagues. You feel at home in an environment where collaboration, ownership and continuous improvement are key.

You bring:
- 5+ years of experience in (Analytics) Engineering;
- University degree with a technical or data focus;
- Strong SQL and data modeling skills; experience with Snowflake and cloud platforms (Azure);
- Experience with CI/CD, DevOps, git and code reviews;
- Experience with data visualization tools;
- An interest in the energy transition and in Vattenfall's purpose is considered important;
- Strong communication skills and the ability to translate data into insights that stakeholders can act on.
