About the role

Southern Cross is guided by purpose, powered by people and technology. We're here to help almost one million members across Aotearoa New Zealand live well for longer - work with real scale, real responsibility, and a real opportunity to make a meaningful difference.

As we grow to be about assurance as well as insurance - a more proactive partner in our members' health - we're building a new Data & AI function from the ground up. We're looking for a Senior Analytics Engineer to build the trusted, well-modelled data that everything else in the function depends on.

This is a senior technical role for someone who wants to shape how data flows through an organisation: building the models, setting the standards, and turning raw data into datasets people can trust and act on.

About the role

Reporting into the Innovation & Advanced Analytics Lead, you'll build and maintain the dbt models that transform raw data into the analytics-ready datasets the business runs on - the layer between the data warehouse and the people who use it every day.

You'll work close to the data and close to the business: refining metric definitions with analysts and stakeholders, designing marts and semantic layers for self-service reporting, data products, agentic agents and ensuring decision-makers can confidently rely on consistent, well-governed data.

This function is still being built, so you'll help shape the standards the team works to as it grows - testing, documentation, lineage and code review.

What you'll do

•    Build, test and maintain dbt models that turn raw data into trusted, analytics-ready datasets
•    Design and improve data marts and semantic layers that power self-service analytics, data products and agentic agents
•    Optimise query performance and efficiency across Snowflake and dbt
•    Investigate and resolve data quality issues, and build the automated checks that catch them earlier next time
•    Partner with analysts, product managers and stakeholders to align business definitions with technical implementation
•    Write and maintain automated tests, documentation and data lineage so the model can be trusted and traced
•    Participate in code reviews and help set the engineering standards for the analytics codebase
•    Look for opportunities to simplify complex models and remove redundancy as the platform scales

What you'll bring

•    Deep, hands-on experience in analytics engineering, data modelling and ELT/ETL patterns
•    Strong SQL and fluency with modern data stack tooling - Snowflake, dbt and cloud data platforms
•    Solid understanding of dimensional modelling, metric design and data governance
•    A product mindset for data: usability, scalability and business value guide the trade-offs you make
•    The ability to translate ambiguous business requirements into scalable, well-structured models
•    Strong collaboration and communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences
•    A curious, continuous-improvement mindset - always looking to improve quality, speed and simplicity
•    High attention to detail balanced with pragmatic delivery
