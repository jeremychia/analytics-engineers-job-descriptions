Senior Power BI Developer / BI Technical Lead
act digital
Porto, Porto, Portugal

🚀 About the Role

We are looking for a highly experienced Senior Power BI Developer / BI Technical Lead to design and evolve enterprise-level Business Intelligence solutions.

In this role, you will transform complex datasets and business requirements into scalable data models, high-performing dashboards, and actionable insights. You will work extensively with Power BI, advanced SQL, and DAX, taking ownership of the complete reporting lifecycle, from data extraction and modelling to visualization, optimization, security, and publication.

As a senior technical reference, you will also review solutions, mentor less experienced developers, and promote consistent engineering and governance standards across the BI ecosystem.

🎯 Your Mission

As a Senior Power BI Developer, you will:

- Build scalable and business-focused Power BI solutions.

- Design optimized data models for enterprise reporting.

- Develop advanced SQL queries for complex and historical data extraction.

- Translate sophisticated business rules into effective DAX calculations.

- Improve report performance, refresh times, and user experience.

- Define and promote Power BI development and governance best practices.

- Provide technical guidance to junior and mid-level developers.
