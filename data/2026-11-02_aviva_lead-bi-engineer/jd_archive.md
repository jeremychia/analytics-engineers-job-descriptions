Lead BI Engineer
Women in Data®
Norwich, England, United Kingdom

- Location: Norwich

- Salary: Circa £50k

This is a great job for someone who enjoys combining hands-on data engineering expertise with team leadership. You'll be experienced with Snowflake, dbt and SQL and ready to play a key role in shaping how data supports decision-making across Personal Lines.

A bit about the job

You'll join our Personal Lines Business Intelligence team, helping us deliver trusted, high-quality data that supports decisions across the business. The role exists to lead the development and enhancement of our modern data transformation platform, ensuring data is reliable, scalable and ready for future growth.

You'll coordinate engineering delivery, support and develop a talented team, and help drive our move from legacy platforms to a cloud-based data ecosystem. Working with engineers, analysts and business stakeholders, you'll make sure our data products meet business needs and deliver real value. You'll also play an important role in shaping engineering standards, ways of working and continuous improvement across the team.

Skills and experience we're looking for

- Strong experience using Snowflake, dbt and SQL to build and enhance data transformation pipelines and data models.

- Experience leading technical delivery, setting engineering standards and supporting teams to solve complex challenges.

- Previous people leadership experience, including coaching, development conversations and performance reviews.

- Ability to work closely with stakeholders and translate business requirements into practical engineering solutions.

- Experience working in Agile environments. Scrum Master, Azure DevOps, Jira or wider BI platform migration experience would be beneficial.
