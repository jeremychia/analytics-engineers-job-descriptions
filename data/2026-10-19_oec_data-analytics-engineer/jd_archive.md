Data Analytics Engineer
OEC
Poland

About Us

OEC creates automotive e-commerce technology, helping repair shops, auto part distributors and manufacturers run their businesses more effectively and profitably through e-commerce and data innovation. We increase efficiency for the automotive aftermarket, OEM, collision, mechanical and supply chain space by connecting repair shops, dealers, parts distributors and manufacturers in one seamless, e-commerce platform. OEC makes finding and ordering the right parts simple, fast and accurate.

Job Summary

The Analytics Engineer will help us build and maintain infrastructure for data and business analytics, financial data visualization, and exploratory data analysis. Responsible for managing data systems and ensuring information accuracy to support business operations and requirements. This position will work with OEC engineers to maintain all the database's safety and security to prevent unauthorized access and potential leaks of confidential data.

What You’ll Accomplish:

- Develop and maintain data infrastructure for analytics here at OEC.

- Build pipelines to extract data from primary and secondary sources.

- Build and support data visualization for Business and Product needs.

- Work with business and tech Product Managers to define reporting and prepare reports to assist with tracking and achieving their goals.

- Analyze parts and application data for trends, patterns, errors.

- Work with Engineers and Software Developers to identify potential data issues and help fix them.

- Build and maintain our data architecture by staging, transforming and finalizing datasets that meet our high standards of quality through out of box & custom testing as well as QA and arere used across the organization.

- Develop and document this central source of truth for OEC and assist in our internal digital transformation.
