(NYSE: ITT) is a diversified global leading manufacturer of highly engineered critical components and customized technology solutions serving the flow, aerospace and defense, transportation, industrial, and energy markets. Position Summary

Are you an experienced Analytics/Visualization Engineer with a passion for transforming complex data into clear, impactful insights?

Do you enjoy leading teams, collaborating with business stakeholders and delivering enterprise-wide analytics solutions using Power BI and Microsoft Fabric?

Do you want to be part of a global industry leader?

At ITT, we are seeking an experienced Lead Analytics Engineer drive our data visualization and analytics strategies across the enterprise. As part of the Data Analytics & AI team, you will supervise a team of Analytics Engineers, work closely with Data Engineers and AI/ML Engineers, and partner with business stakeholders across the organization to deliver trusted, scalable, and impactful analytics solutions.

This role requires both strong technical expertise and people leadership, ensuring that reporting solutions are built using modern best practices while enabling data-driven decision making across the enterprise.

Essential Responsibilities

To succeed in this role, you will need to:

Demonstrate excellent leadership skills, with the ability to inspire, mentor, coach, and develop a high-performing team of Analytics Engineers.

Have a strong understanding of business intelligence, data visualization, and analytics engineering best practices.

Be highly driven and able to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Be passionate about delivering intuitive, high-quality reporting solutions that provide meaningful business insights.

Be comfortable collaborating with both technical and non-technical stakeholders across the organization.

Drive standardization, governance, and best practices for analytics development across the enterprise.

Foster a culture of continuous improvement, innovation, and business partnership.

Ensure that reporting solutions and related delivery processes are scalable, repeatable, secure, and measurable across people, processes, and technology.

More specifically you will:

Lead and supervise the day-to-day activities, priorities, and professional development of the Analytics Engineering team.

Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand business challenges and translate requirements into scalable analytics and reporting solutions.

Lead the design and development of interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI, ensuring a high level of user experience and functionality

Oversee the day-to-day responsibilities and priorities of a team of analytics engineers

Develop and maintain data models, calculations, and KPI definitions to support business reporting and analytics

Work closely with the Data Engineers and AI/ML Engineers to define data models, semantic models, and reporting requirements that support enterprise analytics, as well as integrate predictive analytics, AI insights, and advanced analytical capabilities into business reporting where appropriate.

Provide technical leadership on visualization strategies, helping to set standards for dashboard design, performance optimization, and storytelling

Ensure data quality and governance in reporting environments, establishing standards for data visualization best practices and consistency

Stay updated on the latest trends, tools, and best practices in the field of data visualization and data modeling, advocating for the adoption of modern techniques

Help spread the knowledge of data visualization to business users, fostering a data-literate culture across the organization

Promote self-service analytics while ensuring governance, consistency, and data quality across reporting solutions.
