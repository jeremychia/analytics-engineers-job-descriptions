Role Description

This role would be perfect with someone with a solid understanding of relational databases (particularly building complex views and functions in SQL or PostgreSQL) who is ready for their next challenge building Tableau dashboards in a business facing role.

You will be hands-on developing Tableau dashboards, but developers in our team are responsible for the full data pipeline. Building the queries, views and functions in SQL is often the most complex part of this process, so we want you to have those skills already.

You'll need to be confident building rapport with people from across the business as well as using your SQL skills to bring together the data they need to make faster, better decisions. If you have existing SQL skills, are a quick learner and understand what makes good visual design, we can teach the Tableau skills.

What it's like to work in the Team

You will be part of Live Nation UK & Ireland's Business Intelligence team. We are a business focussed team, and our mission is to help the business better understand itself.

We sit at the heart of Live Nation UK&I, working with teams across our concerts, festivals and venues business to deliver systems, solutions, reporting and insight.

We are a collaborative and diverse team of creative problem solvers, with plenty of autonomy and the freedom to explore new ideas. With the opportunity to shape new ways of working and deliver projects, we have a real impact across the business.
