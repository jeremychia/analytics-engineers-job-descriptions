About the Role

We are seeking a skilled and detail-oriented Analytics Engineer with 1–3 years of experience to join our growing Analytics team.

The ideal candidate will bridge the gap between data engineering and business intelligence by transforming raw data into clean, reliable, well-structured, and reusable analytical datasets. The candidate will be responsible for developing data transformation pipelines, building data models, implementing data quality checks, and optimizing datasets for reporting and analytics.

This role requires strong SQL skills, experience with data transformation frameworks, an understanding of data warehousing concepts, and the ability to collaborate with Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, Product, and Client teams to deliver trusted data solutions for enterprise loyalty programs.

What You'll Do

- Build and maintain SQL-based data transformation models to convert raw data into clean, analytics-ready datasets.

- Develop, test, and optimize data models using SQL, dbt, or similar transformation frameworks.

- Design dimensional data models, including fact tables, dimension tables, and analytical data marts.

- Integrate data from multiple sources, including transactional databases, loyalty platforms, APIs, and external systems.

- Translate business requirements into reusable datasets, standardized metrics, and analytical models.

- Define and maintain key business metrics related to loyalty performance, customer engagement, points accumulation, reward redemption, and retention.

- Implement data quality checks, validation rules, and reconciliation processes to ensure data accuracy.

- Troubleshoot data discrepancies, monitor pipeline execution, and optimize queries and transformation workflows.

- Collaborate with BI, Data Engineering, Product, and other teams to support dashboards, reporting, and business analytics.

- Maintain data documentation, model dependencies, and transformation logic while following coding, testing, and version control best practices.
