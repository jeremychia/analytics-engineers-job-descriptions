Business Intelligence Engineer, ShipTech
Amazon
Hyderabad, Telangana, India

Description

Shiptech Visibility team is responsible to deliver end-to-end supply chain visibility by blending telemetry data from multiple sources—IoT sensors, GPS trackers, EDI feeds, and carrier APIs—with varying accuracy into high-confidence contextual milestones. We eliminate visibility gaps across the logistics network and enable businesses to operate with real-time exception management and shipment predictability. We provide high-fidelity tracking signals with geofencing capabilities, accurate EDD predictions, and contextual shipment milestone updates. We secure today's small parcel operations while building tomorrow's intermodal connectivity infrastructure.

The ideal candidate relishes working with large volumes of data, enjoys the challenge of highly complex business contexts, and, above all else, is passionate about data and analytics. The candidate is an expert with business intelligence tools and passionately partners with the business to identify strategic opportunities where data-backed insights drive value creation. An effective communicator, the candidate crisply translates analysis result into executive-facing business terms. The candidate works aptly with internal and external teams to push the projects across the finishing line. The candidate is a self-starter, comfortable with ambiguity, able to think big (while paying careful attention to detail), and enjoys working in a fast-paced and global team.

Key job responsibilities

- Work closely with the BIEs, DEs and SDEs in the team and collaborate effectively with product managers to create scalable solutions for business problems.

- Use AI-first approach in solving problems to achieve speed and throughput

- Create program goals and related metrics, track progress, and manage through obstacles to help team achieve objectives

- Identify opportunities for improvement or automation in existing data processes and lead the changes using your business acumen and data handling skills.

- Use the best practice on data integrity, design, test, implementation, documentation, and knowledge sharing.

- Contribute to transportation operations strategy development based on data analysis

- Lead strategic projects to formalize and scale organizational processes

- Build and manage weekly, monthly and quarterly business review metrics.

- Build data reports and dashboards: Leverage your data skills and technical means (SQL, Excel, etc.) to improve on business efficiency across programs.
