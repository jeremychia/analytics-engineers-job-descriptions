The Workforce Strategy (WFS) team within the Technology Business Enablement (TBE) organization helps American Express align its technology workforce with enterprise needs. The team serves as a strategic advisory partner to senior technology leadership providing Enterprise Technology Services (ETS), ensuring the right talent is available at the right place, time and within optimized global cost structure to support technology portfolio demand. WFS collaborates with ETS Portfolio, ETS Finance, Talent Acquisitions, Global Real Estate, Technology Vendor Management and other enterprise partners to deliver forward-looking, data-driven insights on workforce planning, organizational design, and location strategy - enabling coordinated decisions that support scalable growth and long-term business outcomes

The Sr. Manager, Analytics Engineering and Data Strategy will play a key role in establishing future-ready workforce data foundations that power analytics, AI, reporting, and intelligent decision-making across ETS. The role sits at the intersection of business strategy, analytics engineering, data architecture, and data products, translating complex business and metric needs into trusted, scalable, and reusable data capabilities.

This role combines strong business acumen with deep technical expertise. The successful candidate will work directly with business and technology stakeholders to understand the decisions and outcomes they are trying to enable, discover and assess underlying data, design appropriate data and transformation architecture, and build reusable analytics data products that can support multiple downstream use cases.

The role will help move WFS from fragmented, report-specific data preparation toward a governed analytics ecosystem where critical data and business logic are reusable, trusted, documented, and engineered for scale.

How will you make an impact in this role?

The Sr. Manager, Analytics Engineering and Data Strategy will lead the development of scalable analytics data foundations and reusable data products that connect enterprise data with workforce metrics, analytics, reporting, AI, and business decision-making.

The role will take complex and sometimes ambiguous business and data problems from discovery through architecture, engineering, adoption, and ongoing ownership. This individual will work across Business, Data Engineering, Architecture, Data Governance, Analytics, and Technology teams to determine how data should be sourced, modeled, transformed, governed, and made reusable across multiple workforce use cases.

The role will also help establish modern analytics engineering practices, architecture principles, and development standards that improve reliability, quality, maintainability, and speed while enabling analysts and business teams to spend less time sourcing, joining, cleaning, and reconciling data and more time generating insights and driving business outcomes.

Responsibilities

Build the Analytics Data Foundation

- Lead the design and development of trusted, analytics-ready data foundations that transform complex enterprise and workforce data into reusable, business-ready datasets

- Lead discovery across complex or fragmented workforce data domains to understand source systems, business definitions, data grain, keys, history, dependencies, quality considerations, and downstream consumption needs

- Design scalable data models, transformation layers, and architectures that support workforce metrics, reporting, advanced analytics, and AI

- Establish consistent approaches to business rules, historical data, reusable dimensions, data quality, and lineage

Develop Trusted, Reusable Data Products

- Partner with business and technical stakeholders to translate analytical needs into scalable, reusable data products rather than one-off solutions

- Define data-product architecture, transformations, business logic, quality standards, documentation, and ownership

- Balance immediate business priorities with long-term architectural scalability, maintainability, and reuse

Establish Modern Analytics Engineering Practices

- Lead hands-on development of complex data transformations using SQL, BigQuery, and Python, leveraging tools such as dbt, Git, and Apache Airflow or comparable technologies

- Establish engineering standards for modular development, version control, testing, deployment, orchestration, and monitoring

- Implement automated data-quality, validation, reconciliation, and lineage capabilities to improve trust and reliability

Drive Cross-Functional Collaboration and Data Strategy

- Serve as a technical bridge across Business, Analytics, Data Engineering, Architecture, Governance, and enterprise technology teams

- Shape the analytics engineering and workforce data strategy, identifying opportunities to simplify fragmented data ecosystems and build scalable capabilities

- Enable reliable data consumption across Power BI, Excel, SQL, Python, and emerging AI solutions

- Mentor team members and promote engineering practices focused on reusability, automation, quality, and business value

- Mentor and upskill analysts and other team members in SQL, data modeling, data-product thinking, reusable transformation patterns, data quality, and modern analytical development practices

- Promote a data product mindset centered on business value, reliability, reusability, maintainability, automation, quality, and ownership
