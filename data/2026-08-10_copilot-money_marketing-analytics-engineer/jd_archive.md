Who we are

Copilot Money is on a mission to help people stress less about money. We're building a new kind of financial platform that empowers people to take control of their finances, make informed decisions, and achieve their goals. We're a high-energy, fast-paced startup looking for determined, hands-on folks to join our team and make a real impact on people's financial lives.

The Role

In this role, you'll be part of our Engineering team, working cross-functionally with Marketing and Finance.

This is a hybrid position based in New York City you'll be required to come into our office at least 2 times a week along with the rest of the team. We work from home the rest of the week, but the office is always available in case you want to make use of it more often.

Key Responsibilities

Understand the data needs of finance, marketing, and product teams in terms of key data models and centralized reporting, and translate that into technical requirements.

Define, build and manage key data pipelines, foundational data products, dashboards and tools to enable self-serve analytics to scale across the company.

Become an expert in Copilot Money's data models and the company's data architecture.

Own attribution and conversion measurement end-to-end: server-side tagging (sGTM), conversion APIs, and audience sync across paid channels (Meta, Google, etc.) and payment providers (Apple, Stripe).

Own analytics instrumentation across web, iOS, and the marketing site (GA4, Amplitude): event taxonomy, tracking setup, event audits, and closing data gaps so product, marketing, and finance can trust the numbers.

Maintain marketing integrations (sGTM, Customer.io, Impact, AppsFlyer, etc.) and stand-up new integrations.

Support consent and compliance tooling (Transcend, Google Consent Mode, cookie consent) to maintain privacy-safe standards as regulations evolve.

Manage marketing website operations: landing pages, tracking setup, troubleshooting, and technical support for SEO/AEO/GEO work.

Explore and build automation systems that improve brand visibility across search and AI-generated answers (i.e., designing content architecture and schema frameworks to increase citation frequency in LLMs.)
