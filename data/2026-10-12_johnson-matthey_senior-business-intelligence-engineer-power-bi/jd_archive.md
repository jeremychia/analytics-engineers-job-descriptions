Senior Business Intelligence Engineer (Power BI)
Johnson Matthey
Vilnius, Vilniaus, Lithuania

Pay Range: €61,500 - €77,000

At Johnson Matthey, your work will matter. As a Senior Business Intelligence Engineer (Power BI), you’ll join a world leader in platinum group metals with over 200 years of history and innovation. You’ll help tackle the world’s biggest challenges - cutting emissions, powering modern society and enhancing lives - as we continue to apply, refine and recycle metals that matter, for a healthier and more resilient future.

The role:

As a Senior Business Intelligence Engineer (Power BI), you will help drive our goals by:

- Building informative reports and dashboards that deliver timely, accurate insights to stakeholders - supporting improved decision-making and driving tangible business results. You will be creating innovative Power BI solutions that enhance transparency and empower data-driven decisions.

- Enabling self-service analytics by acting as a Power BI subject matter expert, educating users and promoting adoption of reporting best practices across the organisation.

- Ensuring data integrity and trust by improving data models and championing data quality throughout the reporting lifecycle.

- Delivering embedded analytics solutions by integrating Power BI insights into operational processes, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and streamlining workflows through automation.

- Owning end-to-end solution delivery, from gathering requirements to building, testing, and deploying scalable data solutions that meet diverse business needs.

- Exploring advanced capabilities like Microsoft Fabric and CoPilot to enhance analytics offerings and unlock strategic value.

Key skills that will help you succeed in this role:

- 3+ years of experience in Power BI reporting and analytics project implementation, with a proven ability to design, develop, and deliver complex reporting solutions. This includes semantic models, reports and dashboards development, UI design, DAX, Power Query/M language, and a solid understanding of data visualisation best practices.

- Strong knowledge of the Microsoft Power Platform, including key Power BI components such as Power BI Desktop, Power BI Service, Power BI Gateway, and Power BI Report Server.

- Solid understanding of data modelling and analytics solution design, with the ability to reverse engineer complex problems and optimise data structures for performance and usability.

- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to bridge technical and business needs, challenge constructively, and work effectively with cross-functional stakeholders.

- Fluency in English, both written and spoken.

- Previous exposure to manufacturing and operational reporting is highly desirable.

- Power BI certification (e.g. Microsoft Certified: Data Analyst Associate) and knowledge of Microsoft Fabric (architecture, core services, Power BI integration, real-time analytics) are also highly desirable.

Even if you only match some of the skills, we’d love to hear from you to discuss further!
