About Arki Finance

Arki Finance is a Singapore-based wealth management fintech building a more personalised and intelligent way for individuals to manage their wealth.

Our platform combines investment expertise, technology, and data to help clients structure and manage their wealth across different needs and time horizons.

As we scale our client base, AUM, and product capabilities, data is becoming central to how we operate, understand our clients, and improve what we build.

The Role

We're hiring an Analytics Engineer to build the data foundation for Arki's next stage of growth — a hybrid role sitting between data engineering and analytics, with direct exposure to the CEO and senior management.

This is not primarily a dashboarding role. We need someone who can build the underlying data infrastructure, create trusted data models, and then use that data to answer important business questions.

What You'll Do

Build Arki's Data Foundation

Design and maintain data pipelines connecting Arki's core systems — onboarding, transactions, investment accounts, portfolios, CRM, and product applications

Develop and maintain a central analytics data warehouse

Build reliable ETL/ELT workflows turning raw operational data into analysis-ready datasets

Develop reusable data models for clients, accounts, transactions, portfolios, and products

Establish clear definitions and a consistent source of truth for key business metrics

Implement data-quality checks, monitoring, and reconciliation processes

Partner Directly With the CEO on Business Analytics

Deliver regular and ad hoc analysis on client growth, funded-client conversion, AUM, gross/net inflows, withdrawals, retention, product adoption, revenue economics, and forecasting

Go beyond reporting what happened — investigate why, and recommend what Arki should do about it

Understand Client Behaviour

Analyse the customer journey from registration through onboarding, funding, investing, and ongoing engagement

Build funnel and cohort analyses to see where clients convert, disengage, or deepen their relationship with Arki

Develop segmentation based on behaviour, product usage, and financial characteristics

Support Product Development

Partner with Product and Tech to define data requirements for new features, ensure proper event tracking, and measure feature adoption

Help design and evaluate experiments; provide data-driven recommendations on user journeys and conversion

Build for the Future

Lay the groundwork for predictive analytics, client propensity models, personalisation, and AI-enabled decision support down the line

What We're Looking For

3–5 years in analytics engineering, data engineering, data analytics, or a related role

Strong SQL and Python for data processing and analysis

Experience building and maintaining ETL/ELT pipelines and designing analytical data models

Experience with relational databases and modern cloud data warehouses

Strong grasp of data quality, validation, and reconciliation

Comfortable working independently with imperfect or evolving datasets

Able to communicate technical findings clearly to senior, non-technical stakeholders

Useful, not required: dbt, Airflow, Snowflake/BigQuery/Redshift/Databricks, PostgreSQL, AWS/GCP/Azure, Tableau/Power BI/Looker/Metabase, Mixpanel/Amplitude, Git, API integrations

Fintech, banking, wealth, or asset management experience is a plus, but candidates from consumer tech, e-commerce, or SaaS backgrounds are equally welcome.

Who Thrives Here

You'll likely do well if you enjoy building things from the ground up, take ownership rather than waiting for instructions, care about the quality of underlying data (not just the dashboard on top), naturally investigate why numbers move, and are comfortable working directly with a CEO and senior leadership.

Why Join Arki

You'll join at a stage where the data function is still being built with direct CEO visibility and the chance to shape not just reporting, but how Arki makes decisions, defines its metrics, and eventually powers more personalised financial experiences for clients.
