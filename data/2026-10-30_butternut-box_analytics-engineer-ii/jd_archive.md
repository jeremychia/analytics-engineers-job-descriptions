🐾 The job:

A bit about data at Butternut: We’re hiring a mid-level Analytics Engineer to join our data team at Butternut. In this role, you'll work closely with our data analysts and data scientists to create clean, tested, well-documented and well-modelled datasets. Your work will support teams and projects across the business, from experimentation to production.

Our data stack includes Snowflake for data warehousing, dbt for data transformation and testing, and Looker for business intelligence. We also use Python for automation and integrations, AWS for infrastructure, and GitHub for version control and CI/CD.

You'll build and maintain foundational data models that support reporting, data science and self-serve analytics. You’ll have the opportunity to build next generation analytics using generative AI and agentic tools to help your colleagues across the business get more value from the data. Alongside this, you’ll use Python to automate workflows and improve the reliability and efficiency of our data systems, making it easier for colleagues to access and use data confidently.

If you enjoy solving problems, building reliable systems and helping people make better decisions with data (and you love dogs), this might be the role for you.

You can expect to get stuck into:

- Working with our in-house product engineering teams to ensure source data supports downstream requirements.

- Building and maintaining SQL transformations, tests and documentation in dbt.

- Developing Python code to automate data workflows and integrate systems.

- Contributing to data modelling best practices and advising the team on how to write reliable, maintainable transformations.

- Working with stakeholders across the business to make clean, intuitive datasets available through Looker and AI data analyst tools.

- Working with our analysts and data scientists to identify opportunities to improve data quality, optimise performance and automate manual processes.

(Please note that this is not an exhaustive list, but is intended to give you an indication of what your day-to-day might look like here at Butternut.)

👀 What we're looking for:

- You have experience using dbt to build production-grade data models and test suites.

- You have practical experience writing maintainable Python code for data processing, automation or integrations.

- You bring an engineering mindset to data, with a focus on testing, documentation and maintainability.

- You have excellent SQL skills, including a solid understanding of how to optimise query performance.

- You have a good practical understanding of version control and Git.

- You have natural curiosity and a keen eye for detail.

- You can explain complex processes, requirements and data concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- You work collaboratively with technical teams and business stakeholders to understand requirements and develop useful solutions.

It would be a bonus if you have:

- Experience building and maintaining ETL or ELT pipelines.

- Experience with AWS.

- Experience with BI tools, particularly Looker, LookML or the Looker API.

- Experience with generative AI or agentic tools, or an interest in learning how to use them safely to provide value from data.
