Web Analytics Engineer
If Insurance Baltic
Tallinn, Harjumaa, Estonia

Drive change with your expertise! Join us as a Web Analytics Engineer and work on strategic initiatives with a collaborative team!

About the role

As a Web Analytics Engineer, you will ensure accurate tracking and measurement across our digital channels, transform data into actionable insights, and help optimize our digital sales and marketing performance.Your main goal will be to provide insights and recommendations that help optimize digital campaigns, improve channel performance, and drive continuous development of our web sales channel.

What are you going to do?

- Be the go-to person for Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics (GA360/GA4), including server-side tracking in Google Cloud.

- Set up and manage tags, cookies, and tracking tools so we can measure what’s happening on our websites.

- Help marketing teams run campaigns by making sure tracking works smoothly across Google, Meta, and other ad platforms.

- Build easy-to-use reports, dashboards, and funnels so everyone can see how things are performing.

- Connect our marketing tools and platforms to ensure reliable and seamless data flow.

- Troubleshoot tracking issues and collaborate closely with marketers, analysts, and developers.

- Keep our documentation clear and up to date, while making sure we follow GDPR and best practices.

- Take part in Agile projects, support ongoing tasks, and share knowledge with the team.

- Communicate progress, findings, and recommendations to stakeholders in a clear and actionable way.

What do we expect from you?

Must have

- Experience working with OneTrust and managing cookie consent.

- Hands-on skills in Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 360/GA4.

- A solid understanding of JavaScript and HTML—you don’t need to be a developer, but you know your way around the basics.

- Fluency in English and at least one Baltic language.

Nice to have / We can also help you to develop

- Familiarity with Google Consent Management Platform (CMP), Google Cloud Platform, or Google Marketing Platform.

- Experience with Meta for Business (Facebook/Instagram Ads).

- Knowledge of LinkedIn Marketing Solutions and Microsoft Advertising.

- A relevant bachelor’s degree or higher.

- Previous experience working in an Agile team environment.

- Good communication, collaboration, and organizational skills.

- Practical experience working with data analytics, reporting, or data development.

- Exposure to tools like Hotjar, VWO, or Supermetrics.
