Job Description

What will you be doing? 

We're seeking a passionate and experienced Data Analytics Engineer to join a thriving team and help in the

design, build and maintenance of scalable data solutions, that bring together data from across the organisation.

You'll ensure data is well structured, reliable and easy to use for reporting, analytics and in decision‑making. Working as part of the Data Platform Team, you'll help create a high‑quality, future‑ready data environment that supports business needs now and we continue to grow!

This is a permanent opportunity to be based at our Southampton office with office attendance expected to be 2 days per week.

As a Data Analytics Engineer your responsibilities will include among others,

Architect and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines, optimising for performance, reliability, cost, and data quality across multiple data sources

Maintain and troubleshoot medallion architecture and dimensional modelling (star schemas) to support enterprise reporting, KPIs, and Power BI semantic models

Design, implement, and maintain Lakehouse and Warehouse structures in Microsoft Fabric, including data modelling standards and semantic layers

Provide operational support, monitoring, troubleshooting, and performance tuning for Microsoft Fabric ingestion, dataflows, and transformation workloads

Identify, resolve, and prevent data quality issues through validation rules, monitoring, alerting, and root‑cause analysis with source system owners

Design, build, and maintain enterprise‑standard Power BI reports and dashboards, aligned to agreed data models, governance, and reporting standards

Qualifications

Skills and Experience

To be successful in this role, you should have,

Demonstrable experience leading the end-to-end delivery of data and analytics initiatives, ensuring outcomes are aligned to both data strategy and broader business objectives

Ability to own and drive key data projects from concept to production, balancing delivery pace with quality, scalability and measurable business impact

Experience in partnering with senior stakeholders to shape priorities, translate business strategy into data solutions and ensure delivered products enable effective decision-making

Strong experience in designing and delivering data solutions on Microsoft Fabric (Lakehouse, Warehouse, semantic models, Power BI), with a strong focus on scalable architecture and data modelling principles

Advanced SQL and Python/PySpark, applied across ETL/ELT development, optimisation, and automation of data pipelines

Strong engineering practices experience including performance tuning, platform monitoring and CI/CD with version control (GitHub) within modern data workflows
