Job Overview

About The Role

Love getting stuck into data and finding the story behind the numbers? As a BI Developer at AO, you'll help us make smarter decisions every day.

We're building a modern cloud data platform and looking for someone who can turn data into clear, useful insights that people across AO can actually use. You'll create reports, dashboards and data models, test new ideas, and help bring new data sources into our BI tools.

You'll work closely with Data Engineers, Data Platforms and other BI Developers, sharing knowledge and helping us continuously improve the way we use data across the business.

If you're someone who's curious, enjoys solving problems and gets a buzz from turning complex data into something simple and impactful, this could be for you!

Here's What You Can Expect To Be Doing

You’ll be gathering requirements from stakeholders, designing intuitive visualisations, and developing robust data models that help AO run at pace. You’ll support our migration to a modern data platform, maintain clear technical documentation, and respond quickly to incidents and change requests. Continuous improvement is in your DNA—you’ll optimise performance, ensure security and compliance, and uphold coding standards to keep our BI solutions top-notch. Your work will help drive better decisions, faster action, and a culture where data powers possibilities.

A Few Things About You

- Proficient with ETL processes (SSIS, AWS Glue, DBT) and BI tools (Power BI, Sigma, etc.)

- Skilled in data modelling and modern data platforms (Snowflake, Databricks, etc.)

- Excellent SQL and database knowledge

- Passionate about data, with a sharp eye for detail and quality

- Confident communicator, able to make complex info easy to understand

- Experienced in agile environments and a true team player

- Creative problem solver who loves a challenge

- Adaptable and comfortable with change

- Tech enthusiast, always curious about new tools and trends

- Meticulous and thorough in everything you do

- Bonus points for experience with AWS, AI tools, big data tech, or data governance
