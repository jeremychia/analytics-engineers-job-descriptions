Role Purpose

As Analytics Engineer, you will join the Data Platform team to support the development of Samsara's Industrial AI Platform, collaborating with science, engineering, and computation teams across experiment, process modelling, and production data. As an Analytics Engineer you will develop the ontology, schema design, transformation and data quality foundations that turn raw data into trusted, model-ready datasets.

 Your focus will be building Samsara's Unified Data Model – mapping core entities across Design, Bioengineering, Bioproduction, Technology Scale-up, Chemistry, Process Engineering, Analytics, and Operations – and delivering model-ready datasets to the Industrial AI Platform. Working from foundations in a fast-moving environment, leadership, comfort with uncertainty, adaptability, and iterative design will be essential.

Responsibilities 

Ontology and Data Modelling

Own the shared ontology and schema design for Samsara's core scientific and engineering entities.

Iterate the ontology and data model as new verticals arise.

Data Quality and Transformation

Develop data pipelines to transform raw data into usable datasets for science, engineering, and computation teams.

Build and maintain data quality checks and validation pipelines for high-value scientific and engineering datasets.

Ensure production-layer tables are reliable, clearly documented and queryable by scientists and technical stakeholders.

Stakeholder Collaboration

 Spend time with scientists and engineers to understand the meaning, limitations, and context of data before modeling it.

Lead a cross-functional group of data stewards across Design, Bioengineering, Bioproduction, Technology Scale-up, Chemistry, Process Engineering, Analytics, and Operations, to ensure alignment and collaboration with Data Platform.

Communicate data definitions, assumptions and quality expectations clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.

Help build a data-centric culture where teams understand why ontology, metadata, and quality standards matter.

Skills and Competencies

Essential

Experience in analytics engineering, data modelling and data transformation within a scientific, engineering or technical environment.

Strong SQL, dbt and analytics skills, paired with software best practices in Python.

Demonstrated ability to design schemas, ontology, or data models that reflect real-world scientific or operational processes.

Strong understanding of data quality, lineage, documentation and metadata standards.

 Ability to work closely with scientists, engineers, and ML teams to translate domain knowledge into trusted datasets.

Highly Valued

Experience with Databricks/Snowflake, AWS, Apache Iceberg and lake-house architecture.

Exposure to ELN, LIMS, laboratory instrumentation data, physics-simulation data, production line data, or process engineering datasets.

Understanding FAIR data principles, ISA-88 or comparable scientific and manufacturing data standards.

Background in bioprocess engineering, chemical engineering, biotechnology, life sciences, manufacturing or climate tech.
