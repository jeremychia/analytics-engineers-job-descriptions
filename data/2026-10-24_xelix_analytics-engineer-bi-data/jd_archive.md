About The Role

We are now hiring for an Analytics Engineer to join our new BI / Data team. This role is an exciting opportunity to turn raw data into the trusted metrics and self-service analytics that every team at Xelix, from Finance to Product, makes decisions with, built AI-native so a small team delivers trusted metrics in weeks rather than quarters. Whether you're an experienced mid-level engineer ready for broader ownership or already operating at senior level, you'll have real influence on how Xelix defines and uses its data.

What You'll Be Doing

- Model clean, joined tables in dbt for customers, contracts, usage, product outcomes, support, revenue and cost
- Work AI-native from day one: AI coding agents draft models, tests and documentation, while you own definitions, review and quality
- Own the metrics layer: one tested definition each for ARR, customer retention, realised customer value, touchless rate and cost to serve, with a first version in use within your first six weeks
- Move existing Metabase and customer review reporting onto governed models, and retire duplicate dashboards together with their owners
- Enable Finance, RevOps, Commercial, Product, Engineering and Services to self-serve, including plain-language analytics on top of the metrics layer, and help choose our analytics tooling
- Agree definitions with business owners and document them so people and AI tools read them the same way

What You’ll Bring

- Experience as an analytics engineer or BI developer, building models in dbt on a cloud data warehouse
- You already work AI-native: you use AI coding agents daily for SQL, dbt models, tests and documentation, know how to verify what they produce, and understand how semantic layers make AI answers reliable
- Excellent SQL and data modelling, including dimensional models and metrics or semantic layers
- Experience with a BI tool such as Metabase, Omni, Looker or Power BI, and with enabling non-technical users
- Business sense: you understand SaaS metrics like ARR, churn and expansion, and can turn questions into data
- Clear communication and patience in getting stakeholders to agree on one definition

Interview Process

While the exact process may vary slightly depending on the role, our typical interview stages are:

- Introductory Call – A short Teams conversation with a Talent Partner to discuss your background and the opportunity.
- Hiring Manager Interview – A 30–45 minute Teams meeting to explore your experience and fit for the team.
- Technical Task or Presentation – A role-relevant exercise to demonstrate your skills and approach.
- Final On-site Interview – An in-person meeting with our senior leadership team and co-founders at our office.

We strive to make the process clear, efficient, and respectful of your time.
