International Commercial Senior Analytics Engineer
Cartier
Meyrin, Geneva, Switzerland

We would like to talk to you about the opportunity to join our Maison as International Commercial Senior Analytics Engineer, reporting to the International Commercial Data Science Manager.

In this role, you lead and deliver high-visibility data initiatives that directly drive our commercial operations. Heavily project-focused, your primary priority will be executing complex analytics projects—leveraging Google BigQuery, dbt, Looker (LookML), and Python to build scalable solutions rapidly and efficiently. While you will also oversee core platform maintenance and architectural updates, your focus will be on high-impact project delivery.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

High-Impact Project Delivery

- Project Execution: Lead the end-to-end technical delivery of major commercial analytics projects.

- BigQuery & dbt Data Modeling: Design, build, and optimize enterprise-grade data models in Google BigQuery using dbt and advanced SQL.

- Looker Development: Build and expand our semantic layer using LookML, delivering high-performing dashboards and reports for key business stakeholders.

- Stakeholder Collaboration: Partner closely with business partners to translate project requirements into clean, scalable technical implementations.

Architecture & Platform Maintenance

- Platform Upgrades & Migrations: Act as the technical owner when platform updates, architectural shifts, or system migrations occur, ensuring seamless transitions.

- System Maintenance: Perform lightweight, proactive upkeep of our BigQuery models, dbt projects, and Looker environments to maintain optimal performance, data quality, and cost efficiency.

- You have strong hands-on experience optimizing queries, managing schemas, and building data models natively in BigQuery.

- You have deep expertise in dbt and advanced SQL modeling.

- You have a proven track record of building and managing Looker semantic models with LookML.

- You collaborate effectively with commercial teams, providing practical technical solutions and guidance.
