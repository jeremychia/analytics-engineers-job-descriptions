Job Title:
 BI Engineer

Job Type: 
Permanent (Hybrid)

Job Description

Our client, a large organisation operating within the financial services sector, is seeking an experienced BI Engineer for a permanent, Westmeath-based position. The successful candidate will design, develop and support business intelligence solutions that transform complex data into clear, reliable and actionable insights. Ideally, applicants will be based in West Dublin or Westmeath, or be open to relocating within reasonable commuting distance of the office.

Job Responsibilities

Design, build and maintain interactive Power BI dashboards and reporting solutions aligned with agreed business requirements.

Convert business needs, reporting specifications and metric definitions into effective visualisations, measures, filters and drill-through functionality.

Develop and enhance semantic data models, including relationships, hierarchies, calculations and supporting metadata.

Create efficient and maintainable DAX calculations while applying appropriate performance and error-handling practices.

Monitor and improve the performance of Power BI reports by reviewing model structures, calculation logic, filtering and visual loading times.

Manage reporting assets within Microsoft Fabric and Power BI Service, including workspaces, applications, refresh schedules, access controls and deployment processes.

Implement suitable security measures, including workspace permissions, audience access and row-level security.

Support user acceptance testing, production releases, enhancement requests and post-implementation issue resolution.

Produce and maintain clear technical and user documentation covering dashboards, datasets, calculations, data sources and refresh processes.

Assist with ongoing reporting activities, access requests, recurring reporting cycles and business-as-usual support.

Experience Required

Minimum of 3 years’ experience developing and supporting Power BI solutions within a commercial environment.

Strong practical experience with Power BI Desktop, Power BI Service, DAX and Power Query.

Demonstrated experience creating and maintaining semantic models and reporting datasets.

Strong SQL skills, including the ability to develop, optimise and troubleshoot complex queries and database views.

Experience validating data outputs and supporting datasets used by business intelligence solutions.

Good understanding of dimensional modelling, reporting structures and the relationship between BI platforms and data engineering pipelines.

Experience translating business requirements into accurate, user-friendly and maintainable reporting solutions.

Proven ability to manage multiple priorities across project delivery, system enhancements and operational support.

Strong stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to communicate technical concepts to non-technical audiences.

Ideally based in West Dublin or Westmeath, or willing to relocate to facilitate regular attendance at the Westmeath office.

Desirable Skills

Experience with Microsoft Fabric, including workspace management, semantic models, security, refresh processes and deployment practices.

Previous experience working within financial services or another highly regulated environment.

Familiarity with cloud-based analytics platforms and modern data architecture.

Understanding of UX and UI principles as they apply to dashboard and report design.

Experience supporting reporting governance, controlled releases and environment separation.

Knowledge of Power BI capacity management and advanced report performance optimisation.

Educational Requirements

A third-level qualification in Computer Science, Data Analytics, Information Systems or a related discipline.

Relevant Microsoft, Power BI or data analytics certifications would be advantageous.
