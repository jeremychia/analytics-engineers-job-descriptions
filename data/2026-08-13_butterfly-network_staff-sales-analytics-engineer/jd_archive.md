Company Description

Butterfly Network, Inc. (NYSE: BFLY) is driving a digital revolution in ultrasound imaging and sensing with its proprietary Ultrasound-on-Chip semiconductor technology and software solutions. Butterfly first proved its technology in the point-of-care ultrasound market – commercializing the world's first single-probe, whole-body portable ultrasound device, which is now on its best-selling, third-generation: Butterfly iQ3. The Company combines its advanced hardware with cloud software and AI, an enterprise workflow solution (Compass AI) and other offerings to drive adoption of affordable, accessible ultrasound. Butterfly also enables third-party development of imaging AI apps through Butterfly Garden, its software development kit and AI partnership initiative.

In addition to its medical imaging products, Butterfly Embedded is the Company's Ultrasound-on-Chip licensing and co-development program designed to enable a new wave of ultrasound-enabled technologies across non-competitive healthcare markets and beyond. Through Butterfly Embedded, partners can build and scale novel ultrasound applications powered by Butterfly's proprietary semiconductor chip and software platform. Butterfly's innovations have been recognized by Prix Galien USA, Fierce 50, TIME's Best Inventions and Fast Company's World Changing Ideas, among other achievements.

We're a team of bold thinkers, problem-solvers, and innovators ready to shape the future of medical imaging. Let's build something extraordinary together!

Job Description

The Revenue Analytics Engineer owns the data foundation that powers Butterfly's commercial team. You'll build and maintain the reports, dashboards, and data integrations that Sales, Sales Ops, and Commercial leadership depend on — while also owning the CRM data quality that makes those analytics trustworthy. This role sits at the intersection of analytics engineering and revenue operations, and requires someone comfortable working both in the data layer and directly with Sales teams.

Key Responsibilities

- Build and maintain Salesforce reports and Sigma dashboards for commercial stakeholders
- Manage embedded Sigma analytics within Salesforce
- Own CRM data hygiene — partnering with Sales Ops, Sales Leaders, and reps to ensure data is accurate and complete
- Manage Data Cloud integrations pulling from the Databricks data lake into Salesforce
- Handle ad hoc commercial reporting requests
- Support AI initiatives tied to Sales activity, commercial strategy, and company OKRs

Qualifications

Baseline skills/experiences/attributes:

- 5+ years of experience in BI and analytics, with hands-on proficiency in Sigma, Tableau, Power BI, or a comparable modern BI platform
- 5+ years of SQL experience in a data warehouse or data lake environment (Databricks, BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift, or equivalent)
- 5+ years of experience in a Salesforce-heavy environment — either as a report analyst or administrator, or as a BI/analytics engineer embedded in a CRM-centric organization
- Demonstrated ability to work cross-functionally with Sales, Sales Operations, and business stakeholders to drive data quality and CRM hygiene at scale

Ideally, you also have these skills/experiences/attributes (but it's ok if you don't!):

- Experience with dbt for data transformation and modeling
- Familiarity with GitHub and Jira in an agile delivery environment
- Hands-on experience building AI automations, skills, or agents
