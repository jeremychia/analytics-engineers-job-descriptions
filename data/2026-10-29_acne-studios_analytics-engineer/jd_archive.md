What You Will Do

As Analytics Engineer your main contribution will be to build the data foundation that turns raw, validated data into business-ready models used across the company. You will report to Manager Data Insights. You will play a vital role in making data accessible, reliable and consistent for everyone at Acne Studios by:

- Designing and maintaining dimensional models (facts, dimensions, lookups) in the gold layer, following star schema principles
- Building and operating reusable data transformation pipelines using PySpark and SQL on Databricks
- Structuring and exposing data for consumption by Power BI and external systems such as Centric and Dema
- Contributing to building a centralized semantic layer in Databricks
- Implementing and maintaining core business metrics, logic and KPIs
- Ensuring data quality through automated testing and validation
- Documenting datasets and making them easy to find and use
- Partnering with stakeholders to translate business needs into requirements and data models
- Supporting analysts working in Power BI and Databricks Dashboards with clear and structured guidance
- Communicating proactively with business users about what data is available, what it means and how to use it
- Contributing to improvements in transformation patterns, testing frameworks, tools and ways of working
