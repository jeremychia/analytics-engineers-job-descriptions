About Eftsure

At Eftsure, we're on a mission to protect businesses from payment fraud and cyber risk. As we continue to scale globally, data plays a critical role in helping teams make better decisions, move faster, and create exceptional customer experiences.

We're looking for a Senior Analytics Engineer to take ownership of our analytics engineering capability and help shape the future of our data platform.

This is a high-impact role for someone who enjoys turning complex data into trusted, business-ready assets. You'll work across teams and regions, partnering with stakeholders to design robust data models, deliver self-service analytics, and establish best practices that enable the business to confidently use data every day.

If you're passionate about analytics engineering, data modelling, modern data stacks, and helping organisations become truly data-driven, we'd love to hear from you.

What You'll Do

Own our analytics engineering layer

You'll design, build and maintain scalable data models that transform raw and staged data into trusted, analytics-ready datasets. Working across our silver and gold layers, you'll create clean, reliable and well-documented data assets that become the foundation for reporting, analytics and decision-making.

Partner with the business

You'll work closely with teams across Product, Customer, Finance, Marketing, Operations and Leadership to understand business challenges and translate them into meaningful data solutions. You'll challenge assumptions, shape requirements, and ensure business logic is applied consistently across the organisation.

Enable self-service analytics

You'll build and support semantic layers, dashboards and reporting solutions that empower teams to answer their own questions. You'll help establish trusted metrics, improve data literacy, and create a culture where people can confidently use data to make decisions.

Raise the engineering bar

As a senior member of the team, you'll define analytics engineering standards and best practices across modelling, testing, documentation and governance. You'll mentor colleagues, review work, and help grow analytics engineering capability across Eftsure.

Drive continuous improvement

You'll stay current with emerging tools, technologies and industry practices, bringing new ideas and approaches into our platform. You'll evaluate opportunities, prototype solutions and contribute to the evolution of our data roadmap.
