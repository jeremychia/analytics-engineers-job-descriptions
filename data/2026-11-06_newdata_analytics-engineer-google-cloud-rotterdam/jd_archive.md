Would you rather build the data model than the dashboard, and do you enjoy explaining to the client why?

NewData is looking for three Analytics Engineers for a client. The client is a Rotterdam data and cloud consultancy of about fifty people from more than twenty nationalities. They build data platforms on Google Cloud for companies in retail, e-commerce, media and travel, among others.

Analytics Engineer here means you think like a data engineer. You build pipelines and data models that are correct, tested and documented. You are more technical than a BI specialist or data analyst, and broader than a data scientist. And because you work with clients, you can explain what you build and why.

What will you do?

- Design and build data models with dbt or Dataform and SQL, from conceptual to physical

- Build and maintain pipelines and integrations in Google Cloud, using BigQuery, Cloud Composer, Cloud Run, Pub/Sub and Cloud Storage

- Work Agile in sprints, with everything as code, test-driven development and security built in from the start

- For marketing clients: connect data from Google Analytics and ad platforms, activate it in media channels and measure what a campaign delivers

- Work with clients to define the business concepts and rules in their data, and turn them into a model

- Work with tests, Git, CI/CD and, where needed, Terraform

- Use AI daily for coding, testing and documentation, and check the output critically

- Help clients test an AI idea with a prototype, and take what works to production

- Present your results to the client

What are we looking for?

- A bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, software engineering, data engineering or a similar field

- Hands-on experience with data engineering and data modelling. Coming from software engineering and have you built data pipelines and integrations? You are welcome too

- Experience with a cloud platform. Google Cloud is a plus, moving over from AWS or Azure is fine

- Strong SQL and Python or Java

- Knowledge of dimensional modelling, such as star schemas

- Strong communication: you are a consultant and sit at the table with clients

- Experience with marketing data, such as Google Analytics or ad platforms, or a Google Cloud certification (Developer, Data Engineer or Architect) is a plus

- You live in the Netherlands and speak good English. Dutch is a plus
