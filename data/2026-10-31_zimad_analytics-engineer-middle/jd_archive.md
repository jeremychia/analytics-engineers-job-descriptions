Job Description

We are looking for Analytics Engineer. We build mobile games. User acquisition, monetization, LiveOps and product decisions all run on data — and this role owns the modeling layer everything else depends on: the data marts, metric definitions and semantic layer behind reporting, ad-hoc analysis and our AI-driven tools.

Key Responsibilities
- Build and maintain reliable data marts for the UA, monetization, product and LiveOps teams
- Prepare data models that both people and AI agents can use
- Maintain core metric definitions together with the team so the whole company can trust the numbers
- Monitor data quality with tests, freshness checks and comparisons against raw data, and investigate when numbers don’t match
- Optimize ClickHouse query performance

Requirements
- 2+ years in analytics engineering, BI development, data analysis or data engineering, building data models that others rely on
- Strong SQL: joins, window functions, aggregations, and the ability to read and debug other people’s queries
- Hands-on experience with ClickHouse: MergeTree engines, choosing ORDER BY and partition keys, and working with arrays
- Experience with analytical data modeling: choosing the right table structure and grain for how data will be queried, and building incremental, reliable models
- English proficiency at the B1–B2 level

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with data modeling tools such as dbt
- Understanding of mobile app metrics (ROAS, LTV, ARPDAU, retention)
- Experience using AI tools in data work
- Experience with workflow orchestration: scheduling pipelines, managing dependencies and handling failures
- Experience with Superset or another BI tool
