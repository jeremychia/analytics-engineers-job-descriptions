Job Summary:

We are seeking a highly experienced, hands-on, and strategic Analytics Engineer to design and develop trusted, scalable data analytics products. This role will interface directly with business and technology stakeholders. You will define requirements, model data for analytics, and develop data pipelines that transform raw and operational data into well-modeled, business-ready datasets that support marketing, web insights, behavioral analytics, business intelligence, and AI-powered analytics.

The ideal candidate demonstrates strong business acumen, communication, SQL, data modeling, and analytics development skills. They have deep technical expertise in Google Analytics, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, and Power BI. The ideal candidate combines business analysis with hands-on experience developing reusable ELT transformations, dimensional models, semantic-ready datasets, and data quality controls. This individual will play a critical role in enabling data-driven decision-making and advancing enterprise data modernization initiatives.

Primary Functions:

Web, Digital & Marketing Analytics

- Serve as a technical subject matter expert for Google Analytics and enterprise web analytics data.

- Design analytics data models that support web traffic, acquisition, engagement, conversion, campaign, and customer behavior analysis.

- Develop and maintain reliable data pipelines and transformations for Google Analytics data and related digital marketing sources.

- Integrate web behavioral data with customer, booking, sales, marketing, and other enterprise data to enable cross-channel analytics.

- Partner with and enable Marketing, Digital, Product, and business stakeholders to translate measurement requirements into scalable analytical datasets.

- Establish consistent definitions and reusable data models for sessions, users, traffic sources, campaigns, events, conversions, and digital journeys.

- Validate web analytics data quality and reconcile reporting differences across source platforms, analytical datasets, and BI solutions.

Data Architecture & Engineering

- Design conceptual, logical, and physical data models for domain-specific solutions based on business requirements, using 3NF and dimensional modeling approaches as appropriate.

- Design reusable analytics data products and structures across the Data Warehouse, Data Marts, Data Lakehouse, and Reporting/Analytics layers.

- Design and develop modular, reusable ELT transformations that meet data quality, SLA, and performance requirements.

- Apply analytics engineering practices including version control, automated testing, documentation, lineage, code review, and CI/CD.

- Own data quality, reconciliation, observability, and performance for curated analytical datasets.

- Support production analytics data products and resolve data issues across the analytical value chain.
