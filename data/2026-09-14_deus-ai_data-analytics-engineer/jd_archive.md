⚜️ About You
This role is a great fit if you have:
- 5+ years of experience working in data modelling and analytics, with proven production experience in dbt — not pilots or courses, but real models running and maintained in a live environment.

- Strong SQL skills on a cloud data warehouse, ideally Snowflake.

- Solid experience with dimensional modelling (Kimball), especially designing and accelerating fact tables.

- Comfort working with Git‑based workflows, including peer review as a natural part of your process.

- Experience building semantic models in Power BI, including DAX measures and row‑level security (RLS).

- Fluent English for daily collaboration.

…and some more points:
- You enjoy refining definitions with business analysts and controllers.

- You’re comfortable reviewing the work of colleagues and contributing to shared standards.

- You value clarity, ownership, and delivering models that are easy to understand and maintain.

Bonus points if you have:
- Experience with finance or sales data (ERP, CRM) and an eye for definition differences across an organisation.

- Exposure to NetSuite, Dynamics 365, Tagetik, or Pigment as data sources.

- Experience with CI/CD for Power BI and the PBIP format.

- Interest in AI‑assisted development within this stack.

⚜️ What You’ll Be Doing
- Building dimensional models (Kimball) in dbt on Snowflake, from staging to the serving layer.

- Developing core sales pipeline domains: opportunity funnel, sales actuals, delivery and margin reporting, and later group management reporting.

- Extending your dbt models into the semantic layer in Power BI.

- Implementing historical logic (dbt snapshots, SCD2), data quality tests, and contributing to the CI/CD pipeline.

- Working closely with business analysts and controllers to sharpen definitions and ensure alignment across countries.

- Reviewing colleagues’ work and contributing to a high‑quality modelling standard.

- Collaborating in a distributed team across NL, DE, CH, PT, and MY, using English daily.

(…and there’s always space to explore what excites you.)
