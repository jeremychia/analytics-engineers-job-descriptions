Why Join Centra?

What You'll Do:

- Build and optimize scalable cloud-based data pipelines,
- Design reliable data models and establish trusted business metrics,
- Develop high-performance data platforms and serverless APIs,
- Integrate new data sources and improve existing data infrastructure,
- Shape technical architecture and drive continuous platform improvements.

What We're Looking For:

- Experience building modern data platforms or data products,
- Strong SQL skills and experience with data modelling and transformation tools,
- Hands-on experience with AWS, GCP, Azure, or similar cloud platforms,
- Ability to make sound technical decisions with a product-focused mindset,
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform/CDK); MLOps experience is a plus.
