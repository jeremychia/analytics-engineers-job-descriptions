About the role

Enhance is hiring an Analytics Engineer in Dubai to turn our data into insights that improve personal training (PT) across our partner gyms.

Enhance powers personal training for gym operators around the world. Our B2B SaaS platform helps gym chains sell, schedule and manage PT, and we also run PT services directly inside our partners' clubs. That gives us a rare view of PT across many brands, clubs and trainers, and this role turns that view into better decisions for every partner.

You'll sit in the Data team and work closely with club managers, PT leads, Operations and Finance. You'll own the models and metrics behind personal training: how each club and trainer performs, how sessions are sold and used, and how pricing affects revenue and retention. If someone asks "which trainers have the highest rebooking rate, and is our 10-session pack priced right?", your models give the answer, and everyone gets the same answer.

What you'll do

- Build and own the data models. Design and maintain transformation models (dbt or similar) for PT packages, sessions booked, delivered and cancelled, trainer schedules, club capacity and revenue.

- Define our key metrics. Create one shared set of definitions for the core numbers we track, such as how busy trainers are, how many clients stay and renew, and how much revenue each club and trainer brings in.

- Shape pricing decisions. Analyse package price, size, discounts and trainer commission to understand margin per session and price sensitivity. Then recommend changes and help test them.

- Optimise operations. Show peak demand by club and time slot, idle trainer hours and capacity gaps, so we can schedule and staff better.

- Power self-serve reporting. Shape the tables behind dashboards so club managers and PT leads can answer their own questions without writing SQL.

- Keep data trustworthy. Add tests, freshness checks and documentation, and fix data issues before they reach a dashboard.

- Work across teams. Partner with data engineering on ingestion, and with Operations, Finance and club leadership on what they need next.
