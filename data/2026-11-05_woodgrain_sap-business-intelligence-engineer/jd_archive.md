Job Summary

The SAP Business Intelligence Engineer is responsible for designing, developing, optimizing, and supporting SAP Business Warehouse (BW/BW4HANA) and BusinessObjects (BOBJ) solutions, including data modeling, extraction, integration, and reporting. This role provides senior-level technical expertise in SAP S/4HANA integration, data architecture, and business intelligence delivery to ensure reliable, scalable, and accurate reporting capabilities. Working collaboratively with business stakeholders and IT teams, this position translates complex business requirements into technical solutions, leads complex troubleshooting efforts, establishes development standards, and identifies opportunities to improve data quality, system performance, and operational efficiency.

Duties & Responsibilities

- Design, develop, maintain, and optimize SAP BW 7.x/BW4HANA solutions, including InfoProviders (DSOs, InfoCubes, CompositeProviders), transformations, DTPs, process chains, Open Hub destinations, and BEx Queries.

- Develop and support SAP BusinessObjects solutions, including IDT universes, WebI reports, CMC administration, and report scheduling, ensuring accurate, reliable, and efficient reporting.

- Design and maintain SAP BW integrations with SAP S/4HANA, including ODP, CDS View extraction, source system connectivity, RFC configuration, DataSource replication, delta load management, and ODQMON monitoring.

- Apply knowledge of the S/4HANA data model, including ACDOCA and the Universal Journal, to support financial and operational reporting requirements.

- Translate complex business requirements into technical specifications, data models, and scalable solutions aligned with business objectives and enterprise data architecture.

- Lead technical analysis, performance optimization, and root cause resolution for complex data extraction, transformation, integration, reporting, and production support issues.

- Manage assigned solutions throughout the development lifecycle, including design, development, testing, deployment, documentation, and ongoing maintenance.

- Establish and promote technical standards, documentation practices, data quality controls, and testing procedures to improve system reliability, maintainability, and operational efficiency.

- Collaborate with business stakeholders and cross-functional IT teams, including Security and Basis, to deliver reporting solutions, resolve technical dependencies, and support appropriate user access.

- Identify opportunities to automate processes, improve data integrity, and enhance existing systems; evaluate emerging SAP analytics technologies and recommend improvements as appropriate.
