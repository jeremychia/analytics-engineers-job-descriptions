JOB TITLE: Senior Analytics Engineer

SALARY: £84,051 - £95,000

LOCATION: London

HOURS: Full-time

WORKING PATTERN: Our work style is hybrid, which involves spending at least two days per week, or 40% of our time, at one of our office sites. Colleagues with disabilities can be supported with workplace adjustments including hybrid working expectations in line with our Flexibility Works policy.

What you'll be doing…

We're looking for a Senior Analytics Engineer to join Curve's central data team, helping to build a robust and scalable analytics platform that transforms raw data into trusted, analytics-ready data products. You'll lead the design and development of production-grade ELT pipelines using dbt and BigQuery, own the modelling of complex and business-critical datasets across areas such as payments, credit and growth, and drive best practices in data modelling, testing, CI/CD and performance optimisation. Working closely with Data Engineering, BI Engineering and business stakeholders, you'll help ensure data is accessible, reliable and cost-efficient, while mentoring other analytics engineers and shaping the standards that support a high-performing data function.

Why Join us?

If you think all banks are the same, you're wrong. We're a pioneering, fast-changing business that's shaping finance as a force for good. If you're after a role where you can have an impact and do the best work of your career, you've just found it

What we're looking for?

5+ years' experience in analytics engineering, data engineering or a related discipline

Deep hands-on expertise using dbt as a primary transformation tool

Expert-level SQL skills and experience modelling large-scale datasets in a cloud data warehouse

Strong experience with BigQuery, including incremental models, partitioning and cost optimisation

Experience designing and owning complex data models from requirements through to production

Proven ability to establish and maintain engineering standards, testing strategies and CI/CD practices

Experience implementing data quality controls, monitoring and automated testing

Strong understanding of software engineering principles including Git, version control and modular development

Ability to work directly with business stakeholders and translate requirements into scalable data solutions

Experience mentoring, coaching or supporting the development of other analytics engineers

Knowledge of orchestration tools such as Airflow or Composer

Familiarity with cloud platforms, particularly Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

And it would be great if you had any of the following...

Experience with real-time or streaming data pipelines

Knowledge of infrastructure as code tools such as Terraform

Experience working with Kubernetes or container orchestration platforms

Experience within Fintech, Financial Services, Payments or Retail Banking

Previous experience supporting or mentoring junior team members

Exposure to Looker or similar BI reporting platforms
