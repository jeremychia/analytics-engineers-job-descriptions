Project Description:

- This role sits within a small team (~5) supporting business stakeholders within a Singapore sovereign fund.

- The role focuses on business analysis, Tableau dashboard development and data analytics, translating business requirements into effective reporting and analytical solutions that support data-driven decision-making.

- The ideal candidate is a highly capable Business Analyst with excellent requirements analysis skills and strong hands-on Tableau development expertise. They should be able to understand business needs, translate them into clear functional requirements and independently develop intuitive, meaningful and reliable dashboards.

- The role also involves data analysis, stakeholder engagement and coordination of delivery activities from requirements gathering through testing and implementation.

Responsibilities:

- Drive business requirements gathering and analysis, engaging stakeholders to understand business needs, challenge unclear requirements and translate business problems into clear functional specifications, business rules, KPIs and acceptance criteria.

- Independently design and develop Tableau dashboards, translating business requirements into effective visualisations, interactive dashboards and analytical views that provide meaningful business insights.

- Translate business questions into analytical requirements, defining appropriate metrics, dimensions, calculations, filters and drill-down capabilities to ensure dashboards address the underlying business needs.

- • Work closely with stakeholders to refine dashboard design and usability, ensuring visualisations are intuitive, relevant and aligned with business expectations.

- Perform data analysis to identify trends, anomalies and key drivers, supporting business users in investigating issues and deriving insights from data.

- Use SQL to extract, analyse and validate data, supporting dashboard development, data reconciliation and investigation of discrepancies.

- Investigate reporting and data issues, working with relevant stakeholders and technical teams to identify root causes and implement appropriate solutions.

- Work with data and technology teams to clarify data requirements, validate business logic and ensure dashboards and analytical outputs accurately reflect agreed requirements.

- Coordinate delivery activities, including requirements tracking, prioritisation, UAT, issue follow-up and implementation.

- Identify opportunities to enhance existing dashboards, improve reporting processes and reduce manual effort through better visualisation and analytics.

Mandatory Skills Description:

- 3-5 years of experience in business analysis, business intelligence, data analytics or reporting roles.

- Strong business analysis capabilities, with demonstrated experience in requirements elicitation, stakeholder engagement, problem definition, functional specifications, business rules and translating business needs into clear, actionable requirements.

- Excellent hands-on Tableau development skills, with proven experience independently designing and building dashboards, creating calculated fields, using LOD expressions and table calculations, implementing interactive features and developing meaningful visualisations from business requirements.

- Strong ability to translate business requirements into appropriate dashboard layouts, KPIs, metrics and analytical views, with a good understanding of data visualisation principles and dashboard usability.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to investigate business questions, interpret data, identify anomalies and explain findings clearly.

- Working proficiency in SQL to retrieve, analyse and validate data, investigate discrepancies and support dashboard development.

- Good understanding of data validation and reconciliation, ensuring dashboard calculations and reported figures are accurate and consistent with agreed business logic.

- Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to clarify requirements, explain analytical outputs and work effectively with business and technical teams.

- Ability to work independently, manage competing priorities and take ownership of assigned analytical deliverables.

Nice-to-Have Skills Description:

- Background in financial services, asset management or investment management, with exposure to Public Markets and/or Private Markets.

- Familiarity with investment data, portfolio reporting, performance monitoring or financial metrics.

- Experience with Tableau Server or Tableau Cloud, including dashboard publishing and refresh management.

- Familiarity with SQL-based data platforms such as Snowflake.

- Experience with JIRA or similar tools for requirements and delivery tracking.

- Experience coordinating UAT and supporting implementation of reporting solutions.
