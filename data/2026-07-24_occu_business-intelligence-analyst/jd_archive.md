Role Overview
The Business Intelligence Analyst is Occu's go-to expert for turning data into insight. Sitting within the Operations Centre of Excellence and partnering closely with the COO and CFO, the role designs, builds and continuously improves the dashboards and reporting that give the business a single, trusted view of performance.

The role builds on Yardi data and Power BI to develop practical, accurate and easy-to-use reporting across Property Management, Block Management, Leasing, Asset Management, Resident Services and central functions. It owns the end-to-end build: sourcing and modelling data, writing Power Query and DAX logic, and delivering secure, self-serve dashboards that replace fragmented spreadsheets and legacy reports.

Working alongside operational and finance stakeholders, the role helps extend and improve the reporting model so that client, asset, leasing and compliance information is reported from one reliable source of truth.

This is an opportunity to be at the forefront of shaping and informing decision making within the real estate market. This role will work closely with key stakeholders and client relationship leads. The successful candidate will shape the way real estate reporting is delivered, setting a new standard for the industry.

Key Responsibilities

Dashboard Development and Reporting
- Design, build and maintain Power BI dashboards across operations, leasing, asset management, finance and compliance.
- Develop business-specific reporting that supports day-to-day decision-making and leadership oversight.
- Replace legacy custom reports and standalone spreadsheets with BI-based, single-source-of-truth equivalents.
- Deliver both detailed operational views and executive summaries, with suitable extract capability where required.
- Ensure dashboards are accurate, performant and genuinely used by frontline and leadership teams.

Data Modelling, Power Query and DAX
- Model data from Yardi and related business inputs into robust, reusable semantic models.
- Write and optimise Power Query transformations to shape, clean and combine data from multiple sources.
- Develop custom DAX measures for advanced logic such as void analysis, case ageing, yield and occupancy metrics.
- Bring business-specific fields into the reporting model and maintain a clear, documented data dictionary.
- Ingest structured trackers and external files where required, mapping them to the property, unit and tenancy hierarchy.

Cross-Business Data and Analytics
- Pull and analyse data across Property Management, Block Management, Leasing, Asset Management, Resident Services and Finance.
- Surface client and asset-management insight, including running yield, capital values, valuations, void performance, demographics and market benchmarking.
- Define meaningful KPIs with business owners and ensure reporting reflects real operational performance.
- Turn data into clear, actionable recommendations that support prioritisation, performance and growth.

Data Integration and Reporting Inputs
- Work with teams across the business to identify, capture and standardise the data fields required for reporting.
- Bring leasing and customer journey data into Power BI, including lead volume, conversion rate, days to reserve, lead source and enquiry-to-move-in funnel metrics.
- Integrate additional operational and third-party data sources through appropriate structured inputs or scheduled refreshes.
- Support the scoping and prioritisation of new data structures that improve reporting quality and business insight.

Data Governance, Security and Compliance
- Implement and maintain appropriate data access controls, including Row Level Security where required.
- Produce master, redacted and aggregated versions of dashboards to comply with GDPR and support relevant stakeholder needs.
- Support regulatory and compliance reporting using structured, auditable data inputs.
- Promote strong data quality, consistent system usage and audit-ready, well-documented outputs.

Stakeholder Engagement and Enablement
- Act as the link between end users, data specialists and stakeholders to ensure BI solutions meet operational needs.
- Provide clear guidance, training and documentation to drive adoption and self-serve use of dashboards.
- Gather feedback and continuously improve reporting in line with business priorities.
