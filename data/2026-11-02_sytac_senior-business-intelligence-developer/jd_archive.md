Senior Business Intelligence Developer
Sytac
Rotterdam, South Holland, Netherlands

At Sytac, we build high-performing data and engineering teams for leading international organisations. We combine technical craftsmanship with a pragmatic, hands-on culture. Our data and BI professionals get the autonomy to integrate complex data landscapes, build dashboards and turn raw data into valuable business intelligence.

For our client, an international player in the maritime and logistics sector, we’re looking for a Senior Business Intelligence Developer. In this role, you’re the crucial link between business and IT. You’ll be responsible for developing, maintaining and optimising reports, data flows and BI solutions.

What you’ll do

- BI & Dashboards: Develop, maintain and manage advanced reports and dashboards in Power BI and Grafana for various European business units.

- Data Pipelines & Integration: Extract, model and analyse data from data lakes and data warehouses, and build reliable data flows and interfaces between business systems.

- Stakeholder Management: Work with international stakeholders to identify information needs and translate them into concrete management and operational reports.

- Advice & Optimisation: Advise on continuous improvements to the data and reporting architecture, in coordination with international colleagues and teams in Spain.

Who we’re looking for

- Experience: Proven experience as a Data Analyst, BI Developer, Data Engineer or similar role in an international context.

- BI & Data Visualisation: Extensive, in-depth knowledge of Power BI and experience with Grafana.

- Data & Engineering Stack: Strong skills in SQL Server, Python, data lakes, data warehouses and version control (e.g. GitHub).

- Integration Knowledge: Experience with data integrations, APIs, interfaces and connecting a wide range of source systems.

- Personality: Analytical, proactive and independent. You communicate well enough to move easily between business needs and technical solutions.

- Languages: Dutch mandatory and excellent written and spoken English is required, as you’ll work closely with international colleagues, including the team in Spain.

- Based in the EU (no visa sponsorship available).

Bonus points

- Knowledge of Power Apps and experience with Windows Server.

- Experience with SAP HANA and SAP SuccessFactors.

- Knowledge of or experience in the maritime or logistics sector.
