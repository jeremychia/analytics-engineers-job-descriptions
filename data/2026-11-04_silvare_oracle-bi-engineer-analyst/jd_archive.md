Role Overview
We are looking for an Oracle BI Engineer / Analyst for our client. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and supporting data integration and business intelligence solutions, helping organizations transform data into actionable insights. You will work closely with business stakeholders, data teams, and technical experts to deliver reliable reporting, analytics, and data integration capabilities in a dynamic environment.

What You'll Be Doing

- Design, develop, and maintain data integration workflows using Informatica technologies, including IDMC, CDI, and PowerCenter

- Develop, enhance, and support business intelligence solutions using Oracle Business Intelligence Enterprise Edition (OBIEE)

- Build and maintain analyses, reports, dashboards, and data visualizations to support business reporting requirements

- Develop and optimize SQL queries for data extraction, transformation, validation, and reporting purposes

- Collaborate with business stakeholders and technical teams to gather requirements and translate them into effective BI and data integration solutions

- Support data warehousing initiatives, ETL processes, and data modeling activities

- Ensure data quality, consistency, and reliability across reporting and integration solutions

- Perform troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and resolution of issues related to data integration and reporting platforms

What You'll Bring

- Experience with Informatica IDMC, CDI, PowerCenter, or related data integration technologies

- Experience with Oracle Business Intelligence Enterprise Edition (OBIEE) or similar Business Intelligence platforms

- Strong SQL skills with the ability to write, optimize, and troubleshoot complex queries

- Good understanding of data warehousing concepts, ETL methodologies, and data integration best practices

- Experience working with relational databases and enterprise reporting solutions

- Ability to gather business requirements and translate them into technical solutions
