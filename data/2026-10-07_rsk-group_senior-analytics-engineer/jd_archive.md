Senior Analytics Engineer
RSK Group
London, England, United Kingdom

The Vacancy

As part of our continued growth, RSK Group are seeking a Senior Analytics Engineer to join our expanding Data & Analytics team.

This is a full time, hybrid role (minimum 3 days per week in the office) based at our office in London - Chancery Exchange.

You'll play a key role in shaping our data strategy, driving best practice and enabling data-driven decision-making at every level of the business.

Key Responsibilities:

- Take technical ownership of the DBT transformation layer on our Snowflake warehouse - its architecture, standards, modularity, testing and documentation.

- Own the certified metrics layer: one agreed definition per group metric, drillable by business and division, documented and version-controlled.

- Broker metric definitions with the business - getting autonomous operating companies to a single, defensible way of counting, and holding the line once it is agreed.

- Lead the design of Power BI semantic models and datasets so that reporting is fast, reliable and genuinely self-serve.

- Extend certified coverage as new businesses are connected, so that group numbers describe more of the group rather than a subset of it.

- Rationalise the reporting estate - consolidate and retire low-value assets, and make the ongoing maintenance cost of a new asset explicit before it is built.

- Make data quality visible: quality and coverage indicators on the reporting itself, and a route for recurring issues to move from flagged to automatically resolved.

Person Specification:

- A relevant degree (e.g. computer science, engineering, mathematics) or equivalent practical experience.

- Substantial analytics engineering or BI development experience, including time in a senior or lead role owning a transformation and metrics layer.

- Expert SQL, including performant queries and the design of well-structured, maintainable data models.

- Expert DBT - macros, packages, testing, documentation and CI - with a track record of keeping a large model estate clean.

- Deep Power BI experience: semantic/data modelling, DAX, performance tuning, and building reports people actually trust.

- Experience with cloud data warehouses; exposure to Snowflake is highly desirable.

- Strong grasp of dimensional modelling (star schema), metric layer design and ELT best practice.

- Demonstrable experience defining and governing business metrics across multiple business units, including navigating disagreement about definitions.

Salary and Benefits:

- £50,000 - £80,000 depending on experience

- Contributory pension scheme

- Life assurance

- Flexible benefits programme
