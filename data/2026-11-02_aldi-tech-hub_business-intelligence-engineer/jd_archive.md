Business Intelligence Engineer (m/f/d)
ALDI Tech Hub · Cracow, Małopolskie, Poland

The ALDI Tech Hub is part of the ALDI Nord Group of Companies – one of the leading international retail enterprises with 5,600 stores and 90, 000 employees in 9 countries. With a tradition stretching back over 100 years, ALDI stands for the invention of discount retailing. Our mission is to provide people everywhere and at all times with what they need for their daily lives: high-quality products at the lowest possible price – simply and swiftly. This also includes making shopping as easy as possible. We ensure this not only in our stores, but just as much behind the scenes: In addition to powerful IT infrastructures for our stores, warehouses and offices, we are working on innovative technologies that will make shopping even easier in the future. The ALDI Tech Hub is an important part of fulfilling this mission.

Your tasks
- Together with your team, you design, develop, and operate scalable solutions for ALDI Nord’s international Data & Analytics platform.
- You develop solutions for advancing the international Business Intelligence strategy with regard to BI architecture and the self-service approach, based on leading Microsoft cloud technology.
- You support project teams in the design and implementation of a holistic BI and analytics solution approach—from data provisioning to interactive analysis dashboards.
- You ensure the stable operation of our BI tools within the framework of operations, monitoring, and incident management.
- Your define key performance indicators and implement them in Power BI reports and cubes/datasets.
- You develop and implement measures to improve the performance of BI tools for end users- always balancing the best cost-to-benefit ratio.
- You closely collaborate with Data Engineers on data requirements through joint concepts and user story refinements (SCRUM).
- Your main focus is in Business Intelligence, but ideally you also bring experience in backend development (e.g., connecting new data sources, developing and optimizing ETL pipelines, data modeling).

Requirements
- Completed studies in business informatics, statistics, business intelligence, data analytics, or a comparable qualification.
- At least 3 years of professional experience in Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, Data Engineering, or a comparable field.
- Strong knowledge in developing audience‑appropriate reports and dashboards for various stakeholders, and the ability to analyze and model complex datasets and share this knowledge with your team.
- Advanced programming skills in R, Python, SQL, or similar technologies, as well as strong experience with business intelligence tools such as Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, or Qlik Sense.
- Strong analytical thinking, structured work approach, and enjoyment of solving complex new tasks.
- A high degree of independence, accuracy, and quality awareness.
- Ability to handle uncertainty and strong motivation to design new processes and concepts.
- Good English skills, both written and spoken.
