About The Role

We are seeking a dedicated and experienced Senior Data Analytics Engineer to join our healthcare-focused technology team. As a Senior Data Engineer in the healthcare industry, you will be an integral part of developing cutting-edge data products that contribute to improving patient care, streamlining healthcare operations, by applying machine learning / artificial intelligence capabilities, while ensuring data security and compliance.

Responsibilities

- Analytical data modelling: Create tailored datasets to support Product, Operations, Clinical Teams. Develop and maintain automated reports, analyses, and dashboards to monitor the performance of key product features, ensuring that stakeholders have access to the most updated and reliable data. Define each model's grain, keys, relationships, and business logic so that teams can use consistent, trusted datasets across reporting and analysis.

- KPI discovery and definition: Work with teams to understand their requirements and translate them into clearly defined, computable KPIs. Agree each metric's business meaning, calculation logic, data sources, and reporting cadence, then validate and monitor it regularly with stakeholders.

- Technical leadership and ownership:Agree priorities and acceptance criteria with stakeholders, document architectural trade-offs, establish modelling and review standards, and mentor colleagues. Own the investigation and resolution of issues affecting the reliability, performance, and cost of analytical data products.

- Data Pipelines framework: Contribute to improving our data pipelines framework to develop scalable, reliable, and maintainable data pipelines, from bronze to silver and, from silver to gold. Advocate for data pipeline best practices, including idempotency.

- Data Quality frameworks: Detect and flag issues such as missing fields, incorrect types, or schema mismatches at the source. Develop automated tests and quality checks integrated into pipelines, covering data completeness and data consistency, both across ingestion and processing layers.

- Define Analytics Tracking: Lead the definition and implementation of analytics tracking for in-app events, particularly when new features are developed, such as new pages and buttons.

- ML & AI pipelines: Contribute to building machine learning pipelines in Python, collaborating closely with AI Scientists and AI Engineers.
