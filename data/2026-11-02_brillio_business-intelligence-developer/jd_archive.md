Business Intelligence Developer
Brillio
Romania

We are looking for a BI Developer to design, develop and maintain business intelligence solutions that help teams make informed, data-driven decisions. You will work with stakeholders to understand reporting needs, transform data into reliable insights, and build dashboards and reports that are clear, accurate and useful.

The role is suited to someone with strong SQL and data analysis skills who can work across data sources, communicate technical findings clearly, and contribute to dependable reporting and analytics practices.

Responsibilities

- Collaborate directly with stakeholders to gather requirements, define KPIs and translate business needs into reporting and analytics solutions.

- Design and develop Power BI dashboards and reports, covering the full lifecycle from requirements analysis to implementation and support.

- Build and maintain data models using star schema principles, integrating data from SQL Server and Excel-based sources.

- Develop and maintain MicroStrategy reporting solutions, including reports, schema objects and cubes.

- Perform data validation, testing and troubleshooting activities to ensure reporting accuracy and consistency.

- Migrating reporting solutions while independently delivering end-to-end BI projects.

Requirements

- Experience developing business intelligence reports, dashboards or analytics solutions.

- Solid experience with Power BI, SQL Server, Data modeling, ETL, Databricks and AI enablement.

- Understanding of data modelling, data transformation and reporting principles.

- Ability to analyse data, validate results and investigate data quality issues.

- Experience working with stakeholders to translate business needs into reporting requirements.

- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.

- Clear communication skills and the ability to explain insights to technical and non-technical audiences.

- Experience with data warehouses, data lakes or cloud-based data platforms.

- Familiarity with ETL or ELT processes and data integration tools.

- Experience with Power BI DAX, Tableau calculations or comparable analytical expressions.

- Knowledge of data governance, access controls and information security practices.

- Experience with version control, deployment processes or automated testing for BI solutions.
