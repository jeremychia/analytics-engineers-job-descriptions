The Role

Are you passionate about transforming complex data into meaningful business insights? Do you enjoy building scalable data models, developing efficient data transformation pipelines, and delivering enterprise-grade Business Intelligence solutions?

We are looking for a BI Engineer who combines strong analytical thinking with hands-on expertise in Power BI, PySpark, SQL, and Data Modelling. You will work closely with Data Engineers, Product Owners, Architects, and Business Stakeholders to build robust reporting solutions and enable data-driven decision making across the organization.

Key Responsibilities

- Design and develop scalable Power BI dashboards and enterprise reporting solutions.

- Build and maintain reusable semantic data models to support business reporting and self-service analytics.

- Develop complex PySpark transformation logic for cleansing, enriching, aggregating, and preparing large datasets for analytical consumption.

- Design and implement dimensional data models including Fact & Dimension tables, Star Schema, and Snowflake Schema.

- Collaborate with Data Engineering teams to integrate data from multiple enterprise sources into reporting layers.

- Develop optimized SQL queries, views, and stored procedures to support reporting and analytics.

- Perform data validation, reconciliation, and root cause analysis to ensure reporting accuracy and consistency.

- Optimize dashboard performance through efficient DAX measures, query optimization, and model tuning.

- Translate business requirements into technical reporting solutions and analytical models.

- Partner with Product Owners and business stakeholders to define KPIs and reporting standards.

- Promote BI best practices around governance, reusable datasets, naming conventions, and documentation.

- Support production issues, data investigations, and continuous improvements across the BI platform.
