Description
State Street Investment Management (SSIM) is a global leader in asset management, entrusted with more than $6 trillion in assets. For more than three decades, investors worldwide have benefited from our disciplined, precise investment process and powerful global platform offering access to every major asset class, capitalization range and style across the indexing and active spectrums. SSIM is the investment management arm of State Street Corporation, one of the world’s leading providers of financial services to institutions.
Markets Intelligence & Strategic Insights (MISI) is the business unit within the Business Office at State Street Investment Management that supports the organization in reporting & analytics, data infrastructure/quality, sales enablement as well as Strategy and Planning. The vision of the MISI team is to be an indispensable information resource to help achieve desired goals and objectives. Our mission is to use information to provide a reliable foundation for decision-making, strategy development, opportunity realization and targeted business improvements.
The Analytics Engineer role is a hands-on, technical position within the Markets Intelligence and Strategic Insights team. The role will be instrumental in developing and enhancing Tableau dashboards and reports that deliver clear, actionable insights that inform decision-making across the business. The successful candidate will also play a key role in designing, building, and maintaining the analytical data infrastructure. Working closely with stakeholders across Strategy, Capital Markets, and Technology, this role focuses on developing robust ETL pipelines, integrating disparate datasets, and enabling scalable, reliable analytics across the business.
Key Responsibilities
- Focusing on impactful analyses that will significantly contribute to business objectives, the role will consist of contributing across the full analytical lifecycle, including data modelling and the development and maintenance of Tableau dashboards and reports that deliver clear, timely insights to technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Designing, implementing, and maintaining high-quality ETL and Lakehouse pipelines across Databricks and Amazon Redshift to support the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) project by centralizing and integrating disparate datasets (e.g. Salesforce/CRM, holdings and finance data) into a scalable analytical infrastructure
- Collaborating with Strategy and other business and technology stakeholders to understand key business and strategy questions, and producing analytical products that directly support commercial and strategic objectives
- Develop and enhance enterprise-grade Tableau dashboards that communicate trends, drivers, and performance clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Ensure dashboards meet standards for performance, usability, and interpretability
- Contribute to design decisions around data freshness, transformation layers, and analytical layer. Proactively identify data quality issues and partner with upstream teams to resolve them
- Work closely with the local VP to support team management and strategic planning
Required Qualifications
- A BS/MS in Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, Engineering, or related field (or related experience)
- 6+ years of relevant experience in analytics, analytics engineering, business intelligence, or data engineering roles, with at least 2–3 years building analytics solutions in a complex, enterprise data environment
- Demonstrated experience owning end‑to‑end analytical products (data modeling, transformations, dashboards, and stakeholder delivery) operating at a senior individual contributor level and influencing analytical approach, standards and design across projects
- Experience building and maintaining ETL pipelines in platforms such as Databricks with proficiency in Python, SQL and PySpark and a solid understanding of data modelling best practices
- Experience owning production reliability, validation and reconciliation against source systems, timely resolution of failures and clear documentation
- Experience partnering closely with IT and engineering teams to move analytical solutions through formal SDLC and change-management processes
- Strong collaboration skills and a question-first mindset, highly organized and comfortable in fast-moving environments, working across teams in different locations as well as independently, including mentoring junior team members
Preferred Qualifications
- Familiarity with and interest in financial markets and market data
- Additional experience in developing, automating, and scaling Tableau dashboards or similar BI tools
- Experience working with Databricks, Apache Spark, Amazon S3, Redshift or similar cloud data platforms
- Prior experience supporting and working on analytics teams within financial services or capital markets environments
- Familiarity with Salesforce/CRM or similar client data platforms
- Familiarity with Google Cloud / Google Enterprise AI tools (e.g. Vertex AI, Gemini)
- Exposure to AI-supported analytics or agent design patterns such as RAG, orchestration and routing
