Manager - Tax Data Analytics Engineer
Wolt
London, England, United Kingdom

About Wolt

At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity and earnings to the neighborhoods of the world. In 2014 we started with delivery of restaurant food. Now we’re building the delivery of (almost) everything and you’ll find us in over 500 cities in 30 countries around the world. In 2022 we joined forces with DoorDash and together we keep on dreaming big and expanding across the globe.

Working at Wolt isn’t always easy, but it’s definitely exciting. Here you’ll learn more, build more, and ship more than in most other companies. You’ll be challenged a lot, but also have a lot of fun on the way. So, if you’re a self-starter with drive and entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life.

We are looking for an experienced Data Analytics Engineer to join our growing Tax Data Mart team. This role will be a key contributor to the team and will be responsible for building the tax data operations function for our Deliveroo and Wolt legal entities. This includes building and operating the data pipelines and infrastructure that power tax analytics and support decision-making across the company.

As DoorDash continues to grow its global footprint across multiple business lines, we are looking for a candidate with experience working with Tax teams to gather requirements, and designing scalable data processes to support them.. This person will grow our unique community via your blend of tax, data technology, and process improvement experience.

About The Role

You will be reporting to the Senior Manager of Tax Data & Operations, and play an integral role in DoorDash’s data function, owning the processes for ingesting, transforming, and controlling our data, ensuring accuracy, and making it available to analytics and business users.

As a member of this team, you will lead the design, implementation, and operation of data pipelines and related processes to support the Company’s exponential growth.

What You’ll Be Doing

- Lead the collection of Tax Data and Reporting requirements through close relationships with Tax team counterparts

- Design, build, and maintain scalable, reliable data pipelines (ETL/ELT) that serve data across the company

- Develop and optimize data models and warehouse structures to support analytics and reporting

- Participate in new business initiatives and identify their impact on existing data system(s) and processes.

- Work closely with Analytics, Data Science, and business partners in the development and validation of new data sources.

- Identify and document business, functional and technical requirements to support new business initiatives and to enhance existing system functionality.

- Leverage AI & AI assisted tooling to build and automate recurring data tasks & pipelines.

- Monitor data quality, reliability, and observability, and resolve data-related issues for our internal business partners.

- Participate in defining the Company’s data tooling needs, including the maintenance of existing systems.

- Prepare documentation related to our data systems and processes.

- Share knowledge and best practices with engineers and analysts across the team.

Our humble expectations

- You have 7+ years of experience in a Tax data & technology or similar role.

- Experience using Data Build Tool (DBT) or a similar product to manage data flows.

- Experience using Claude, Cursor and similar AI tools.

- You are an expert at using SQL with experience using Snowflake.

- Proficiency in Python (or a comparable language) for building data pipelines.

- Experience with modern data stack and orchestration tools such as Airflow, Spark, or Kafka.

- You have strong attention to detail and analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.

- You’re an independently motivated person with a proven ability to manage multiple projects and prioritize work independently.

- You have excellent written and verbal communication skills.

- Project management experience, including creation of project plans, milestones, and due dates.

- Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related degree.
