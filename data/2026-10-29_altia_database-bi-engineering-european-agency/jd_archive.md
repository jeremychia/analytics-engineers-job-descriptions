🚀 #WEAREALTIA

ALTIA is a multidisciplinary team focused on delivering advanced data-driven solutions that generate measurable impact and enable smarter decision-making. Working across international environments, we combine analytics, artificial intelligence, and business expertise to transform data into strategic value. Our mission is to help organizations evolve through innovation, scalable data platforms, and high-quality analytical solutions, fostering continuous learning, collaboration, and technological excellence.

We are looking for a Senior Database & BI Engineer with extensive, high-level expertise in database architecture, enterprise data warehousing, relational database engineering (Oracle & PostgreSQL), ETL/ELT integration pipelines, and analytical/reporting ecosystems to join our team in supporting the evolution, design, migration, and maintenance of large-scale European Commission data platforms and core application systems.

The role involves designing, building, migrating, and optimizing modern Database and Business Intelligence architectures across cloud, on-premises, and hybrid environments. The specialist will lead secure and scalable database design, conceptual/logical/physical data modeling, procedural programming (PL/SQL, PL/pgSQL), release/deployment scripting, performance tuning, and the implementation of complex ETL/ELT pipelines and analytics solutions. Working within structured European management frameworks (PM² and Agile methodologies), the position requires high technical rigor, data quality enforcement, and secure management of sensitive operational information.

In close collaboration with project leads, service managers, developers, analysts, and cross-functional international teams, the specialist will translate complex functional needs into scalable, secure, and high-performing database and data engineering solutions.
