 About Digital Green

 Digital Green is a pioneer global not for profit organization, utilizing digital platforms and community-driven approaches to amplify the voices of smallholder farmers and improve their livelihoods. Our mission is to create a world where farmers use technology and data to build prosperous communities. By harnessing the power of technology, we facilitate knowledge sharing, capacity building, and market linkages, enabling farmers to adopt sustainable agricultural practices and increase their productivity and income.
We are dedicated to transforming the lives of under-served smallholder farmers worldwide through innovative technology solutions. Backed by leading philanthropic organizations such as the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation (BMGF), Walmart Foundation, USAID, and UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (UKFCDO), we are committed to leveraging data and technology to empower smallholder farmers and strengthen agricultural extension systems.
Job Summary

 We’re seeking a Sr. Analytics Engineer I to strengthen our ability to learn from user data—turning millions of farmer interactions and behavioral signals into insights that guide design, growth, and strategy.
FarmerChat’s analytics infrastructure is at an early and critical stage. The main goal of this role is to strengthen the data foundation and launch a targeted set of high-priority, high-visibility analytical projects—helping us understand how farmers find, use, and benefit from FarmerChat across India, Kenya, Ethiopia, Nigeria, and other priority geographies.
You’ll join a lean but growing data and analytics team embedded within FarmerChat’s global strategy function, working closely with cross-functional product and tech teams. This is a senior individual-contributor role: you’ll set the standards and best practices this function runs on, not just execute against ones already in place.
Key Responsibilities

Data Foundation & Pipelines
- Build and maintain automated data pipelines, transformations, and dashboards across our analytics and engagement ecosystems on BigQuery.
- Stand up and stabilize the data infrastructure for long-term scale, including repository migration and DevOps maintenance.
- Build a strong analytics foundation with clear, reliable dashboards showing how farmers discover and use FarmerChat across regions and genders.
Metrics & Insights
- Implement metrics and visualizations to track user acquisition, engagement, and retention, and produce dashboards that let product, research, and leadership teams make data-informed decisions.
- Partner with leadership, donors, and country teams to turn analytics into a recurring insights cycle they can rely on can inform what is working and what needs to improve.
 Standards & Cross-Functional Partnership
- Data warehousing: Manage and extend central data warehouses and databases for querying and reporting.
- In partnership with the Director of Strategy & Insights, set best practices for prioritizing, documenting, and executing data analysis requests.
Qualifications & Skills

- 5+ years of experience in data engineering & analytics with digital platforms. Bachelor’s required, Master’s desirable.
- Expertise in SQL, Python and Bigquery.
- Experience building visualizations to make sense of data.
- Experience setting up or maintaining ELT pipelines for a live product (raw → staging → transformation layers), with preferably more weight on the transformation layer.
- Hands-on data engineering experience with cloud data platforms such as GCP, AWS, or Azure.
- Proven ability to build and maintain data models using dbt, Databricks, or similar tools.
- Familiar with data ingestion and orchestration tools such as Apache Airflow, Airbyte, or Fivetran.
- Familiar with Google Analytics, MoEngage, Mixpanel, or similar product analytics systems, and eager to learn new tools.
- Experience working with high-volume event data from mobile apps or conversational products, and using it to inform product or growth decisions.
- Experience using GitHub for version control and implementing CI/CD pipelines.
- Detail-oriented, self-motivated, and able to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team.
Nice to Have
- Experience working across time zones and cultures, synthesizing inputs from product, research, MEL, and country teams into concise recommendations.
- Track record of partnering with product or growth teams to define funnels, cohorts, and guardrail metrics for experiments.
What You'll Build

- A clear picture of FarmerChat’s current analytics setup, with the key fixes identified for tracking, dashboards, and data flows needed to support scale.
- A reliable analytics foundation with dashboards showing how farmers discover and use FarmerChat across regions and genders.
- A recurring insights cycle for leadership, donors, and country teams—covering the annual cycle of farmer concerns by geography, key differences between farmer segments, and pest & disease heat maps.
