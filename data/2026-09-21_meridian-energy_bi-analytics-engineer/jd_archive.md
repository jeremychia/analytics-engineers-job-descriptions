Mō te tūranga | About the role
Meridian are hiring a BI Analytics Engineer to enable high‑value analytics and decision‑making by designing and maintaining trusted, analytics‑ready data models that translate business questions into reusable, reliable data assets and support scalable, self‑service analytics across Meridian.
The right person will have experience with data engineering, BI analytics and development. We’re looking for someone who can consult with the business to build a strategic enterprise view of how to deliver data modelling across domains.
Ngā pūkenga kei a koe mō tēnei tūranga | What you’ll bring

- Strong experience shaping analytics and semantic layers, focused on analytical usability, metrics and downstream consumption.

- Strong experience developing and operating analytics models on a cloud data platform (e.g. Databricks, Snowflake, Fabric).

- Strong proficiency in modern analytics engineering tooling and practices (e.g. dbt, Spark Declarative Pipelines / Delta Live Tables).

- Strong data modelling experience across Dimensional / Kimball, Data Vault and One Big Table (OBT), plus semantic modelling and business metric definitions.

- Python and SQL skills, with experience building complex, maintainable transformation logic (advantageous).

- Experience in cross-functional delivery teams, with a growth mindset and a preference for non-hierarchical, collaborative, transparent cultures; thrives in a fast-paced environment.

- Degree in a quantitative or technical discipline (e.g. computer science, engineering) or 5+ years' relevant experience.
