About the Role

Join Carrefour Global Sourcing (CGS), the Asian sourcing hub of a Fortune Global 500 retailer. We are seeking an autonomous, self-driven Analytics Engineer to own data excellence across our Quality, Packaging, CSR, and Costing functions.
In this role, you will bridge operational realities with executive strategy by engineering robust data pipelines, establishing a "Single Source of Truth," and building interactive "Control Tower" dashboards that turn raw data into high-impact business decisions.

Key Responsibilities
- Data Discovery (Inventory & Profiling): Audit, map, and document a comprehensive inventory of 100% of existing department metrics, KPIs, and data sources to formally define our "Single Source of Truth" within the first 90 days of onboarding.
- Data Requirements (Specs & Translation): Partner and translate operational needs into formal business requirements across our 4 key CGS departments (Quality, Packaging, CSR and Costing), ensuring perfect alignment between executive reporting and business strategy by Early Q4.
- Data Collection (Pipeline Collaboration): Collaborate and implement automated data pipelines alongside the Digital Team, acting as the key business liaison to reduce manual data extraction time by 50% across our 8 data extraction workflows within 9 months.
- Data Quality & Governance: Implement and enforce automated quality controls and a strict "clean at the source" policy, delivering training to 100% of selected relevant operational teams and service providers to achieve a 98% data accuracy rate within 1 Year.
- Data Analysis & Exploration: Analyze and optimize complex datasets (Quality, Packaging, CSR, and Costing) to identify trends, delivering at least 4 actionable, data-driven process optimization recommendations quarterly that demonstrably mitigate operational risks.
- Data Visualization (Tower Control): Design, build, and deploy interactive Quality "Tower Control" dashboards using an Agile approach, achieving a minimum of 4 out of 5 user satisfaction rate among target stakeholders for real-time decision-making within the first 6 months.
- Strategic Communication: Present and track data-driven recommendations to GS teams on a monthly basis, acting as the bridge between raw data and operational execution to ensure 100% of operational and executive KPIs are accurately communicated and actionable.

*This description highlights core duties; additional tasks may be assigned based on evolving department and business needs.

- Advanced visualization and spreadsheet skills (Tableau, DataStudio/Looker Studio, Excel, Power Query/Macros, Google Sheets, AppScript).
