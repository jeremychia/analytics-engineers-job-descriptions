What you will do

We’re looking for a Senior Analytics Engineer to help us build reliable, scalable data products for enterprise customers and internal teams. This is a hands-on role with a strong focus on SQL, dbt, data modeling, data-product development, and practical AI integration—not traditional dashboarding alone.

You’ll own work from a stakeholder question through analysis, modeling, quality assurance, and delivery. You’ll also help strengthen how the team works with dbt and contribute to our shift toward scalable data delivery. You will take on direct customer-facing responsibilities, with support from the team as you build that experience.

Where you will have impact

- Design, build, and improve reliable production data models and products using SQL, dbt, Python, and BigQuery.

- Turn business and customer questions into scalable solutions, owning delivery from scoping and analysis through modeling, testing, documentation, QA, and actionable insights.

- Diagnose and improve data quality, query performance, and cost across large, complex datasets.

- Partner with Product, Data Science, Engineering, Commercial, and Account Management to define requirements, explain trade-offs, and manage expectations on feasibility and timelines.

- Use AI to accelerate development, analysis, scoping, and documentation, and help explore AI-powered capabilities such as automated quality checks and agentic customer or sales tooling.

- Share your dbt expertise, strengthen team development practices, contribute to enterprise and internal initiatives, and eventually join the evolving on-call rotation for enterprise data products.

About our team

Lighthouse is not only a data-driven company, we are a data company. The heart of all our products is data. It enables hotels to make the right decisions and fuel our analytical solutions. Being a growth company enables us to regularly attract new and interesting datasets, which can unlock new product directions.
