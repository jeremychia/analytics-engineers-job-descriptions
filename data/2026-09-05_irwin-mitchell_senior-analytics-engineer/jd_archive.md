Your Role and What You'll Be Doing

As a Senior Analytics Engineer, you'll lead curated data modelling within our Lakehouse environment, translating complex business requirements into robust SQL and PySpark transformation pipelines. You'll take ownership of the data serving layer, ensuring datasets are performant, well‑governed and optimised for consumption across the range of Analytics deliverables. You will play a key role in ensuring that business requirements for data and associated definitions are translated into that curated data layer.

You'll also act as a technical mentor within the team, setting standards, reviewing designs and supporting the development of less experienced analysts and engineers.

What you'll do

Lead the design and delivery of curated, analytics‑ready dimensional and semantic models

Own KPI definitions, conformed dimensions and semantic consistency across domains

Build and maintain complex SQL and PySpark transformation pipelines on Databricks

Ensure data quality, testing, observability and performance across the curated layer

Lead modelling design reviews and contribute to architectural decisions

Partner closely with Data Analysts, Data Architects, Data Engineers, and business stakeholders

Provide technical leadership, mentorship and guidance to analysts and engineers

Contribute to CI/CD, testing and governance standards across the Data Platform

About You

What we're looking for

Significant experience working as an Analytics Engineer in a modern data platform environment

Expert‑level SQL skills and strong experience with PySpark

Deep understanding of dimensional modelling, semantic layers and analytics engineering best practices

Experience working with Databricks and Lakehouse architecture

Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to translate complex concepts clearly to analysts and business SMEs

A collaborative, proactive mindset with a passion for high‑quality, trusted data

Experience mentoring or leading other engineers
